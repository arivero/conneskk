## Statements

**Proposition 50.1 (the rank-one block and the connector share one point of the twistor fibre).** Let X in Hom_C(L_p, L_p^perp) be a Yukawa block with masses m_- = 0 < m_+ = m, so that X = m lambda mu^* with unit vectors mu in L_p and lambda in L_p^perp (Proposition 46.2 (2)), and let the connector mark the line Cw in L_p.

1. The map n -> c(n) w is a real-linear isometry of T_p S^4 = R^4 onto L_p^perp (as a real four-space); so lambda = c(n) mu for a unique unit n in T_p S^4, and X = c(h) P_mu with h = m n, P_mu = mu mu^*. The Hermitian block is Y = {c(h), P_mu} = c(h) P_mu + P_mu c(h); its j-linear half is c(h)/2 and its j-antilinear half is {c(h), rho_mu}/2 with rho_mu = P_mu - P_{j mu}, both of operator norm m/2 and Hilbert-Schmidt norm m.
2. The following are equivalent: (a) Cw is an eigenline of X^* X (the eigenline condition of Corollary 46.4); (b) C mu = Cw or C mu = C jw; (c) the stabilizers of X and of the connector's line in K_p share a circle.
3. Under (b), the massive right-handed states are those of Cw (x) conj(V), nu_R and u_R, when C mu = Cw, and those of Cjw (x) conj(V), e_R and d_R, when C mu = Cjw. With the block universal on V (Corollary 16.8, Y (x) 1_V) the first case gives the mass m to the up quark and to the Dirac neutrino and the second to the down quark and the charged lepton; in the scope of lemma 18, Proposition 21.1 keeps cos^2 theta_NC = 5/8 and the ratio 8/3 in the first case only. In the first case, X = c(h) P_w: one point [w] of the twistor fibre P(L_p) = S^2 over p, one point of CP^3 = G/H, carries the Majorana block, on the line P_w Hom(V, W) P_v = C e, and the massive right-handed states, on P_w Hom(V, W); the massive left-handed line is C c(h) w in L_p^perp.
4. In that case the joint stabilizer of X and of the connector's line in K_p is the circle U(1)_h = {(a_theta, b_theta)}, with b_theta = e^{i theta} on w and e^{-i theta} on jw and a_theta = e^{i theta} on c(h) w and e^{-i theta} on j c(h) w; c(n) commutes with it. Let J_w be the complex structure of T_p S^4 defined by c(J_w n') w = i c(n') w. Then J_w is an orthogonal complex structure, Sp(1)_b acts on T_p S^4 = Hom_H(L_p, L_p^perp) by t -> t b^{-1}, and U(1)_b = {b_theta} acts on (T_p S^4, J_w) by the scalars e^{-i theta}: (T_p S^4, J_w) is the complex doublet (2)_{+-1} of Proposition 42.2, the Higgs doublet of hypercharge 1/2 with the complex structure supplied by the Majorana point, and h is a vector of it, with |h| = m. The blocks commuting with U(1)_h on both chiralities are the X' = c(n)(y_1 P_w + y_2 P_{jw}) with y_1, y_2 complex, the diagonal blocks of Proposition 49.3 with the circle of the lifted transposition replaced by U(1)_h, j-linear exactly when y_2 = conj(y_1).

*Proof.* (1) For n, n' in T_p S^4, c(n) c(n') + c(n') c(n) = 2 n . n', and c(n) w lies in L_p^perp since c(n) anticommutes with Gamma_p; so Re <c(n) w, c(n') w> = Re <w, c(n) c(n') w> = n . n' (the anticommutator is 2 n . n' times the identity and the commutator is anti-Hermitian, with purely imaginary expectation), which makes n -> c(n) w a real isometry from R^4 into the real four-space L_p^perp, hence onto. Write lambda = c(n) mu for the unit n so obtained from mu; then X = m c(n) mu mu^* = c(h) P_mu, and Y = X + X^* = c(h) P_mu + P_mu c(h) since c(h) is Hermitian and P_mu is a projection. Put P_mu = (Pi_p + rho_mu)/2, where P_mu + P_{j mu} = Pi_p because mu and j mu are orthogonal and span L_p; j P_mu j^{-1} = P_{j mu}, so j rho_mu j^{-1} = -rho_mu and j Pi_p j^{-1} = Pi_p. Then Y = (c(h) Pi_p + Pi_p c(h))/2 + {c(h), rho_mu}/2 = c(h)/2 + {c(h), rho_mu}/2, since Pi_p c(h) = c(h)(1 - Pi_p); the first term is j-linear and the second j-antilinear. For the norms, c(h) has operator norm |h| = m and {c(h), rho_mu} = c(h) rho_mu + rho_mu c(h) with (c(h) rho_mu)^* (c(h) rho_mu) = rho_mu c(h)^2 rho_mu = m^2 rho_mu^2 = m^2 Pi_p, so both halves have operator norm m/2 and squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm (m^2/4) x 4 = m^2. (2) X^* X = m^2 P_mu has the eigenlines C mu and its orthogonal complement in L_p, which is C j mu; so Cw is an eigenline exactly when Cw = C mu or Cw = C j mu, that is C mu = Cw or C mu = C jw. The equivalence with (c) is Corollary 46.4. (3) The lambda-weight line of the summand C of A_SM is Cw by Theorem 20.2, so by the dictionary the states of Cw (x) conj(V) are nu_R (on the lepton line) and u_R (on v^perp), and those of Cjw (x) conj(V) are e_R and d_R; with Y (x) 1_V, the right-handed states of mass m are those of the massive line tensored with all of conj(V). (4) For (a, b) in K_p, a X b^{-1} = X with X = m c(n) P_w means a c(n) w (b w)^* = c(n) w w^* ... more precisely a c(n) P_w b^{-1} = c(n) P_w; applied to b w this gives a c(n) w <w, b w>-dependence: a c(n) w (w^* b^{-1}) = c(n) w w^*, so b^{-1} must fix the line Cw and a must fix the line C c(n) w with the same phase, b w = e^{i theta} w and a c(n) w = e^{i theta} c(n) w; being j-linear, b and a act as e^{-i theta} on jw and j c(n) w. For c(n): a_theta c(n) b_theta^{-1} w = e^{-i theta} a_theta c(n) w = c(n) w and a_theta c(n) b_theta^{-1} jw = e^{i theta} a_theta j c(n) w = e^{i theta} e^{-i theta} j c(n) w = c(n) jw, so c(n) commutes with U(1)_h. J_w is well defined because n' -> c(n') w is a real isometry onto L_p^perp and multiplication by i is an isometry of L_p^perp; J_w^2 = -1 and J_w is orthogonal, so it is an orthogonal complex structure on T_p S^4. Under (a, b) in K_p, the element t = c(n')|_{L_p} of Hom_H(L_p, L_p^perp) goes to a t b^{-1} (the action of Proposition 42.2), and for a = 1, b = b_theta: t b_theta^{-1} w = e^{-i theta} c(n') w = c(cos theta n' - sin theta J_w n') w, so U(1)_b acts on (T_p S^4, J_w) by the scalars e^{-i theta}, a complex doublet of charge -1 of U(1)_b in the normalization of lemma 42 (charge +1 with the opposite orientation of the circle), the (2)_{+-1} of Proposition 42.2. **Proposition 50.2 (the projectors and the grading as compressions of height functions).** In the convention of the Setting, for every B in End_C(C^4),

    Q(5 w'^* B w' / w'^* w') = B + tr(B) 1.

Consequently:

1. 5 Q(p . x) = Gamma_p = 2 Pi_p - 1: the grading is the compression of the height function 5 cos d(., p) of S^4 at the axis (lemma 20, reading 2).
2. Q(h_w) = P_w for h_w([w']) = 5 |<w', w>|^2/|w'|^2 - 1 = 5 cos^2 d([w'], [w]) - 1: the projector on the connector's line is the compression of the height function of CP^3 at the marked point [w]; in the same way P_v = Q_V(h_v) on the second sheet, which is the same kernel with the same functions (Example 16.9, item 2).
3. The block of Proposition 50.1 is Y = m {5 Q(n . x), Q(h_w)}, and the Majorana block is supported on the line P_w Hom(V, W) P_v = Q(h_w) Hom(V, W) Q_V(h_v); the j-antilinear half of Y is i c(delta') Gamma_p with delta' = -J_w h/2 (for the orientation conventions of the Setting; +J_w h/2 for the opposite complex structure), the three-form term of lemma 49 whose dual is the quarter turn of the Higgs vector in the complex structure of the Majorana point.

*Proof.* The assignment B -> Q(f_B), f_B([w']) = 5 w'^* B w'/w'^* w', is complex linear and U(4)-equivariant, since f_{U B U^*}([U w']) = f_B([w']), the Fubini-Study measure is U(4)-invariant and the compression intertwines the action of U(4) on functions with its conjugation on the kernel; End_C(C^4) = C 1 + sl(4) under conjugation, with sl(4) irreducible, so by Schur's lemma Q(f_B) = alpha B_0 + beta tr(B) 1 with B_0 the traceless part. B = 1 gives f = 5 and Q(5) = 5, so beta = 5/4; B = Gamma_a gives f = 5 x_a and Q(5 x_a) = Gamma_a by Proposition 10.1 in the stated convention, so alpha = 1; hence Q(f_B) = B - (tr B/4) 1 + (5/4) tr(B) 1 = B + tr(B) 1. (As a check without Schur's lemma: with Q(f) = 4 int f P_{w'} dmu for the normalized measure, since int P_{w'} dmu = 1/4, the moments E|z_1|^4 = 1/10 and E|z_1 z_2|^2 = 1/20 on the unit sphere of C^4 give <w, Q(5 |<., w>|^2) w> = 2 and <jw, Q(5 |<., w>|^2) jw> = 1, and the U(3)-invariance under the stabilizer of w gives Q = 2 P_w + (1 - P_w) = P_w + 1.) (1) is B = Gamma_p. (2) is B = P_w, tr P_w = 1, minus the compression of the constant 1; the second sheet is a copy of the same kernel. (3) The first statement is Proposition 50.1 (1) with c(n) = 5 Q(n . x) (Proposition 10.1) and P_w = Q(h_w); the connector's line C e = C w v^* is P_w Hom(V, W) P_v. For the three-form: the j-antilinear odd Hermitian blocks are the real span of the i Gamma_a Gamma_p, a orthogonal to p (Proposition 46.1 (3)), so {c(h), rho_w}/2 = i c(delta') Gamma_p for a unique delta' in T_p S^4; rho_w = P_w - P_{jw} is the generator i times of U(1)_b, the Clifford action on L_p of the two-form omega_w(a, b) = <J_w a, b> up to normalization, and for a vector h and a two-form omega the anticommutator {c(h), c(omega)} is the Clifford action of the three-form h wedge omega, whose Hodge dual in four dimensions is the contraction of h with the dual two-form; omega_w is self-dual or anti-self-dual according to the chirality L_p on which it acts, so the dual of h wedge omega_w is (up to sign) the contraction of h with omega_w, which is J_w h. **Proposition 50.3 (the lepton and quark Yukawas separate exactly on the connector's algebra).** In the data of Corollary 16.8 with V_X = W, A_{F,X} = A_X an even subalgebra containing Gamma_p (H_R + H_L or a subalgebra containing the grading), V_Y = V = C^4 ungraded with an algebra A_Y in M_4(C), let P_1, ..., P_r be mutually orthogonal projections on V summing to 1 and Y_1, ..., Y_r odd Hermitian blocks on W, and define

    D x = sum_i Y_i x P_i  (x in Hom(V, W)),    D y = sum_i P_i y Y_i  (y in Hom(W, V)).

Then D is self-adjoint and odd, commutes with J_F, and satisfies order zero; its order-one defect is [[D, a], b°] x = sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x [b_Y, P_i] on Hom(V, W) and sum_i [P_i, a_Y] y [b_X, Y_i] on Hom(W, V). Hence:

1. (Universal block.) If all Y_i are equal, order one holds for every A_Y (Corollary 16.8).
2. (Split block.) If every P_i commutes with A_Y, order one holds for every choice of the Y_i.
3. (Converse.) If the Y_i are linearly independent, order one holds exactly when every P_i commutes with A_Y, since [Y_i, Gamma_p] = -2 Gamma_p Y_i are then linearly independent. For two terms, D = Y_l (x) P_v + Y_q (x) (1 - P_v): order one holds exactly when Y_l = Y_q or P_v commutes with A_Y.
4. On the Pati-Salam sheet algebra A_Y = M_4(C) of Example 16.9 the block is universal: the lepton line and the three colours carry the same Y, so in each generation the Dirac neutrino and the up quark have the mass m_+ and the charged lepton and the down quark the mass m_- (Proposition 46.2), the lepton-quark unified Yukawas k^nu = k^u, k^e = k^d of the spectral Pati-Salam model. The commutant of P_v in M_4(C) is {lambda P_v + m : m in M_3(C) on v^perp} = C + M_3(C), the second-sheet part of the algebra A(T_kappa) of Theorem 20.2 for a connector on the line v; so the split block Y_l (x) P_v + Y_q (x) (1 - P_v) and the connector on v select the same second-sheet algebra, and on it both keep order one.
5. (One lepton line.) If a split block with lines P_{v'}, Y_l != Y_q, and a connector on the line v both keep order one on an algebra A_X + A_Y with A_Y containing End(v^perp) (colour unbroken), then Cv' = Cv.

So the top-only family of lemma 21, Y_l = 0 and Y_q = {c(h), P_w} (x) P_g, is finite data of Corollary 16.8 with order one exactly on the connector's algebra C + H + M_3(C), the lepton line being the connector's line and the massive right-handed line the Majorana line.

*Proof.* Self-adjointness: <x', D x> = sum_i tr(x'^* Y_i x P_i) = sum_i tr((Y_i x' P_i)^* x), using Y_i^* = Y_i, P_i^* = P_i = P_i^2 and the cyclicity of the trace; the same on the second summand. Oddness: the grading is Gamma_p (x) 1 on Hom(V, W) and -1 (x) Gamma_p^c on Hom(W, V) (Theorem 16.7), and the Y_i anticommute with Gamma_p. J_F D (x, y) = ((sum P_i y Y_i)^*, (sum Y_i x P_i)^*) = (sum Y_i y^* P_i, sum P_i x^* Y_i) = D (y^*, x^*) = D J_F (x, y). Order zero holds since the left actions (a_X on x, a_Y on y) commute with the right actions (b_Y on x, b_X on y). For order one, [D, a] x = sum_i (Y_i a_X - a_X Y_i) x P_i = sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x P_i, since a_X acts on the left of x and P_i on the right; b° acts by x -> x b_Y, so [[D, a], b°] x = sum_i [Y_i, a_X] (x b_Y P_i - x P_i b_Y) = sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x [b_Y, P_i]; the second summand is the same computation with the roles exchanged. (1) With Y_i = Y the sum is [Y, a_X] x [b_Y, sum P_i] = 0. (2) is immediate. (3) If order one holds, then for every a_X, b_Y the operator x -> sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x [b_Y, P_i] vanishes, that is sum_i [Y_i, a_X] (x) [b_Y, P_i]^t = 0 in End(W) (x) End(V); with a_X = Gamma_p the first factors -2 Gamma_p Y_i are linearly independent, so every [b_Y, P_i] = 0. For two terms with P_2 = 1 - P_1 the defect is [Y_l - Y_q, a_X] x [b_Y, P_v], which vanishes for all a_X, b_Y, x exactly when Y_l = Y_q (so that the first factor is 0 for every a_X) or [b_Y, P_v] = 0 for all b_Y, the first factor being nonzero for a_X = Gamma_p otherwise. (4) P_v is central in no subalgebra of M_4(C) acting irreducibly on V, and its commutant is the block algebra End(Cv) + End(v^perp) = C + M_3(C), which is the second-sheet component of A(T_kappa) in Theorem 20.2, equation (6). (5) P_{v'} must commute with End(v^perp) + 0, so it preserves v^perp and restricts to it as a scalar; a rank-one projection cannot restrict to 1 on a three-space, so it restricts to 0, and v' lies in the orthogonal complement of v^perp, which is Cv. The last statement combines (4) and (5) with Proposition 50.1 (3). ∎

## Reading

The three finite data of the window, the axis, the connector and the Yukawa block (lemma 46, Reading), now stand in this relation: the connector marks the point [w] of the twistor fibre over the axis and the line v of the second sheet; the top block is the Higgs vector h in T_p S^4, read in the complex structure J_w of the Majorana point, applied to the projector on the Majorana line; and both projectors and the grading are compressions of height functions at the marked points, P_w = Q(h_w), P_v = Q_V(h_v), Gamma_p = Q(5 p . x). The rank-one pattern, in which the quaternionic and anti-quaternionic halves have equal norm (Proposition 46.2 (2)), is the statement that the three-form term of lemma 49 is the quarter turn of the Higgs vector, delta' = -J_w delta; the custodial breaking |Im(conj(q) q')| = (m_+^2 - m_-^2)/4 of lemma 46 is here |det(delta, delta')|, maximal for the top. The top and the neutrinos share the lines: the right-handed states of both lie on the Majorana line w and the left-handed ones on C c(h) w, the difference being the factor conj(V), the lepton line for the neutrinos and the three colours for the top; so the exchange of the top with the neutrinos of lemma 21 (Proposition 21.3) and lemma 34 (Proposition 5.2, with four families) is an exchange of the colour triplet with the generation triplet at fixed lines, recorded here as a reading. With the universal block the Dirac neutrino mass is the top mass, and the connector's kappa on the same line gives the neutral block [[0, m_t], [m_t, kappa]] on (nu_L, nu_R) in each generation, with light mass about m_t^2/kappa for kappa >> m_t (lemma 44, Proposition 44.2, with y = m_t); for m_nu = 0.05 eV this is kappa of about 6 x 10^14 GeV, between the meeting point of lemma 26 (2.2 x 10^14 GeV) and the unification scale, a remark under the hypothesis of the universal block, which the separation of Proposition 50.3 (4) removes. The heavy top, by the explicit-mass criterion, leaves the algebra C + M_3(C) of the label 9 with the summand C embedded along its left-handed line: the same algebra that lemma 06 reaches from the strict heavy limit, that lemma 44 retains along the seesaw scaling and that the corpus assumed for the dominant top; and the top-only family of lemma 21 is available as finite data of Corollary 16.8 only after the connector, since without the lepton line the block is universal and the leptons are as massive as the quarks. For the dictionary note: the Yukawa block of the top is the Higgs vector times the Majorana projector; the grading and the two projectors are compressed height functions; order one on the Pati-Salam sheet is the lepton-quark unification of the Yukawas, and its relaxation to the connector's algebra is their separation.

## Scope
