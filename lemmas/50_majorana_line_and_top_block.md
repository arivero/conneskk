**Proposition 50.3 (the lepton and quark Yukawas separate exactly on the connector's algebra).** In the data of Corollary 16.8 with V_X = W, A_{F,X} = A_X an even subalgebra containing Gamma_p (H_R + H_L or a subalgebra containing the grading), V_Y = V = C^4 ungraded with an algebra A_Y in M_4(C), let P_1, ..., P_r be mutually orthogonal projections on V summing to 1 and Y_1, ..., Y_r odd Hermitian blocks on W, and define

    D x = sum_i Y_i x P_i  (x in Hom(V, W)),    D y = sum_i P_i y Y_i  (y in Hom(W, V)).

Then D is self-adjoint and odd, commutes with J_F, and satisfies order zero; its order-one defect is [[D, a], b°] x = sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x [b_Y, P_i] on Hom(V, W) and sum_i [P_i, a_Y] y [b_X, Y_i] on Hom(W, V). Hence:

1. (Universal block.) If all Y_i are equal, order one holds for every A_Y (Corollary 16.8).
2. (Split block.) If every P_i commutes with A_Y, order one holds for every choice of the Y_i.
3. (Converse.) If the Y_i are linearly independent, order one holds exactly when every P_i commutes with A_Y, since [Y_i, Gamma_p] = -2 Gamma_p Y_i are then linearly independent. For two terms, D = Y_l (x) P_v + Y_q (x) (1 - P_v): order one holds exactly when Y_l = Y_q or P_v commutes with A_Y. Writing D x = sum_k E_k x Z_k with linearly independent E_k, order one holds exactly when every Z_k commutes with A_Y. Indeed [[D, a], b°] = [[D, b°], a] by order zero, and for a = Gamma_p the operator [D, b°] is odd and commutes with the grading, hence vanishes. This is Krajewski's decomposition of a first-order operator into a left-linear and a right-linear part, with the left-linear odd part zero because the grading lies in the left algebra.

*Proof.* Self-adjointness: <x', D x> = sum_i tr(x'^* Y_i x P_i) = sum_i tr((Y_i x' P_i)^* x), using Y_i^* = Y_i, P_i^* = P_i = P_i^2 and the cyclicity of the trace; the same on the second summand. ## Reading

The three finite data of the window, the axis, the connector and the Yukawa block (lemma 46, Reading), now stand in this relation: the connector marks the point [w] of the twistor fibre over the axis and the line v of the second sheet; under the eigenline condition and the up-type assignment, the top block is the Higgs vector h in T_p S^4, read in the complex structure J_w of the Majorana point, applied to the projector on the Majorana line; and both projectors and the grading are compressions of height functions at the marked points, P_w = Q(h_w), P_v = Q_V(h_v), Gamma_p = Q(5 p . x). The rank-one pattern, in which the quaternionic and anti-quaternionic halves have equal norm (Proposition 46.2 (2)), is the statement that the dual of the three-form term of lemma 49 is the quarter turn of the Higgs vector, delta' = -J_mu delta, in the complex structure of the massive right-handed point [mu] of the fibre, which is [w] under the eigenline condition. Conversely, every delta' orthogonal to delta and of the same length is -J_mu delta for exactly one [mu], so the rank-one blocks are the pairs (h, [mu]) (by lemma 49's formula, |delta| = |delta'| with delta orthogonal to delta' gives m_- = 0, hence rank one, hence c(h) P_mu). The custodial breaking |Im(conj(q) q')| = (m_+^2 - m_-^2)/4 of lemma 46 is here the area |delta wedge delta'|, which at fixed m_+^2 + m_-^2 is maximal exactly for the rank-one pattern. The top and the neutrinos share the lines. The right-handed states of both lie on the Majorana line w, and the left-handed ones on C c(h) w, which for the neutrinos is the neutral line of the embedding lambda_h of Proposition 50.4, and their Dirac line when the block is universal. The difference is the factor conj(V): the lepton line for the neutrinos, the three colours for the top. So the exchange of the top with the neutrinos of lemma 21 (Proposition 21.3) and lemma 34 (Proposition 5.2) can be read as a transposition of the colour triplet of one generation with the generation triplet of the lepton line, at fixed lines in W. In conj(V) (x) C^3 these are a 3 x 1 and a 1 x 3 block of a 4 x 3 array, and the transposition becomes a symmetry of the array only with four families, as in lemma 34. This is recorded as a reading. With the universal block the Dirac neutrino mass is the top mass, and the connector's kappa on the same line gives the neutral block [[0, m_t], [m_t, kappa]] on (nu_L, nu_R) in each generation, with light mass about m_t^2/kappa for kappa >> m_t (lemma 44, Proposition 44.2, with y = m_t); for m_nu = 0.05 eV and m_t = 173 GeV this is kappa of about 6 x 10^14 GeV, of the order of the meeting point of lemma 26 (2.2 x 10^14 GeV). With the top mass run to that scale, about half its low-energy value in the Standard Model (recalled, not computed here), kappa is about 1.5 x 10^14 GeV, so the comparison holds within the factors of the running. With kappa equal on the three generations, the same relation gives m_c^2/kappa, about 3 x 10^-6 eV, for the second neutrino, far below the solar scale of about 9 x 10^-3 eV. So the remark concerns the heaviest neutrino, and the others need a hierarchical Majorana matrix. It is a remark under the hypothesis of the universal block, which the separation of Proposition 50.3 removes. The heavy top, by the explicit-mass criterion, leaves the algebra C + M_3(C) of the label 9 with the summand C embedded along its left-handed line: the same algebra that lemma 06 reaches from the strict heavy limit, that lemma 44 retains along the seesaw scaling and that the corpus assumed for the dominant top; and the top-only family of lemma 21 keeps order one on the bimodule of Corollary 16.8 only on a second-sheet algebra inside the commutant of a lepton projector, which the connector supplies with its own line; on the Pati-Salam M_4(C) the block is universal and the leptons are as massive as the quarks. For the dictionary note: the Yukawa block of the top is the Higgs vector times the Majorana projector; the grading and the two projectors are compressed height functions; order one on the Pati-Salam sheet is the lepton-quark unification of the Yukawas, and its relaxation to the connector's algebra is their separation.

## Scope

Finite linear algebra on the quaternionic sheet with the Clifford structure of Example 16.9 and the connector of Theorem 20.2; one generation except in the remark after Proposition 50.1; the axis, the connector and the Yukawa block are inputs, as in lemmas 42 and 46; Proposition 50.2 holds in the convention of lemma 10, where Q(5 x_a) = Gamma_a, and the sign of delta' is fixed by the definition of J_w (it changes with J_{jw} = -J_w in place of J_w); the up-type assignment of Proposition 50.1 (3) is an input, which Proposition 21.1, in the scope of lemma 18 (the classical bosonic trace at fixed cutoff), supports for a quark-only block, while for the universal block the two cases give the same values; Proposition 50.3 concerns sheet-diagonal operators on the bimodule of Corollary 16.8 with projections on the second sheet, which for a split block lie outside the compressed form d_X (x) 1 of that corollary; its proof applies verbatim to three generations, with W (x) C^3 and Gamma_p (x) 1 in place of W and Gamma_p; Proposition 50.4 is the algebraic centralizer by the criterion [D_F, a] = 0, and Counterexample 18.8 shows that mass decoupling in the spectral action does not select it dynamically. Nothing here derives the block, the connector or the axis, and the identity of Proposition 50.2 is Example 16.9, item 2, extended complex-linearly: at level one on CP^3 the contravariant symbol of B is 5 times its covariant symbol w'^* B w'/w'^* w' minus tr(B), so h_w is the contravariant symbol of P_w (Berezin's covariant and contravariant symbols; paper/LITERATURE.md, section P); in general, at level one on CP^{N-1}, Q((N + 1) sigma_B - tr(B)) = B with Q(f) = N int f P dmu.
