## Reading

The three views give the same object, the sheet W tensored with a line: in the two-sheet data the line is the lepton line Cv of the second sheet, in nine dimensions it is the left-handed Weyl spinor of spacetime, and in the flavour reading the 24 states of three generations are the adjoint of the five light quarks. The connector that the lepton generation needs, in lemma 49's reading, is the Majorana block itself: its order-one condition identifies the C of the right-handed singlets with the C of the antileptons exactly when the Majorana line is a weight line of the lifted transposition, the eigenline condition that lemmas 46, 49 and 50 met from three sides, and the identification yields the Standard Model algebra on the thirty-two states with the lepton module as the summand H_F^(1) and, over the lepton algebra C + H alone, a nondegenerate trace-form pairing, where lemma 41 found a degenerate one over the three summands: the degeneracy of the Standard Model pairing is the skewness of a three-by-three form, and the lepton generation by itself has none. The nine-dimensional view is the one the project's labels name: a single Majorana spinor of R^{1,8} on M^4 x K_5 is one lepton generation with nu_R, its weak isospin and T_3R being the stabilizer K_p of the fibre, its Dirac masses the internal momenta shifted by the spin structure, quantized because a Majorana field has no Wilson line, and its Majorana mass wall data that selects the KO-7 side (T2c); the quark generation is the same fibre with a colour triplet, which is complex and admits the Wilson line of lemma 51, a continuous mass parameter, so in this reading the lepton masses are quantized and the quark masses are not, a difference to test against the hierarchies of T8. For the third view, the adjoint of the flavour SU(5) is the representation of the mesons of the five light quarks, and the three lepton generations have exactly its electric charges, with the neutral leptons in the place of the flavour gauge algebra and the charged leptons in the place of the up-antidown mesons, the generation index matching the down flavours (d, s, b) and the two charged lepton states the up flavours (u, c): the content of the user's counting, recorded here as a representation statement under U(1)_em with the finer matching a reading; the quark partners sit in the (15, 3-bar) with the charge-4/3 sector of Proposition 21.4, and the top is outside both. Lead: the 24 of the leptons and the 24 of the adjoint are both the fibre of a line bundle tensored with a generation triplet, and the flavour group SU(2)_u x SU(3)_d acts on the adjoint while nothing in the finite geometry acts on the generation index; a three-generation finite geometry in which the generation space carries the 3-bar of a flavour SU(3)_d would make the reading a structure.

## Scope
