# Weak multiplets on the flag and the conic in the monopole collapse

The proposed six-dimensional intermediate spaces are the product $X=\mathbb P(V)\times\mathbb P^1$ and the incidence flag $F\subset\mathbb P(V)\times\mathbb P(V^*)$, with $V=\mathbb C^3$. We fix the defining colour action of $SU(3)$ and use projective spaces of **lines**. We first vary the fermion bundle and compute its exact kernel. We then hold the monopole bundle and base metric fixed and shrink the two-sphere fibres of $N(1,0)\to S^5$. The observable in this second family is the Dirac spectrum in a fixed, finite energy window.

## The two surfaces and their symmetry

Let $\pi_1,\pi_2$ be the two projections of $F$, and write $\mathcal O_F(a,b)$ for the restricted hyperplane bundles in that order. Over a line in $\mathbb P(V)$ the product restricts to $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$. On the flag the universal quotient $V/p$ restricts to $\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O$, giving the Hirzebruch surface $\mathbb F_1$. Its odd intersection form distinguishes it from the product even after adding hyperbolic planes by connected sum. [Altavilla et al., Corollary 3.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.11100)

Over a smooth conic $C$ both surfaces are products. On the flag a point of $B=\pi_1^{-1}(C)$ is the ordered pair of intersections of a projective line with $C$, with the marked point first. The tangent gives a repeated point. Thus $B=C\times C$ and $f=\pi_2|_B$ is the quotient by interchange, branched on the dual conic $C^\vee$. The secant coefficients have degree $(1,1)$, giving $f^*\mathcal O(1)=\mathcal O_B(1,1)$. Pulling the Hopf circle of unit covectors back along $f$ gives $N_C\to B$ of Chern class $\pm(1,1)$ and a double cover $N_C\to S^5$ branched over $\mathbb{RP}^3$ (the circle bundle of degree $\pm2$ over $C^\vee$). The total space upstairs is the circle bundle $T^{1,1}\cong S^2\times S^3$. These constructions fix the conic. Colour takes the construction for $C$ to the one for its translate. [Topology of the circle bundle](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9807080)

For the product metric, colour on the first factor commutes with weak rotations on the second. Their effective action on points is $PSU(3)\times SO(3)$. On $F=SU(3)/T^2$ an equivariant diffeomorphism is determined by the image $gT^2$ of the identity coset. This coset is fixed by $T^2$ exactly when $g$ normalizes $T^2$. The centralizer of the given action in diffeomorphisms is therefore $N(T^2)/T^2\simeq S_3$. A commuting connected weak group on this flag must act elsewhere, for example on a multiplicity of the fermion bundle. The product's circle lift $M^{111}$ and the action of its centres on ordinary spinors are distinct tests. [Lemma 32, centre action and gauge twists](../results/32_z6_kernel_tensor_modes.md)

## Bulk chiral multiplets

**Proposition 53.1.** On the flag with an invariant Kähler metric and homogeneous Chern connections, the spin Dirac operator twisted by $\mathcal O_F(a,b)$ has index $ab(a+b)/2$. For $(a,b)=(0,k)$ its full kernel is empty for every integer $k$. The twists $(1,2),(1,1),(2,1)$ have purely even kernels $V,\mathbb C,V^*$, respectively. On the product, the canonical spin$^c$ Dirac operator twisted by $\mathcal O_X(-4,1)$ has purely even kernel $(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)$ under the lifted geometric colour and weak groups.

**Proof.** The incidence equation has bidegree $(1,1)$. Adjunction gives $K_F^{1/2}=\mathcal O_F(-1,-1)$, with its $SU(3)$ lift. The spinor kernel consists of harmonic $(0,j)$-forms with coefficients in $\mathcal O_F(a-1,b-1)$, with chirality $(-1)^j$. The divisor sequence on the product of projective planes computes its Euler characteristic as
$$
\frac{a(a+1)b(b+1)-a(a-1)b(b-1)}4=\frac{ab(a+b)}2 .
$$
For $a=0$ the coefficient restricts to $\mathcal O(-1)$ on every $\pi_2$ fibre, so both fibre cohomology groups and both direct images vanish. For the three positive examples the coefficient is the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ from the second plane, the trivial line, or the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ from the first plane. Only degree zero occurs, and linear forms on $V^*$ and $V$ give $V$ and $V^*$. On $X$ the canonical spin$^c$ spinors are $(0,*)$-forms. Serre duality gives $H^2(\mathbb P(V),\mathcal O(-4))=V$ equivariantly for $SU(3)$. The only other factor is $H^0(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(1))=(\mathbb C^2)^*\simeq\mathbf2$. This proves the product claim, including its spin$^c$ choice on the nonspin manifold. $\square$

On the flag let $SU(2)_W$ act on a trivial parallel multiplicity $W$ and let $z\in U(1)$ act by $z^n$ with $n=6Y$. These lifts commute with the colour lift on spinors and on $\mathcal O_F(a,b)$. The following direct sum gives the usual left-handed representations, writing charge-conjugate singlets as left-handed fields.

| Field | Flag twist $(a,b)$ | $W$ | $n$ | Colour kernel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $Q_L$ | $(1,2)$ | $\mathbf2$ | $1$ | $\mathbf3$ |
| $L_L$ | $(1,1)$ | $\mathbf2$ | $-3$ | $\mathbf1$ |
| $u^c$ | $(2,1)$ | $\mathbf1$ | $-4$ | $\overline{\mathbf3}$ |
| $d^c$ | $(2,1)$ | $\mathbf1$ | $2$ | $\overline{\mathbf3}$ |
| $e^c$ | $(1,1)$ | $\mathbf1$ | $6$ | $\mathbf1$ |
| $\nu^c$ (optional) | $(1,1)$ | $\mathbf1$ | $0$ | $\mathbf1$ |

On each row the central element $(e^{2\pi i/3}I_3,-I_2,e^{i\pi/3})$ acts trivially, so the action descends to the Standard Model quotient by $\mathbb Z_6$. Compactness gives square-integrable internal modes. With a complex ten-dimensional Weyl fermion of the appropriate chirality on a direct product and the uncoupled external Dirac operator, these even modes give massless four-dimensional Weyl fields. A ten-dimensional Dirac fermion supplies both external chiralities. The choices of summands, multiplicities and $U(1)$ characters are inputs. Identifying that $U(1)$ with a particular geometric circle would impose an additional condition. Gauging their action, implementing a Connes real structure and generating Yukawa couplings require further data. [Global-group convention](../results/32_z6_kernel_tensor_modes.md)

## Localization on a fixed conic

**Proposition 53.2.** The intrinsic spin Dirac operator on $B$ twisted by $f^*\mathcal O(k)$ has index $k^2$. There is an explicit graded defect operator on $F$ with one normalizable even zero mode concentrating on $B$.

**Proof.** On the product $B$, $K_B^{1/2}=\mathcal O_B(-1,-1)$. The intrinsic spinor cohomology is that of $\mathcal O_B(k-1,k-1)$ with Euler characteristic $k^2$. For $k=1$ its sole class is the constant. For comparison the two ambient hyperplanes restrict to $\mathcal O_B(2,0)$ and $\mathcal O_B(1,1)$, so $K_F^{1/2}|_B=\mathcal O_B(-3,-1)$. Adjunction supplies the half-normal factor $\mathcal O_B(2,0)$ in the intrinsic spin bundle. This accounts for the different operators in the surface index and the vanishing bulk example.

Let $s_C$ be the conic equation pulled back as a section of $\mathcal O_F(2,0)$. Take the Dolbeault resolution of the two-term complex $\mathcal O_F(-2,0)\xrightarrow{\,t s_C\,}\mathcal O_F$, in degrees $-1,0$, with fixed smooth Hermitian metrics. For $t>0$ define $Q_t$ as the total differential, with the bundle map acting by $(-1)^j t s_C$ on $(0,j)$-forms of the first summand. Holomorphicity gives $Q_t^2=0$. On the compact flag $D_t=\sqrt2(Q_t+Q_t^*)$ has the usual self-adjoint elliptic domain $H^1$. The divisor sequence resolves $\mathcal O_B$. Hodge theory identifies the kernel with $H^*(B,\mathcal O_B)=\mathbb C$ in even total degree. In the square, the derivative terms linear in $t$ cancel at the level of principal symbols by the total grading, leaving a bounded endomorphism $tA$ and the scalar potential $2t^2|s_C|^2$ in addition to $D_0^2$. For every fixed neighbourhood $U$ of $B$, a unit zero mode therefore has squared norm outside $U$ bounded by $\|A\|/(2t\,\inf_{F\setminus U}|s_C|^2)$. $\square$

This example uses a graded bundle and a prescribed mass profile. Its parity is the total complex parity. A fixed divisor preserves its stabilizer. A colour-equivariant localization mechanism and the identification of that parity with physical chirality would require a further construction. The bulk multiplets in Proposition 53.1 preserve the full colour action without selecting a conic.

## The monopole window over the five-sphere

**Proposition 53.3.** Let $E_\lambda=\ker\lambda\subset V$ for a unit covector $\lambda\in S(V^*)=S^5$. Write $p:N(1,0)=\mathbb P(E)\to S^5$, and use the relative hyperplane line $L=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P(E)}(1)$. Fix the round base metric, the reductive principal $SU(2)$ connection on $SU(3)\to S^5$, and the associated invariant round fibre metric and unitary monopole connection. With fibre metric scaled by $\varepsilon^2$ and the spin Dirac operator twisted by $L^2$, the spectrum in bounded intervals whose ends avoid the limiting spectrum converges with multiplicities to that of $D_{S^5,E^*}$. The conic double cover recovers its rank-two coefficient bundle, including at the branch. The limiting operator has odd kernel parity.

**Proof.** A unit covector and the fixed volume form on $V$ trivialize $\det E$ and give its $SU(2)$ frames. Thus the relative spin half-canonical line is $L^{-1}$. On $\mathbb P(E_\lambda)$ the positive vertical zero modes of the twist $L^2$ are $H^0(\mathcal O(1))=E_\lambda^*$, and there are no negative ones. The round unit-radius fibre has a positive gap $\delta$ off this kernel. The trivial spin twist has coefficient $\mathcal O(-1)$ on the fibre and has no vertical zero modes. These are two different flux choices on the same metric.

Let $Z_\lambda=C\cap\mathbb P(E_\lambda)$ with intersection multiplicity. It has length two because a smooth conic contains no line. The evaluation sequence on the fibre is
$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(-1)\longrightarrow\mathcal O(1)
  \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Z_\lambda}(1)\longrightarrow0 .
$$
Both cohomology groups of $\mathcal O(-1)$ vanish. At a secant the resulting isomorphism takes a vertical zero mode to its values at the two marked points. At a tangent it takes the first jet in the **fibre** direction, including the value. The isomorphism varies smoothly through the branch. Indeed on $F$ the divisor sequence for $B$ twisted by $\mathcal O_F(1,0)$ has kernel $\mathcal O_F(-1,0)$ with both $\pi_2$ direct images zero. Pushing down and pulling back along $S^5\to\mathbb P(V^*)$ gives precisely $E^*$. This use of the double cover includes the length-two fibre at a tangent. The metric and connection on $E^*$ come from the homogeneous fibration. For positive monopole charge $n$, the source instead has dimension $n$ and the restriction kernel is $H^0(\mathcal O(n-3))$. Thus charge two is the unique positive charge for which the two intersection data parametrize the entire vertical kernel freely.

For the connection metric $g_\varepsilon=p^*g_{S^5}+\varepsilon^2g_{\rm vert}$ the fibres are totally geodesic. After the constant fibre-volume identification of the spinor spaces, write $D_v$ for the unit vertical operator, $\Gamma_v$ for vertical chirality and $D_h$ for the base Dirac coupled to horizontal transport. That transport is by $SU(2)$ isometries preserving the monopole connection and spin lift. It commutes with $D_v$ and $\Gamma_v$. The split operator
$$
D_\varepsilon^0=\varepsilon^{-1}D_v+\Gamma_v D_h,\qquad
(D_\varepsilon^0)^2=\varepsilon^{-2}D_v^2+D_h^2
$$
therefore restricts to $D_{S^5,E^*}$ on the kernel and has absolute spectrum at least $\delta/\varepsilon$ on its complement. For the Levi-Civita Dirac with the indicated twist the submersion formula adds a bounded curvature endomorphism of norm at most $c\varepsilon$. Indeed the fibres have zero mean curvature. In an adapted unit frame the Koszul formula expresses the mixed terms of the Levi-Civita connection relative to the split connection through $\tfrac12 g_\varepsilon([X,Y]^{\rm vert},U)$, with $X,Y$ horizontal lifts and $U$ vertical unit. These are $O(\varepsilon)$ since the unscaled vertical bracket is fixed. Clifford contraction preserves this order for the spinor endomorphism. The even-dimensional version in the reference also applies after taking the product of total space and base with a periodic circle and restricting to zero circle momentum. The resolvent identity on contours around isolated base eigenvalues now gives convergence of the spectral projections in norm under the kernel identification and equality of their ranks for small $\varepsilon$. [Kaad and van Suijlekom, submersion Dirac formula](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.02873) · [Canonical connection on the monopole kernels](02_invariant_connections_monopole.md#part-a--canonical-connection)

The frame bundle $SU(3)\to S^5$ represents the nonzero class in $\pi_4(SU(2))=\mathbb Z_2$ by the homotopy sequence and $\pi_4(SU(3))=0$. Its fundamental dual is equivalent to the fundamental. The quaternionic structures on five-dimensional spinors and on $E^*$ together give the real structure for the mod-two index. García-Etxebarria and Montero explicitly identify a bundle over $S^5$ whose index is one immediately after their equation (3.4). The trivial bundle has zero index by the positive-scalar-curvature formula on the round sphere. There are just two bundle classes, so the nonzero index is that of $E^*$. Hence an odd-dimensional cluster approaches zero. Persistence of an exact zero at positive $\varepsilon$ and a four-dimensional Weyl chirality require separate arguments. $\square$ [García-Etxebarria and Montero, §3.1 after (3.4)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.00009#page=18) · [Repository literature record, section G](../paper/LITERATURE.md)

The $SU(2)$ acting on $E^*$ is the isotropy and holonomy of the fibration. Its parallel endomorphisms are scalars by Lemma 02. Independent constant weak rotations can instead act on a repeated copy of the bundle. The two marked values also have a precise limitation as Connes data. The algebra of the length-two intersection is $\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C$ at a secant and $\mathbb C[u]/(u^2)$ at a tangent. The tangent algebra has no faithful representation as a commutative algebra closed under Hilbert adjoints, since a normal nilpotent operator is zero. The rank-two fermion space extends through the branch. Extending the observable algebra and Dirac operator of a two-point spectral triple requires additional choices. The intrinsic Dirac operator of the five-manifold $N_C$ would be a separate spectral problem.

## Relation to scale selection

The flag multiplets reproduce the one-generation traces $\operatorname{Tr}T_3^2=\operatorname{Tr}T_c^2=2$ and $\operatorname{Tr}Y^2=10/3$ for $\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf3}T_c^2=1/2$. A common trace in a gauge action therefore gives $\sin^2\theta_W=3/8$ at tree-level matching with all these modes included. This agreement uses the assigned representations. In Proposition 53.3 we prescribe the monopole charge and shrink the fibre at fixed base radius. The omitted vertical modes rise as $1/\varepsilon$ and the retained operator stays at the base scale. This supplies an explicit spectrum for the distinction between changing all masses together and separating a tower. An action selecting the base radius, a physical charged-current sector and threshold matching for $\alpha(0)$ and QCD remain open.
