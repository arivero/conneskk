The three matrices, the orientation cycle and the traces were computed by hand and confirmed with numpy on the 32-state representation (one generation, Dirac neutrinos; script not kept, by the rule of the project).

**Outcome.** Along the chain of heavy operators, A_PS = H_R + H_L + M_4(C) -> A_SM = C + H + M_3(C) -> B = C + M_3(C), the finite data keep the KO-6 signs, order zero and the first-order condition of Theorem 20.2, and they lose the other two axioms of a finite real spectral triple: the trace-form Poincaré duality fails at every step, with intersection forms of rank 2, 2 and 0 (the last because every range rho(p_i) rho(p_j)° in the light block has graded dimension zero, the bimodule balance behind the KO-7 doubling of lemma 06), and orientability holds at the Pati-Salam step, for a sheet-diagonal D of the form of Corollary 16.8, and fails at the two later ones, through the pair nu_R, J nu_R and the pair e_R, J e_R at the Standard Model step and through the central mass blocks at the third. The unimodularity trace of the abelian generator changes along the chain, (4i, 12i) on the full space, (2i, 12i) with the Majorana pair removed, (-2i, 6i) with the up-type sector removed and (-4i, 6i) on the light block, so the inherited lift and a lift reimposed later impose different hypercharge constraints, and only the inherited one gives the photon its charges.

## Setting

One generation with Dirac neutrinos, in the convention of lemma 06, Result 5: F = C + C^3 (lepton line and colour), H_p = (C^2 (x) F)_L + (C^2 (x) F)_R, H = H_p + H_pbar, dim H = 32. For a = (lambda, q, m) in A_SM, the left action is q (x) I_4 on the left-handed particles, Q_lambda (x) I_4 = diag(lambda, conj lambda) (x) I_4 on the right-handed ones, and I_2 (x) B_{lambda, m} = I_2 (x) diag(lambda, m) on the antiparticles; J(p, pbar) = (K pbar, K p); gamma = +1 on p_L and pbar_R, -1 on p_R and pbar_L. The opposite action is a° = J a* J^{-1}. The Pati-Salam parent acts by q_L (x) I_4 on p_L, q_R (x) I_4 on p_R and I_2 (x) m_4 on the antiparticles, and A_SM is its centralizer under the heavy Majorana block (Theorem 20.2, rank one, kappa != 0). The light block of the third step is the 18-dimensional space of d_L, d_R (three colours), e_L, e_R, nu_L and their conjugates, on which B = {(lambda, Q_lambda, m)} acts (lemma 06, Proposition 5), with the kept mass blocks commuting with B (lemma 17, Corollary 17.4).

Minimal projections, one per summand: p_R' = (1, 0, 0), p_L' = (0, 1, 0), p_4 = (0, 0, e_11) for A_PS; p_1 = (1, 0, 0), p_2 = (0, 1_H, 0), p_3 = (0, 0, e_11) for A_SM; for B the minimal projection of its summand C is (1, Q_1, 0) = (1, 1_H, 0) = p_1 + p_2, which is the identity on the nine light particles and on the light antileptons, and p_3 is that of M_3(C). The trace form is Q_ij = Tr(gamma rho(p_i) rho(p_j)°).

## Poincaré duality

**Lemma 41.1 (the trace form is skew).** For a finite real triple with J^2 = +-1, J gamma = -gamma J, an algebra even for gamma and order zero, Q_ij = -Q_ji. *Proof.* For an antiunitary J, Tr(J A J^{-1}) = conj(Tr A). Then Q_ij = conj Tr(J gamma p_i p_j° J^{-1}) = -conj Tr(gamma p_i° p_j) = -conj Tr(gamma p_j p_i°) = -conj(Q_ji) = -Q_ji, using order zero to commute p_i° past p_j and the reality of Q. ∎ (This is the observation of Ćaćić for KO-dimension 6; the corpus recorded it as rs_N7.)

**Theorem 41.2 (the three forms).** In the bases above,

    Q_PS = [[0, 0, -2], [0, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]],   Q_SM = [[0, -2, -2], [2, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]],   Q_B = [[0, 0], [0, 0]].

Each has even rank: Q_PS and Q_SM have rank 2 with kernels spanned by p_R' + p_L' and by p_1 + p_2 - p_3, and Q_B vanishes. So the trace-form Poincaré duality fails at each step of the chain: at the first two because a skew form on an odd-rank K_0 is singular, at the third because every range rho(p_i) rho(p_j)° in the light block has graded dimension zero.

*Proof.* Each entry counts, with the sign of gamma, the states on which the left projection p_i and the right projection p_j° both act as the identity. For Q_SM: p_1 acts as the identity on all of p_R (eight states) and on the lepton line of pbar_L and pbar_R; p_2 on p_L; p_3 on one colour of pbar_L and pbar_R; and p_1°, p_2°, p_3° are the J-transports, so p_1° is the identity on pbar_R and on the lepton line of p_L, p_R, p_2° on pbar_L, p_3° on one colour of p_L, p_R. Then Q_12 counts the lepton line of pbar_L, two states of grading -1; Q_13 one colour of p_R, two states of grading -1; Q_23 one colour of p_L, two states of grading +1; the diagonal entries vanish by Lemma 41.1 (directly: Q_11 counts the lepton line of p_R and of pbar_R with opposite signs). Q_PS follows in the same way with p_R' the identity on p_R, p_L' on p_L and p_4 on one line of F on the antiparticles: Q_{R L} counts p_R against pbar_L, which is empty, and Q_{R 4}, Q_{L 4} count one line of p_R and of p_L. For Q_B, on the light block the projection (1, 1_H, 0) of the summand C is the identity on the nine light particles and on the three light antileptons, p_3 on one colour of dbar_L, dbar_R, and p_3° on one colour of d_L, d_R; Q_13 counts d_L and d_R of one colour, of opposite gradings, so it vanishes, and Q_11 vanishes by Lemma 41.1 (on the particles its range is nu_L, e_L, e_R with graded count +1, on the antiparticles the conjugates with -1). With the projection (1, 0, 0), which lies outside B, the form would be [[0, -1], [1, 0]]. The kernels are read off. ∎

**Remark 41.3 (the form is the bimodule balance).** Q_ij is the graded dimension of the range of rho(p_i) rho(p_j)°, one colour of an isotypic component when p_j = e_11. It vanishes identically exactly when every such range has equal numbers of states of the two gradings, which is the bimodule balance condition behind the doubling of lemma 06 and is weaker than physical vectorlikeness: the light block of the third step keeps the unpaired nu_L, whose particle content restricted to SU(3) x U(1)_em has [V_L] - [V_R] = (1)_0, nonzero, while Q_B = 0, because the trace form sees the full real space, where the neutral class cancels against its conjugate and the characters lambda and conj(lambda) of the real summand C share one projection (lemma 06 draws the same distinction in its no-go under [U, Q] = 0). On the full 32-state space with nu_R, where B acts and the Standard Model index restricts to zero, the form of B also vanishes, and there the two statements agree. At the first two steps the form has rank 2 because the quark doublets and the leptons carry the chiral asymmetry; the kernel vector p_1 + p_2 - p_3 of Q_SM is the direction along which the graded counts of the three summands cancel.

## Orientability

**Proposition 41.4.** (i) The Pati-Salam data are orientable: gamma = rho(p_L' - p_R') rho(1_4)° - rho(1_4) rho(p_L' - p_R')°. (ii) The Standard Model data are not, with or without a right-handed neutrino: nu_R and J nu_R carry the same left character lambda and the same right character lambda and have opposite gradings, so no sum of left-right products separates them (Stephan 2006, hep-th/0610097, for the KO-6 models with neutrinos); and e_R carries (conj lambda, lambda) with gamma = -1 while J e_R carries (lambda, conj lambda) with gamma = +1, on which a real cycle takes complex-conjugate values and cannot be -1 on one and +1 on the other, an obstruction that persists when the nu_R pair is removed (Python, numpy: removing the nu_R and e_R pairs restores orientability). (iii) The light block of the third step is not: the kept mass blocks are nonzero and commute with B (lemma 17, Corollary 17.4), and a nonzero D commuting with both actions commutes with every orientation cycle while anticommuting with gamma; directly, d_L and d_R of one colour carry the same characters (conj lambda on the left, e_11 on the right) and opposite gradings.

*Proof.* (i) On the particles rho(1_4)° is the identity and rho(1_4) vanishes, so the right side is p_L' - p_R', which is +1 on p_L and -1 on p_R; on the antiparticles rho(p_L' - p_R') vanishes and rho(1_4) is the identity, so the right side is -(p_L' - p_R')°, which is -1 on pbar_L and +1 on pbar_R. This is gamma. (ii) and (iii): a cycle c = sum rho(a_i) rho(b_i)° takes the same value on two states with the same pair of characters, while gamma takes opposite values. For the first sentence of (iii), if [D, rho(a)] = 0 = [D, rho(b)°] for all a, b then [D, c] = 0 for every cycle, and c = gamma would give D gamma = gamma D, which with D gamma = -gamma D forces D = 0; this is the corpus statement rs_N9 (historical/corpus/connes_kk_research_state.md, section 5.4), restated here with its proof. ∎

The explicit model of results/orientability_under_heavy_centralizer.md shows the same loss under a heavy centralizer in a four-summand toy; here it happens in the physical chain, at the first heavy step when nu_R is present and at the second step in every case.

## Unimodularity

**Proposition 41.5.** Let X_lambda be the generator of the abelian direction of the summand C, acting in each algebra as its own element: in A_SM the element (i, 0, 0), which acts as diag(i, -i) (x) I_4 on p_R and as i on the lepton line of the antiparticles; in B the element (i, Q_i, 0), which in addition acts as diag(i, -i) (x) I_4 on p_L, that is by i on nu_L and u_L and by -i on e_L and d_L. Let X_c be the common colour phase, i on the three colours of the antiparticles. The traces of the left action are

    full H, 32 states, A_SM:                          Tr X_lambda = 4i,   Tr X_c = 12i;
    Majorana pair removed, 30 states, A_SM:            2i,                12i;
    up-type sector removed, nu_R kept, 20 states, B:  -2i,                 6i;
    light block of the third step, 18 states, B:      -4i,                 6i.

So the unimodularity condition Tr A = 0 of Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli on the one-form (the left action; the adjoint action u J u J^{-1} has determinant one for every unitary, so a condition on it would be empty), imposed on the parent, gives theta_lambda + 3 theta_c = 0 between the two abelian phases, the infinitesimal form of lambda det m = 1, and reproduces lambda^2 (det m)^4 at the 30-state step recorded by the corpus; the same condition reimposed on the light block gives -4 theta_lambda + 6 theta_c = 0, a different embedding of the surviving abelian generator. Under the adjoint action, normalized so that the electron has charge -1, the inherited condition gives the charges (2/3, -1/3, -1, 0) to (u, d, e, nu) and the reimposed one gives (1/6, -5/6, -1, 0): only the inherited lift gives the photon. This is the condition Proposition 22.8 of lemma 22 uses, where the ten generators of u(C_F) reduce to the nine of the photon and the gluons.

*Proof.* Count the states with their phases. Full space: Q_i is traceless on p_R and X_lambda vanishes on p_L, so only the antilepton line contributes, two weak components in two sectors, 4i; X_c has 12i on the antiquarks. Removing nu_R (phase +i) and J nu_R (antilepton line, +i) gives 2i. For B, (i, Q_i, 0) is traceless on p_L and on p_R by itself; removing the up-type sector removes u_L and u_R, three colours each with phase +i, and their conjugates, which carry no abelian phase, so the particle part gives -3i on p_L (nu_L at +i, d_L at -3i, e_L at -i) and -3i on p_R, and the antilepton line keeps 4i: -2i on 20 states; the antiquarks halve to 6i. Removing also nu_R and J nu_R takes i twice: -4i on 18 states. ∎

With the element (i, 0, 0) of A_SM on all four spaces the traces would be 4i, 2i, i, -i; on the last two spaces only B acts, so its element (i, Q_i, 0) is the one to use.

## Scope

One generation with Dirac neutrinos in the stated representation; the trace form on minimal projections as the test of Poincaré duality (a real Kasparov duality is a different question, as the corpus noted); the strict projections of lemma 06 for the third step; at the Pati-Salam step a sheet-diagonal D of the form of Corollary 16.8 (a Pati-Salam-symmetric Yukawa with no Majorana block), since with the Majorana block order one fails there, which is how Theorem 20.2 selects A_SM. With three generations the matrices multiply by three and nothing else changes. The direction the result points to: the finite triple of the main paper carries, along the chain, exactly the data that lemma 22 derives from the interval (signs, order zero, first order), and none that it does not; the two failed axioms are the ones a Kaluza-Klein parent would have to supply through its topology (orientability, Poincaré duality for the parent manifold), and the vanishing of the form at the third step is the bimodule balance of lemma 06, which coexists with the unpaired nu_L.
