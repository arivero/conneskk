**Outcome.** Along the chain of heavy operators, A_PS = H_R + H_L + M_4(C) -> A_SM = C + H + M_3(C) -> B = C + M_3(C), the finite data keep the KO-6 signs, order zero and the first-order condition of Theorem 20.2, and they lose the other two axioms of a finite real spectral triple: the trace-form Poincaré duality fails at every step, with intersection forms of rank 2, 2 and 0, and orientability holds at the Pati-Salam step and fails at the two later ones, for two different reasons. ## Setting

One generation with Dirac neutrinos, in the convention of lemma 06, Result 5: F = C + C^3 (lepton line and colour), H_p = (C^2 (x) F)_L + (C^2 (x) F)_R, H = H_p + H_pbar, dim H = 32. For a = (lambda, q, m) in A_SM, the left action is q (x) I_4 on the left-handed particles, Q_lambda (x) I_4 = diag(lambda, conj lambda) (x) I_4 on the right-handed ones, and I_2 (x) B_{lambda, m} = I_2 (x) diag(lambda, m) on the antiparticles; J(p, pbar) = (K pbar, K p); gamma = +1 on p_L and pbar_R, -1 on p_R and pbar_L. The opposite action is a° = J a* J^{-1}. The Pati-Salam parent acts by q_L (x) I_4 on p_L, q_R (x) I_4 on p_R and I_2 (x) m_4 on the antiparticles, and A_SM is its centralizer under the heavy Majorana block (Theorem 20.2, rank one, kappa != 0). The light block of the third step is the 18-dimensional space of d_L, d_R (three colours), e_L, e_R, nu_L and their conjugates, on which B = {(lambda, Q_lambda, m)} acts (lemma 06, Proposition 5), with the kept mass blocks commuting with B (lemma 17, Corollary 17.4).

Minimal projections, one per summand: p_R' = (1, 0, 0), p_L' = (0, 1, 0), p_4 = (0, 0, e_11) for A_PS; p_1 = (1, 0, 0), p_2 = (0, 1_H, 0), p_3 = (0, 0, e_11) for A_SM; p_1, p_3 for B. The trace form is Q_ij = Tr(gamma rho(p_i) rho(p_j)°).

## Poincaré duality

**Lemma 41.1 (the trace form is skew).** For a finite real triple with J^2 = 1, J gamma = -gamma J and order zero, Q_ij = -Q_ji. *Proof.* For an antiunitary J, Tr(J A J^{-1}) = conj(Tr A). Then Q_ij = conj Tr(J gamma p_i p_j° J^{-1}) = -conj Tr(gamma p_i° p_j) = -conj Tr(gamma p_j p_i°) = -conj(Q_ji) = -Q_ji, using order zero to commute p_i° past p_j and the reality of Q. ∎ (This is the observation of Ćaćić for KO-dimension 6; the corpus recorded it as rs_N7.)

**Theorem 41.2 (the three forms).** In the bases above,

    Q_PS = [[0, 0, -2], [0, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]],   Q_SM = [[0, -2, -2], [2, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]],   Q_B = [[0, 0], [0, 0]].

Each has even rank: Q_PS and Q_SM have rank 2 with kernels spanned by p_R' + p_L' and by p_1 + p_2 - p_3, and Q_B vanishes. So the trace-form Poincaré duality fails at each step of the chain: at the first two because a skew form on an odd-rank K_0 is singular, at the third because the light block is vectorlike.

*Proof.* Each entry counts, with the sign of gamma, the states on which the left projection p_i and the right projection p_j° both act as the identity. For Q_SM: p_1 acts as the identity on all of p_R (eight states) and on the lepton line of pbar_L and pbar_R; p_2 on p_L; p_3 on one colour of pbar_L and pbar_R; and p_1°, p_2°, p_3° are the J-transports, so p_1° is the identity on pbar_R and on the lepton line of p_L, p_R, p_2° on pbar_L, p_3° on one colour of p_L, p_R. Then Q_12 counts the lepton line of pbar_L, two states of grading -1; Q_13 one colour of p_R, two states of grading -1; Q_23 one colour of p_L, two states of grading +1; the diagonal entries vanish by Lemma 41.1 (directly: Q_11 counts the lepton line of p_R and of pbar_R with opposite signs). Q_PS follows in the same way with p_R' the identity on p_R, p_L' on p_L and p_4 on one line of F on the antiparticles: Q_{R L} counts p_R against pbar_L, which is empty, and Q_{R 4}, Q_{L 4} count one line of p_R and of p_L. For Q_B, on the light block p_1 is the identity on the nine light particles and on the three light antileptons, p_3 on one colour of dbar_L, dbar_R, and p_3° on one colour of d_L, d_R; Q_13 counts d_L and d_R of one colour, of opposite gradings, so it vanishes, and Q_11 vanishes by Lemma 41.1. The kernels are read off. ∎

**Remark 41.3 (the form is the bimodule index).** Q_ij is the graded dimension, that is the index, of the (p_i, p_j)-isotypic component of the bimodule. It vanishes identically exactly when every isotypic component has equal numbers of states of the two gradings, which is the vectorlike condition. On the light block of the third step this is the finite-triple form of the statement that the restriction of the chiral representation [V_L] - [V_R] to SU(3) x U(1)_em is zero (lemma 09, Corollary 9.3; the first-stage note of the corpus, section 7): the equivariant index of the Standard Model is nonzero and is read as zero by the unbroken group. At the first two steps the form has rank 2 because the quark doublets and the leptons carry the chiral asymmetry; the kernel vector p_1 + p_2 - p_3 of Q_SM is the direction along which the chiral counts of the three summands cancel.

## Orientability

**Proposition 41.4.** (i) The Pati-Salam data are orientable: gamma = rho(p_L' - p_R') rho(1_4)° - rho(1_4) rho(p_L' - p_R')°. (ii) The Standard Model data with a right-handed neutrino are not: nu_R and J nu_R carry the same left character lambda and the same right character lambda and have opposite gradings, so no sum of left-right products separates them (Stephan 2006, hep-th/0610097, for the KO-6 models with neutrinos). (iii) The light block of the third step is not: the kept mass blocks are nonzero and commute with B (lemma 17, Corollary 17.4), and a nonzero D commuting with both actions commutes with every orientation cycle while anticommuting with gamma; directly, d_L and d_R of one colour carry the same characters (conj lambda on the left, e_11 on the right) and opposite gradings.

*Proof.* (i) On the particles rho(1_4)° is the identity and rho(1_4) vanishes, so the right side is p_L' - p_R', which is +1 on p_L and -1 on p_R; on the antiparticles rho(p_L' - p_R') vanishes and rho(1_4) is the identity, so the right side is -(p_L' - p_R')°, which is -1 on pbar_L and +1 on pbar_R. This is gamma. (ii) and (iii): a cycle c = sum rho(a_i) rho(b_i)° takes the same value on two states with the same pair of characters, while gamma takes opposite values. For the first sentence of (iii), if [D, rho(a)] = 0 = [D, rho(b)°] for all a, b then [D, c] = 0 for every cycle, and c = gamma would give D gamma = gamma D, which with D gamma = -gamma D forces D = 0; this is the corpus statement rs_N9 (historical/corpus/connes_kk_research_state.md, section 5.4), proved here. ∎

The explicit model of results/orientability_under_heavy_centralizer.md shows the same loss under a heavy centralizer in a four-summand toy; here it happens in the physical chain, at the first heavy step when nu_R is present and at the second step in every case.

## Unimodularity

**Proposition 41.5.** Let X_lambda be the generator of the abelian direction of C, acting as i on lambda (so as diag(i, -i) (x) I_4 on p_R and as i on the lepton line of the antiparticles), and X_c the common colour phase, i on the three colours of the antiparticles. The traces of the left action are

    full H (32 states): Tr X_lambda = 4i, Tr X_c = 12i;
    after the removal of the up-type pair, with nu_R kept (26 states): i, 6i;
    on the light block of the third step (18 states): -i, 6i.

So the unimodularity condition Tr A = 0 of Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli, imposed on the parent, gives the relation 4 theta_lambda + 12 theta_c = 0 between the two abelian phases, while the same condition reimposed on the light block gives -theta_lambda + 6 theta_c = 0: a different embedding of the surviving abelian generator. The inherited lift is the one consistent with Proposition 22.8 of lemma 22, where the ten generators of u(C_F) reduce to the nine of the photon and the gluons by the condition inherited from the parent.

*Proof.* Count the states with their phases: on the full space X_lambda has trace 0 on p_R (i and -i in equal numbers) and 4i on the antilepton line (two weak components in two sectors); X_c has 12i on the antiquarks. Removing the up-type pair removes u_R (three colours, phase -i) from p_R, leaving nu_R (i), d_R (three colours, -i) and e_R (-i), total -3i, and the antilepton line stays, so i in all; the antiquark count halves to 6i. Removing also nu_R and its conjugate takes i from p_R and i from the antilepton line: -i and 6i. ∎

## Scope

One generation with Dirac neutrinos in the stated representation; the trace form on minimal projections as the test of Poincaré duality (a real Kasparov duality is a different question, as the corpus noted); the strict projections of lemma 06 for the third step. With three generations the matrices multiply by three and nothing else changes. The direction the result points to: the finite triple of the main paper carries, along the chain, exactly the data that lemma 22 derives from the interval (signs, order zero, first order), and none that it does not; the two failed axioms are the ones a Kaluza-Klein parent would have to supply through its topology (orientability, Poincaré duality for the parent manifold), and their failure at the vectorlike end is the index statement in disguise.
