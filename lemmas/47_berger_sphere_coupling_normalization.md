# The Killing-form normalization on the Berger five-sphere and Connes' matching value

For STRATEGY T18 (the normalization test of the object at 9) and the parked job 04 (lemmas/README, the two Berger formulas), following [lemma 03](03_mass_weighted_kinetic_terms.md) (cos^2 theta_W = 5/8 at matching with the full module light; in its heavy-up family the photon and gluon coefficients keep their ratio) and the lead recorded from GitHub issue 1 of the repository (the round S^5 gives e^2/g_s^2 = 3/8, computed there by Schur's lemma; read by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026). Written by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026, by hand; the two sphere moments, the formula at eta = 1, 2, 1/2 and the charges of the coordinates in the exterior square of the spinor were checked with numpy (Monte Carlo with 2 x 10^6 points for the moments; script not kept). Refereed by Claude Opus 5.5 (effort max, 5 November 2026; report in historical/reviews/2026-11-05_opus_check_47_48.md), who redid the moments, the Berger form at four values of eta, the branchings and the traces: Proposition 47.1 correct; Corollary 47.2 and Remark 47.3 correct with amendments, applied below (the citation of the coupling formula to Weinberg 1983; the proof that the isometry group enlarges only at the round point; the reading of lemma 03; the scope of the eta-curve as a statement about the Einstein term alone, the round-point value being independent of any SO(6)-invariant source by Schur's lemma); the round-point value is implicit in print in holographic form, the charge unit being the superconformal R-charge (Barnes, Gorbatov, Intriligator and Wright 2005).

**Outcome.** On the Berger five-sphere, the round S^5 in C^3 with the Hopf fibre scaled by eta, the Kaluza-Klein kinetic form of the Killing vectors gives, for the SU(3) acting on C^3 and the central U(1) with charge c on the coordinates,

    e^2 / g_s^2 = (3 + eta^2) / (24 eta^2 c^2)    (Corollary 47.2).

The two formulas of the parked job 04, 3(eta^2 + 3)/(8 eta^2) and 3(eta^2 + 3)/(32 eta^2), are the two charge units c = 1/3 and c = 2/3 on the coordinates; the second is the unit fixed by the spinor of SO(6) = SU(4) at the round point, where the SU(3)-singlet Killing spinor has charge 1 and the triplet -1/3, so that the coordinates, in the exterior square of the spinor, carry 2/3. With that unit the round point eta = 1 gives e^2/g_s^2 = 3/8, the value of Connes' finite triple at matching (sin^2 theta_W = 3/8 with g_2 = g_3, the 5/8 of lemma 03 being cos^2 theta_W). So the normalization test of T18 distinguishes the finite triple from the squashed spheres, and the round sphere, the one point of the Berger family where the isometry enlarges from U(3) to SO(6), gives the finite triple's value; at that point the value is independent of whatever holds the shape, since every SO(6)-invariant contribution to the gauge kinetic terms is proportional to the trace form by Schur's lemma. In the Einstein-term reduction the ratio decreases from infinity at eta -> 0 through 3/8 at eta = 1 to 3/32 at eta -> infinity, taking each value once; off the round point the curve depends on the source that supports the non-Einstein shape.

## Setting

Let S^5 be the unit sphere in C^3, with the Hopf fibration S^5 -> CP^2 whose fibre at z is the circle e^{i alpha} z, with unit vertical vector i z. The Berger metric g_eta is the round metric on the horizontal space and eta^2 times the round metric on the vertical direction; its volume is V_eta = eta V_1. The group U(3) acts isometrically on (S^5, g_eta) for every eta > 0, through SU(3) and the central circle z -> e^{i alpha} z, since U(3) preserves the round metric and the field i z. At eta = 1 the isometry group is O(6), with identity component SO(6), whose Lie algebra so(6) = su(4) contains u(3); for eta != 1 it is U(3) x| Z_2, the Z_2 generated by complex conjugation, with identity component U(3): by O'Neill's formulas for the canonical variation of the Hopf fibration over CP^2 (Einstein constant 6), the Ricci tensor of g_eta has the eigenvalue 4 eta^2 on the unit vertical vector and 6 - 2 eta^2 on unit horizontal vectors, which differ unless eta = 1, so an isometry preserves the vertical line field, hence the unit vertical field up to sign, hence the round metric g_1 = g_eta + (eta^{-2} - 1) theta_eta (x) theta_eta with theta_eta the g_eta-dual of the unit vertical field; it then lies in O(6) and maps i z to +- i z, so it is complex linear or antilinear (referee). The same eigenvalues show that g_eta is Einstein only at eta = 1. For a Hermitian traceless T on C^3 the Killing vector of the one-parameter group e^{i t T} is K_T(z) = i T z, and for the central circle with charge c on the coordinates, z -> e^{i c alpha} z, it is K_c(z) = i c z.

The tree-level Kaluza-Klein couplings: the reduction of the Einstein-Hilbert term on a compact internal space K with Killing vectors K_a gives the gauge kinetic term with the matrix

    <K_a, K_b> = int_K g(K_a, K_b) dvol,

so that for an orthogonal basis g_a^{-2} is proportional to <K_a, K_a>, with one overall constant for all generators (the ansatz of Witten, Nucl. Phys. B 186 (1981) 412, equation (3); the integral formula of Weinberg, Phys. Lett. B 125 (1983) 265, in the form of Barnes, Gorbatov, Intriligator and Wright, Nucl. Phys. B 732 (2006) 89, section 3, where the normalization point is stated: the Killing vectors normalized to canonical kinetic terms make the coupling the charge unit; the normalization of each generator on the fields is that charge unit, an input). Within this scope, the Einstein term as the only source of the gauge kinetic terms, the charge unit is the only input; a flux or another source supporting the background contributes further terms (Duff, Pope and Warner, Phys. Lett. B 130 (1983) 254: on an Einstein space the Freund-Rubin contribution is a multiple of the Einstein one through int g(nabla K, nabla K') = int Ric(K, K'), leaving every ratio unchanged). The colour generators are normalized by tr T^2 = 1/2 on C^3.

## Statements

**Proposition 47.1 (the form on the Berger sphere).** For Hermitian traceless T, T' on C^3 and the central generator with charge c,

    <K_T, K_T'>_eta = V_eta (3 + eta^2) tr(T T') / 12,    <K_c, K_c>_eta = V_eta eta^2 c^2,    <K_T, K_c>_eta = 0.

In particular, for tr T^2 = 1/2, <K_T, K_T>_eta = V_eta (3 + eta^2)/24.

*Proof.* At z, the vertical component of K_T is the real inner product of i T z with the unit vertical vector i z, which is <z, T z> (real, since T is Hermitian), and the horizontal part has squared norm |T z|^2 - <z, T z>^2; so g_eta(K_T, K_T) = |T z|^2 + (eta^2 - 1) <z, T z>^2, and g_eta(K_T, K_T') = Re <T z, T' z> + (eta^2 - 1) <z, T z> <z, T' z>. The moments of the uniform measure on the unit sphere of C^n are int |z_i|^2 = 1/n and int z_i conj(z_j) z_k conj(z_l) = (delta_ij delta_kl + delta_il delta_kj)/(n(n + 1)), so for n = 3

    int <T z, T' z> = tr(T T')/3,    int <z, T z> <z, T' z> = (tr T tr T' + tr(T T'))/12 = tr(T T')/12,

the last for traceless T, T'. Hence <K_T, K_T'>_eta = V_eta [tr(T T')/3 + (eta^2 - 1) tr(T T')/12] = V_eta (3 + eta^2) tr(T T')/12. The central Killing vector is vertical with g_eta(K_c, K_c) = eta^2 c^2 |z|^2 = eta^2 c^2, and <K_T, K_c>_eta = c eta^2 int <z, T z> = c eta^2 tr(T)/3 = 0. ∎

**Corollary 47.2 (the coupling ratio).** With g_s the coupling of the SU(3) generators normalized by tr T^2 = 1/2 and e the coupling of the central circle with charge c on the coordinates,

    e^2 / g_s^2 = <K_T, K_T>_eta / <K_c, K_c>_eta = (3 + eta^2) / (24 eta^2 c^2).

1. c = 1/3, the unit in which the coordinates carry charge 1/3 and the colour-singlet cubic z_1 z_2 z_3 carries charge 1 (the singlet Killing spinor then carries 1/2), gives 3(eta^2 + 3)/(8 eta^2); c = 2/3 gives 3(eta^2 + 3)/(32 eta^2): the two formulas of the parked job 04, which belong to two charge units of one computation. In general the spinor triplet carries -c/2 and the singlet 3c/2.
2. At the round point the Killing spinors form the 4 of SU(4) = Spin(6), which restricts to U(3) as 3_q + 1_{-3q}; with the singlet at charge 1 the triplet has charge -1/3 (the charges of the down quark and the positron, up to the overall sign), Q = diag(-1/3, -1/3, -1/3, 1), and the coordinates of C^3, which span one of the two triplets of the vector 6 = Lambda^2(4) = 3_{2/3} + conj(3)_{-2/3} (which one depends on the orientation of the complex structure; c^2 does not), carry c = 2/3, the charge of the up quark. With this unit, e^2/g_s^2 = 3(eta^2 + 3)/(32 eta^2), equal to 3/8 at eta = 1.
3. At eta = 1 the same value follows from Schur's lemma alone: the form is ad-invariant under so(6) = su(4), which is simple, so it is proportional to the trace form in the 4, and tr Q^2 = 3/9 + 1 = 4/3 against tr T^2 = 1/2 gives e^2/g_s^2 = (1/2)/(4/3) = 3/8.
4. In the Einstein-term reduction, the function 3(eta^2 + 3)/(32 eta^2) = 3/32 + 9/(32 eta^2) decreases from infinity at eta -> 0 to 3/32 at eta -> infinity, so each value above 3/32 is taken at exactly one shape, and 3/8 at the round point only. Off the round point the metric is not Einstein, so the shape needs a supporting source whose contribution to the kinetic terms changes the curve: with a term lambda int Ric_eta(K, K) added, int Ric_eta(K_T, K_T) = V tr T^2 [(6 - 2 eta^2)/4 + eta^4/3] and int Ric_eta(K_c, K_c) = 4 V eta^4 c^2, the ratio stays 3/8 at eta = 1 for every lambda and falls below 3/32 at large eta for lambda = 2 (referee).

*Proof.* Items 1 and 2 are substitutions in Proposition 47.1 and the branching of the 4 and of Lambda^2(4) under U(3) in SU(4). Item 3: a U(3)-invariant symmetric form on so(6) that is also invariant under the rest of SO(6), which holds at eta = 1 since SO(6) acts isometrically, is ad(so(6))-invariant, and so(6) is simple. Item 4: the derivative of (eta^2 + 3)/eta^2 = 1 + 3/eta^2 is negative. ∎

**Remark 47.3 (the dictionary).** Connes' finite triple fixes at matching g_3 = g_2 and sin^2 theta_W = 3/8 (lemma 03: cos^2 theta_W = 5/8 at matching with the full module light; in its heavy-up family the neutral-current angle stays 5/8 and the photon and gluon coefficients keep their ratio, so e^2/g_3^2 = 3/8 holds there too, while the charged-W/hypercharge matching ratio moves), so e^2 = (3/8) g_3^2 there; directly from the traces of lemma 03, Part B, per generation the colour trace is 2 and tr Q^2 = 8/3 + 2/3 + 2 = 16/3, so e^2/g_3^2 = 2/(16/3) = 3/8, which is also the SU(5) value, the lemma's Q being the electric charge on the first four entries of the 5 = (d, d, d, e^+, conj(nu)). The round S^5 gives the same ratio of the colour and electromagnetic couplings, at tree level, with the charge unit of its own Killing spinors. So the test proposed in T18, whether the object at 9 fixes the gauge normalization, distinguishes the finite triple from the squashed spheres, and the round sphere gives the finite triple's value; the round point is the point of the Berger family where the isometry algebra enlarges from su(3) + u(1) to su(4), an enhanced-symmetry point in the language of candidate 5 of T18, the matching value sits there, and there it is independent of the source holding the shape. In the Einstein-term reduction the values along the family lie in (3/32, infinity), and the direction of squashing that lowers the ratio is eta > 1, the fibre stretched. The value 3/8 depends only on the embedding u(3) in su(4) with the 4 = 3_{-1/3} + 1_1; the same embedding carries the B - L reading of Pati-Salam, where the 4 is 3_{1/3} + 1_{-1} and Q is -(B - L), and the superconformal R-symmetry of AdS_5 x S^5. At any enhanced point where su(3) + u(1) sits in a simple algebra g the ratio is tr_V T^2 / tr_V Q^2 for any representation V of g, so the matching value appears exactly when the embedding gives Q the SU(5) index, tr Q^2 = (8/3) tr T^2 (referee).

*Prior work (found by the referee).* The formula on the Berger sphere was not found in print (searches on squashed five-spheres and on the weak angle from Kaluza-Klein theory before 1992). The round-point value is implicit in holographic form: Barnes, Gorbatov, Intriligator and Wright (Nucl. Phys. B 732 (2006) 89, arXiv:hep-th/0507146, sections 3 and 6) give for IIB on S^5 the coefficients tau_SO(6) = N^2 and tau_RR = 4N^2/3, with the U(1)_R Killing vector of norm squared 4/9, which is the central generator with c = 2/3 (the holomorphic 3-form at charge 2); with tau_SU(3) = N^2/2 this gives tau_SU(3)/tau_RR = 3/8, and a field-theory check gives the same value from N = 4 super Yang-Mills. So the charge unit of Corollary 47.2 is the superconformal R-charge. Related: Ezawa, Koh and Rajpoot, Phys. Lett. B 147 (1984) 284, Pati-Salam unification from an SU(4) x SU(2) isometry (the B - L reading); Hu and Shen, Phys. Lett. B 178 (1986) 373, the weak angle from a deformed three-sphere, the S^3 analogue of this family. The identifiers are in paper/LITERATURE.md, section F.

## Scope

Tree-level Kaluza-Klein reduction of the Einstein-Hilbert term, with no other contribution to the gauge kinetic terms and no threshold corrections; the Berger family of U(3)-invariant metrics on S^5; the charge unit as an input, fixed here by the SU(4) spinor at the round point and continued along the family. The identification of the SU(3) acting on C^3 with colour and of the central circle with electromagnetism is the reading of the 9 end of T18 (a five-dimensional internal space with isometry su(3) + u(1)); the same embedding carries the B - L and the R-symmetry readings, and the value fixes the index of the embedding and leaves the reading open. Off the round point the Berger metric is not Einstein, so the shape needs a supporting source, whose contribution to the gauge kinetic terms is outside the statement; the value 3/8 at eta = 1 is unaffected by any SO(6)-invariant contribution, by Schur's lemma. The matter content, the running from the matching scale and the physical value of e^2/g_s^2 are outside the statement. The direction the result points to: the round point as the matching point of the dictionary, where both sides agree and the value is source-independent, and the squashing eta as the parameter that the finite triple does not have; for the other geometric families of T18 the question reduces to an index computation, whether the embedding of su(3) + u(1) at the enhanced point gives Q the SU(5) index, and the methods of Barnes, Gorbatov, Intriligator and Wright compute such ratios on other Sasaki-Einstein five-manifolds with flavour symmetry (referee).
