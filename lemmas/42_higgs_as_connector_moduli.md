# The Higgs representation in the window: the tangent of the orbit of the marked lines

For STRATEGY T14, item (ii) of the corpus reading (CORPUS_SALVAGE item 13), following [lemma 28](28_two_walls_connected_parent.md) (the group data G = Sp(2), K = Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b, H = Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b, and Proposition 28.19), [lemma 29](29_window_on_the_disc_bundle.md) (the eight-state window) and [lemma 20](20_connecting_operator.md) (Theorem 20.2 and item 2 of its readings: the connector as a marked pair of points and a coupling). Written by Claude Fable 5.1 on 4 November 2026, by hand; the representation theory is standard and is checked against the isotropy representation stated in lemma 28. Referee pending.

**Outcome.** The window operator of lemma 29 carries no Higgs representation by itself: Sp(2)-equivariance makes its off-diagonal part a scalar (Proposition 28.19). The Higgs representation of the first-stage corpus note (section 6: T_p HP^1 = Hom_H(L, L^perp), the (2, 2) of Spin(4), one doublet of hypercharge 1/2 after marking a U(1)) is the tangent space of the orbit of the marked line that the connector of lemma 20 puts on the quaternionic sheet. That orbit is CP^3, and its tangent at the marked line is the isotropy representation (2)_{+-1} + (1)_{+-2} of lemma 28: a doublet of the weak Sp(1)_a with U(1)_b charge +-1, which is the Higgs doublet, and a singlet of charge +-2 along the twistor fibre, which is an SU(2)-singlet scalar direction of hypercharge 1. The moduli of the connector under Sp(2) are two real numbers, the Hermitian and the symplectic overlaps of the two marked lines.

## Setting

The window of lemma 29 is W + V, two copies of the 4 of G = Sp(2): the core copy W, on which the real functions of S^4 compress to M_2(H), and the wall copy V, on which the real functions of CP^3 compress to M_4(C) (Example 16.9, Theorem 28.21). The connector of Theorem 20.2 is the rank-one block T_kappa x = kappa K x K with K = v w*, that is, a complex line Cw in W, a complex line Cv in V and the coupling kappa; the centralizer of T_kappa is C + H + M_3(C) (lemma 20, Result 2). In the notation of lemma 28, K = Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b is the stabilizer of a quaternionic line L in W = H^2, with Sp(1)_b acting on L and Sp(1)_a on L^perp, and H = Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b is the stabilizer of a complex line l in L, so that G/K = S^4, G/H = CP^3 and K/H = S^2 is the twistor fibre. Charges of U(1)_b are normalized so that the doublet of Sp(1)_b has charges +-1. In the flag algebra of the corpus note (section 5), marking l reduces H_R = Sp(1)_b to C and leaves H_L = Sp(1)_a, the weak isospin, on L^perp.

## Statements

**Proposition 42.1 (no Higgs representation in the equivariant window).** If the window consists of one copy of the 4 in each degree of the end grading, then after equivariant identifications d_M = [[0, conj(lambda_M) I_4], [lambda_M I_4, 0]], and a phase makes lambda_M >= 0 (Proposition 28.19, by Schur's lemma). So the off-diagonal part of the window operator is a multiple of the identity, invariant under G, and it transforms under the isotropy group of any marked point trivially. The question of item (ii) of CORPUS_SALVAGE, whether that part transforms as T_p S^4, has the answer no, for this reason.

**Proposition 42.2 (the tangent of the marked line).** Let w be a unit vector of W = H^2 = C^4, l = Cw its complex line, L = l + jl its quaternionic line, and p the point of S^4 = HP^1 under L. The G-orbit of l is CP^3 = G/H and the G-orbit of L is S^4 = G/K. The tangent spaces at l and L decompose under the stabilizers as

    T_L S^4 = (2, 2) of Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b = Hom_H(L, L^perp),
    T_l CP^3 = (2)_{+-1} + (1)_{+-2} under H = Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b,

where (2)_{+-1} is the restriction of T_L S^4 (the horizontal part, the doublet of the weak Sp(1)_a with U(1)_b charge +-1, one complex doublet of hypercharge 1/2 in the normalization Y = charge/2) and (1)_{+-2} is the tangent of the twistor fibre K/H = S^2 at l (one complex singlet of hypercharge 1, electric charge +1).

*Proof.* sp(2) = sp(1)_a + sp(1)_b + m with m = (2, 2) of real dimension 4, the isotropy representation of S^4; this is the decomposition of so(5) under so(4). Under H, sp(1)_b = u(1)_b + (1)_{+-2}, since the adjoint of Sp(1) restricted to its maximal torus has charges 0, +-2, and (2, 2) restricted to Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b is (2)_{+1} + (2)_{-1}, since the 2 of Sp(1)_b has charges +-1. The isotropy representation of G/H is sp(2)/h = (1)_{+-2} + (2)_{+-1}, as stated in lemma 28, with (1)_{+-2} tangent to the fibre K/H and (2)_{+-1} the lift of the tangent of G/K. The identification T_L HP^1 = Hom_H(L, L^perp) with the action t -> q_a t q_b^{-1} is the corpus note, section 6. ∎

**Proposition 42.3 (the moduli of the connector).** Let w in W and v in V be the two marked unit vectors, with G acting on both copies of the 4 by the same representation, and let h and omega be the G-invariant Hermitian and symplectic forms on C^4 (omega(x, y) = h(jx, y)). The G-orbits of the pair of lines (Cw, Cv) are classified by the two real invariants

    a = |h(w, v)|,   b = |omega(w, v)|,   a^2 + b^2 <= 1,

and the coupling kappa adds its modulus |kappa|. In particular the orbit of the pair is determined by the position of Cv relative to the quaternionic line L of w: a^2 + b^2 is the squared norm of the component of v in L, a the overlap with l and b the overlap with jl.

*Proof.* G acts transitively on the unit sphere of H^2 with stabilizer Sp(1)_a (the stabilizer of w and jw), so the orbit of the pair is the Sp(1)_a-orbit of v. Write v = alpha w + beta jw + u with u in L^perp = H; Sp(1)_a acts on u by the quaternionic scalars, transitively on each sphere of L^perp, and fixes alpha = h(w, v), beta = h(jw, v) = conj(omega(w, v)) up to the convention. Rephasing w by e^{i phi} and v by e^{i theta} sends (alpha, beta) to (e^{i(theta - phi)} alpha, e^{i(theta + phi)} beta), which removes both phases. So the invariants of the pair of lines are |alpha|, |beta|, and |u|^2 = 1 - |alpha|^2 - |beta|^2 is determined. The coupling kappa is unchanged by G and rephased with the lines (lemma 20, item 2). ∎

## Reading

The Higgs doublet of the first-stage note is recovered in the window as the tangent of the orbit of the marked core line, projected to S^4: it is the representation in which the point p of S^4 under the connector moves. The connector of lemma 20 is an input of the window (Theorem 28.21 prescribes the windows; the connector is not derived), so the proposition says where the doublet sits once the connector is a field: in the variation of the marked lines, which is a variation of the finite data, and in the two moduli a, b with the coupling, and never in the equivariant window operator d_M. Beside the doublet, the twistor fibre contributes a charged singlet direction of hypercharge 1; whether this direction is a dynamical scalar, a gauge direction of the U(1)_b, or frozen by the collar connection of lemma 29 (which preserves the line summands, hence the fibre structure) is open, and it is the same question as the grading axis of Proposition 28.19, which marks the quaternionic line L. The dictionary note keeps one row from this: the Higgs doublet is the tangent of the base S^4 at the point marked by the connector, with the twistor fibre as a charged singlet beside it.

## Scope

The window of lemma 29 with its Sp(2)-equivariance; the connector of Theorem 20.2 as a marked pair of lines and a coupling; the normalization Y = charge/2 of the corpus note. Nothing here makes the marked lines dynamical, computes a potential, or fixes the coupling; Proposition 42.3 counts moduli and does not identify them with a vacuum expectation value. The direction it points to: a parent in which the connector's lines vary along S^4, that is, a Higgs field as a section over the base, for which the collar connection of lemma 29 and the engineered connections of T15 are the available tools.
