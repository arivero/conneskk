Part 1 lists the developments by topic, with links to every place that treats them. Part 2 gives an anchored table of contents of each document. Abbreviations in link labels: paper = `historical/corpus/canonical_research.md`; note, solidification, nine-dim = the three derivation appendices in `research_limits/`; notes = `historical/corpus/connes_kk_research_state.md`; checks = `historical/corpus/research_checks_knowledge.md`; strategy = `STRATEGY.md`.

The paper states results with their labels; the appendices hold the derivations it omits; the notes carry the lens-lattice, Fermi-scale and KK-theory line; the lemmas hold the new results (proved by Astra 6 or Claude). The transcripts are the primary records of the chat sessions. All figures, scripts and JSON records were turned into text (see ). The finite checks verify specified constructions; they are not numerical evidence for a Standard Model vacuum, and no complete 9/10/11 compactification is claimed.
