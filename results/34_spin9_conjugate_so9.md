# The so(9) fixed by the Chevalley involution on sl(9) is conjugate in E8 to the Spin(9) of the four-family count, with k = so(9) + 84 and p = 44 + 84, the 44 inside the gravity sector

**Status.** Proposition (Reading 3b, part (c), of lemma 34: a proved statement inside a reading). Stated and checked by Claude Sonnet 5.5 by hand on 4 November 2026; a single-model statement, with no independent check by another model, and the ledger marks the row referee_pending. Ledger row `d11_spin9_conjugate`. Stated after Proposition 12 of paper/generations_note.tex as a single-model statement pending a referee.

**Setting.** The Z_3 grading of e8 by SU(9) of 33_e8_two_gradings_placement.md, on which the Chevalley involution restricted to sl(9) is an outer involution with fixed algebra so(9); and the Spin(9) in SO(16) in E8, embedded through its spinor with 128 -> 44 + 84 (34_nogo_colour_not_in_spin9.md).

**Statement.**

1. The so(9) of the SU(9) grading is conjugate in E8 to the Spin(9) of the four-family count, with fixed algebra k = so(9) + 84 and complement p = 44 + 84, the 44 inside the gravity sector.
2. The involutions of e8 with a 120-dimensional fixed algebra form one conjugacy class, and the spinor embeddings with 128 -> 44 + 84 form one SO(16)-class.

*Scope.* The record holds two placements of the top from different subgroups of E8: in the C_3 sector of the SU(9) grading, and with the fourth family in the 44 of Spin(9). The sectors are different objects, so the two placements do not contradict each other, and no identification between them is claimed.

**Proof.** Lemma 34, Reading 3b (c), states the result; the argument recorded with the ledger row is item 2, applied to the involution whose fixed algebra so(9) + 84 has dimension 36 + 84 = 120. The lemma does not write out a longer proof.

**What it leaves open.** An independent check by a model other than its author. How the 96 states of the three-family frame sit against the conjugated Spin(9) has not been worked out (lemma 34, Reading 3b (c), and item (i) of the open items of paper/generations_note.tex).
