## Reducing the dual seven-form

**Proposition 84.1.** Let the metric be locally an unwarped product of a seven-dimensional spacetime and a closed oriented four-manifold $X$ of volume $V_X$. Write $h$ for a harmonic representative of an integral two-class on $X$ and put $J=\int_X h\wedge *h>0$, $I=\int_X h^2$. Use the source-free two-derivative supergravity equations, one mixed component of the four-form, and no background four-form wholly inside $X$. In the brane units below the induced Bianchi and kinetic coefficients are
$$
 p_M=\frac{m^2 I}{2},\qquad
 Z_A=\frac{m^2J}{T_2^2 V_X},\qquad
 Z_B=\frac{1}{T_5^2 V_X^2},\qquad
 \frac{|p_M|\sqrt{Z_B}}{\pi Z_A}=\frac{|I|}{J}\le1 .
$$
Here $m$ is the charge of a wrapped primitive membrane on a two-cycle with unit $h$-period, in units of the proposed vortex connection. The coefficients $Z_A,Z_B$ multiply minus one half of the squared form norms *relative to the reduced Einstein prefactor* $CV_X$. The equality in the bound occurs exactly when the harmonic form is self-dual or anti-self-dual.

**Proof.** Use $T_2=(2\pi)^{-2}\ell_p^{-3}$, $T_5=(2\pi)^{-5}\ell_p^{-6}$, and normalize $G=T_2 G_4^{\rm phys}$ with membrane periods $2\pi$. In the classical equations $G_7=T_5 *_{11}G_4^{\rm phys}$ obeys $dG_7=-G\wedge G/(4\pi)$ up to simultaneous orientation conventions. Write $G=T_2 F_4^{(7)}+m F_A\wedge h$. Integrate $G_7$ over $X$. The resulting three-form satisfies $dH_3/(2\pi)=-(m^2 I/2)(F_A/(2\pi))^2$. In particular a weak monopole of unit $F_A$ flux gives the four-dimensional axion charge $m^2 I$. The Maxwell coefficient is obtained by integrating the mixed four-form norm. The wholly seven-dimensional $F_4^{(7)}$ has coefficient $CV_X$. Since $H_3=T_5 V_X *_7 F_4^{(7)}$ in this product frame, first-order dualization gives kinetic coefficient $C/(T_5^2V_X)$ for $H_3$. These are two different components of the original field. Finally $T_2^2=2\pi T_5$, and $\int h^2=\|h^+\|^2-\|h^-\|^2$ whereas $J=\|h^+\|^2+\|h^-\|^2$. $\square$ [Supergravity action](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(78)90894-8) · [Brane tensions and charge quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711014)

## Checking the bosonic product solution

**Proof.** The squared four-form norm is $2g^2$. Its contraction has squared norm $g^2$ on a unit projective tangent, $2g^2$ on a unit weak tangent, and zero on the external directions. The trace-reversed Ricci tensor is $\langle\iota_M G,\iota_N G\rangle/2-|G|^2g_{MN}/6$ in physical supergravity units. The projective and weak Ricci coefficients are therefore $g^2/6=6/B^2$ and $2g^2/3=1/r^2$. The flux is closed, co-closed and has vanishing square on this background. These scalar curvatures reproduce the mixed-only branch of Dolan's product calculation. A maximally symmetric five-dimensional choice is anti-de Sitter. ## The two vector residues on the spin lift

**Corollary 84.4 (a circle-momentum current).** Take the electric Freund–Rubin solution on $M^{111}$ and its local metric and flux on $M^{111}/\mathbb Z_j$. $$
 m_{\rm U}^2=\frac{48}{L^2},\qquad
 g_0^2 M_4^2=\frac{1}{2R_{11}^2},\qquad
 \frac{M_1^2}{g_0^2 M_4^2}=2+\frac{3}{j}.
$$
The scalar here is a minimally coupled probe of the internal Laplacian. Its charge-one multiplicity on the primitive bundle is 30. Its eigenvalue is a separate measurement from either universal vector mass.

**Proof.** For the Sasaki–Einstein contact form $d\eta=2J$, the universal bosonic truncation of Gauntlett, Kim, Varela and Waldram contains the Reeb metric connection $A$ and the dual $\widetilde B$ of the vector multiplying $J$ in the three-form. ## A colour instanton tests the membrane charge

**Proposition 84.5.** At the weak-restored member, assume ordinary and mirror quarks have the lifts of 72 and suppose $N$ is the number of ordinary families minus mirror families in the light theory. Assume the entire missing mixed colour–vortex anomaly is carried by periodic axions $\theta_i$ of integer Stückelberg charges $q_i$, with single-valued local Wess–Zumino terms. Then colour instanton matching is possible precisely when $\gcd_i(|q_i|)$ divides $2N$. For one axion from the single-class product of 84.1 the condition is $|m^2I|\mid 2N$ for $mI\ne0$. It tests only the colour component of the anomaly. The higher-dimensional spin and flux conditions and the other anomaly coefficients are additional requirements.

**Proof.** On a spin four-sphere put a unit $SU(3)$ instanton and take the weak, hypercharge and vortex bundles trivial. This background is allowed for $S(U(3)\times U(2))$. The index of each colour fundamental Weyl field has magnitude one, independent of its three colour components. An ordinary weak doublet has two such zero modes of vortex charge two. The right-handed up and down quarks are colour fundamentals and weak singlets. Their left-handed conjugates each contribute vortex charge minus one to the chiral trace. Thus the instanton vertex carries $2\cdot2-1-1=2$ units per ordinary family and the opposite value per mirror. This agrees with the coefficient $-6c_2^c v$ for three families in 80.1, up to instanton orientation. A periodic $\theta_i$ can multiply the unit instanton number only with integral coefficient $n_i$ in the stated local action. The needed variation is $\sum_i n_i q_i=2N$ up to the same sign. Bézout's identity gives necessity and sufficiency in this channel. For the mixed magnetic reduction the unit weak flux gave $q=m^2I$. $\square$ [Three-family anomaly and integer descent](80_axion_and_a_confining_mirror_sector.md) · [Discrete instanton test of Banks and Dine](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9109045)

Breaking by charged root expectation values at a different member also changes its residual symmetry. None of these options follows just from fitting a neutral pole.
