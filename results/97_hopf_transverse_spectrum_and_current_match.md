# Transverse weak currents on the supported spin Hopf product

Keep the volume-line lift, circle period six, fermion weights and magnetic five-form of 94. Use its minimal horizontal covariant derivatives and the quotient two-period tensor of 92. We vary the weak shape and radii within that action and the auxiliary Maxwell norm through its constant modulus. The observables are the transverse charged and neutral poles, the exact photon and its residue, and the left-lepton contact on the finite neck and subsequent shrink. This calculation concerns the sourced colour-singlet spin-one sectors. The coloured scalar stability problem is separate.

## The Hopf metric and its five-form profile

**Proposition 97.1.** In electric-charge sectors $Q=0,\pm1$, the sourced colour-singlet transverse problem on the spin Hopf background reduces to the weak radial forms of 92 coupled to three Abelian profiles. In charge-connection units they are $p$ for the Hopf metric, $c$ for the auxiliary Maxwell field and $b_F$ for one coexact five-form-potential profile. The additional positive forms are
$$
 \begin{aligned}
 K_{\rm ab}&=I_E\langle p^2+b_F^2\rangle+I_C\langle c^2\rangle,\\
 H_{\rm ab}&=I_E\left\langle|\nabla p|^2+|\nabla b_F|^2
                      +\lambda_F(b_F-p)^2\right\rangle\\
 &\quad+ I_C\left\langle|\nabla c|^2\right\rangle
         + f_S^2\langle(3p-2c)^2\rangle,
 \qquad I_E=CVB^2=3I_3,\quad \lambda_F=8/B^2 .
 \end{aligned}
$$
The gradient is for the physical weak metric. In the root derivative replace the product-circle term by $\pm 3p$. For a spinor of Hopf and auxiliary weights $(m,q_C)$ its new source term is $mp+q_Cc$. The exact photon has $p=b_F=1/6$ and $c=1/4$ in the normalization of the unit weak rotation.

**Proof.** For the round colour metric let $\eta$ be the unit Hopf one-form and $J$ its orthonormal horizontal Kähler form, so $d\eta=2J/B$. The invariant flux is $F_5=f_5\eta\wedge J^2/2$. For the unit Hopf generator, $\iota_K F_5=d\beta_K$ with $\beta_K=(f_5B^2/4)\eta\wedge J$. It is co-closed, has squared eigenvalue $8/B^2$, and $Z\|\beta_K\|^2$ per unit colour volume is $B^2$ by the background equation of 94. In a transverse fluctuation the horizontal one-leg component of the flux contains $d_5\beta_K(b_F-p)$. The weak derivative of $b_F\beta_K$ supplies the independent sphere gradient because it has only three colour legs. The Einstein term gives the corresponding metric gradient and kinetic coefficients. The Abelian phase of the weak-neutral volume tensor gives the square $(3p-2c)^2$. Differentiating the invariant volume-line polarization by $p(y)K$ gives $p\,\mathcal L_K\Omega$, since $\iota_K\Omega=0$. Thus the roots contribute only $\pm3p$ to their existing weak covariant norm. Horizontal Chern spinors have the specified minimal lift, giving the source $mp+q_Cc$ and no direct source of the five-form. This does not use the ordinary eleven-dimensional spinor Pauli or vertical Dirac terms.

The rest of the form decomposition provides a closure test. On the colour factor the invariant forms at Hopf weight zero are generated by $1,\eta,J,\eta J,J^2,\eta J^2$. Extra $SU(3)$-invariant forms from $\Omega$ and $\eta\wedge\Omega$ have Hopf weights $\pm3$. The metric, auxiliary Maxwell and five-form have zero auxiliary charge, so those weights can only give half-integer $Q$ after adding an integer weak-tensor axial weight. They do not enter the sourced sectors. Among the remaining invariant internal three-forms, the coexact profile $\beta_K$ is unique with $d_5\beta_K=\iota_K F_5$. The other slots $J\wedge\alpha_\Sigma$ and $\eta\wedge\omega_\Sigma$ have exterior derivatives of bidegree $(2,2)$, orthogonal to the $(4,0)$ and $(3,1)$ terms just used. The external two-form modes of the potential enter via exact internal three-form derivatives, orthogonal to coexact three-form vectors. At zero background external tensor flux this proves decoupling in the transverse action. Extra modes can therefore be present in the full spectrum and have zero residue on these currents. The $S,H_\pm$ scalar variations are longitudinal. The pre-existing electric and magnetic weak-tensor terms depend on the background vortex and tangent curvature, with no linear source from a flat Abelian background. This gives the closed pencil with the specified field degrees. $\square$

For example the Hopf metric now has $I_E(\Delta_\Sigma+q^2)$ before the form elimination. The additional Euclidean Schur term is $I_E\lambda_F(\Delta_\Sigma+q^2)/(\Delta_\Sigma+q^2+\lambda_F)$. Thus its constant profile has inverse photon norm $2I_E$, while the nonconstant spectra are sensitive to the form pole. This is the distinction lost by doubling the norm everywhere in a radial calculation.

In the neutral solver write $X=U(t)\partial_\varphi$, $z=\mu U+Z$ and put $\eta=a/B$. Take $p=U/k+\eta P$, $b_F=U/k+\eta B_F$, $c=3U/(2k)+\eta C_1$. The constant $U=1$ with the two tensor primitives of 92 makes every mass square vanish, and the fermion source there is exactly its table charge. The remaining Abelian stiffnesses in units of $CV$ are $8\eta^2(P-B_F)^2$ and $(f_S^2/CV)\eta^2(3P-2C_1)^2$. The derivative norms of $P,B_F,C_1$ use weights $1,1,I_C/(CVB^2)$. For the neutral fermion source the remaining radial terms are $U'/4+NZ+m\eta P+q_C\eta C_1$ in addition to $Q U$. In the charged raising channel the new three profiles are regular charge-one scalar slots, multiplying their ordinary lepton weak-density overlaps by $(m,q_C,0)=(3/2,-3,0)$, or $(5/2,-1,0)$ for the quark doublet. The same auxiliary weak gradient is eliminated in the kinetic root squares as in 73.1.

## Finite meridian and the charged limit

Choose the first compact fit and continue at fixed meridian $d$, five-form action, $Z_6,I_3,I_Q$, and fixed external coefficient for the locking stiffness as in 94.5. Fixing $B^7a^2D_b$ and the photon norm sets $B$ and $I_C$. The B-spline calculation at $b=.001$ puts the first four nonzero axial scalar levels at $7.012$, $13.940$, $20.736$ and $27.400$ TeV. A $22$ TeV cutoff thus includes three nonconstant meridian levels and the first Hopf scalar at $10.520$ TeV. The lower bound for the weak azimuthal mode is $26.534$ TeV. This is a finite comparison of scalar modes. A six-dimensional heat-trace regime would require a separate slope estimate. The computed first charged pole is about $25$ TeV there. Further uniform shrink at this shape yields the five-dimensional scalar levels of the Hopf sphere.

At fixed $b>0$ the Hopf, auxiliary and five-form charge-one profiles have no spherical zero mode. In physical units each carries a gradient penalty of order $I_i/a^2$ with $I_i$ bounded below by a positive constant on this path. Their mixing into the original weak mass form from the fixed roots vanishes as in 93. After dividing by $CV=3I_3/B^2$, the three Abelian blocks therefore decouple from the light comoving resolvent. Equivalently their kinetic operator in the mass inner product vanishes like $CVa^2$. The independent charged tensor primitives survive for the same reason as in 93. With a conditional fixed $h_N=\lim BH_N$ one obtains the formula of 93.2 with $\sigma_H=h_N^2/(3I_3)$. It gives finite $M_C/H_N$ and a genuinely momentum-dependent normalized exchange. The exact photon norm and charges stay the same. Taking the same comoving hadron input $h_N=0.312/B_{\rm fit}^{-1}$ for both broken members gives the finite-neck and lower static $\Xi$ values about $2.77\times10^{-13}$ and $2.60\times10^{-13}$. In the lower calculation the first charged residue is about $0.000523$. The ratio $M_C/H_N$ is about $1.63\times10^5$ in that calibration. These are charged-sector tests. They do not supply the full coloured threshold along the deformed spin path or justify holding physical light quark ratios constant with unadjusted Yukawas.
