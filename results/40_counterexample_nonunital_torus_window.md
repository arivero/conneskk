# The window of the doubled torus with flux p carries the degenerate representation a on C^p and 0 on conj(C^p), and for p = 3 no unital M_3(C) representation on C^3 + conj(C^3) satisfies order zero with a unital opposite action

**Status.** Counterexample, to the unital finite triple of the first version of Proposition 7.3, item 3. Constructed by Terra 6.1 in the referee report on the 11D line (historical/reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_11d.md, section on Proposition 7.3), together with the unital repair, and written out in the Referee proofs of lemma 40; it is part of the ledger row `d11_torus_flux`, which records checks by hand by the author of lemma 40 (Claude Opus 5.5), by Terra 6.1 and by Claude Sonnet 5.5. The repaired datum M_p(C) + C is stated in the section on the algebra of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex.

**Setting.** The doubled torus of 40_doubled_torus_landau_window.md, with V = H^0(L (x) S_0) = C^p, V-bar its conjugate, and the representation generated by the compressed functions, rho(a) = a on V and 0 on V-bar.

**Statement.**

1. rho(1) is the identity of V and zero on V-bar, so the parent and the finite representations are degenerate on the second summand, and they lack the unital representation of the finite-data setting of lemma 22.
2. For p = 3 no complex-linear unital M_3(C) representation on C^3 + conj(C^3) satisfies order zero with a unital opposite action: the two commuting unital actions would make this six-dimensional space a module for M_3(C) (x) M_3(C)^op = M_9(C), whose nonzero modules have dimensions divisible by nine.
3. Unitizing the represented corner, A = M_p(C) + C with rho(a, lambda) = a on V and lambda on V-bar, gives a unital finite KO-6 datum with D = 0 and order zero, with one additional scalar summand.

*Scope.* Complex-linear representations of finite data on the doubled window.

**Proof.** Lemma 40, Referee proofs, Counterexample (referee) to the unital finite triple of Proposition 7.3: the dimension count for modules of M_9(C).

**What it leaves open.** The repair adds a scalar summand by hand. Deriving the full Standard Model algebra and its representation from such windows remains open.
