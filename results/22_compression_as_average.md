# The compression of a constant observable to the interval window is the average Q_l(a) = sinc(l ad_{D_F})(pi(a)) of e^{ixD_F} pi(a) e^{-ixD_F} over abs(x) <= l, so compressed observables converge to pi(A_F) at rate l^2 while D_F, J_F and Gamma_F stay exact

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 22.4). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5; the analytic example of discontinuity below is by Terra 6.1. Ledger row `l22_compression` (depending on `l22_window`). Theorem 4 of paper/boundary_condition_note.tex, with the generated algebra cited as "\supp{22}"; item 5 is cited in paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex and paper/real_structure_note.tex ("\supp{22}").

**Setting.** The parent of 22_parent_real_triple_order_one.md, and Q_l(T) = W\* T W for bounded T on H_l. For T(x) a bounded function with values in the even operators on H_F, acting pointwise and identically on both spin components, T also denotes the multiplication operator.

**Statement.**

1. Q_l(T) = l^-1 int_0^l cos(x ad_{D_F})(T(x)) dx and Q_l(E T) = -l^-1 int_0^l sin(x ad_{D_F})(T(x)) dx.
2. For constant a, Q_l(a) = sinc(l ad_{D_F})(pi(a)) = (2l)^-1 int_{-l}^{l} e^{ixD_F} pi(a) e^{-ixD_F} dx, with matrix elements pi(a)_{ij} sin(l (mu_i - mu_j))/(l (mu_i - mu_j)) in an eigenbasis of D_F.
3. Q_l(A_l) = Q_l(C^infinity([0, l], A_F)), which contains Q_l(a) for every a in A_F.
4. ||Q_l(a) - pi(a)|| <= (l^2/6) ||[D_F, [D_F, pi(a)]]|| <= (2/3) l^2 ||D_F||^2 ||a||.
5. Q_l([D_X, f]) = [D_F, Q_l(f)] for f in A_l.
6. With a' = Q_l(a) and b' = J_F Q_l(b)\* J_F^-1, ||[a', b']|| <= (8/3) l^2 ||D_F||^2 ||a|| ||b||, and ||[[D_F, a'], b'] - [[D_F, pi(a)], pi°(b)]|| <= (16/3) l^2 ||D_F||^3 ||a|| ||b||.

*Scope.* The convergence concerns the maps Q_l on operator systems, in the setting of Connes and van Suijlekom (arXiv:2004.14115, sections 1 and 3). The generated algebra is discontinuous at l = 0: for M = [[0, 0, 1], [0, 0, 1], [1, 1, 0]], D = [[0, M], [M, 0]] and A_F = C^3 acting diagonally, the first two channel projections have [Q_l(P_1), Q_l(P_2)]_{12} = -l^2/3 + O(l^4). On one-generation Standard Model data a numerical closure (Python, tolerance 10^-7, no certified rank) gives complex dimension 15 for the algebra generated by pi(B) and 30 for Q_l(B) at l = 0.3.

**Proof.** Lemma 22, Theorem 22.4; the proof is there. With K = Gamma_F D_F, the even part of e^{-xK} T e^{xK} is cos(x ad_{D_F})(T); item 3 holds because a functional vanishing on A_l integrates against a smooth density vanishing on [0, l); item 4 is Taylor's formula with integral remainder.

**What it leaves open.** The finite order conditions hold in the limit exactly when they hold for the finite data, and these bounds do not control the generated algebra at l > 0. In the family of 22_family_ten_to_nine.md, l ||D_F(l)|| tends to Theta, and only the light block keeps an l^2 estimate.
