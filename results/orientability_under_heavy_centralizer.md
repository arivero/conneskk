# Orientability is lost under a heavy centralizer while first order survives

**Status.** Theorem, finite and explicit. Construction by the corpus (ledger row rs_N8, 2 November 2026); proof by hand by Terra 6.1 on 3 November 2026, with the bridge exchange taken to be the normalized exchange that preserves the common colour factor. The proof below is Terra's, written out. It is the basis of STRATEGY T17 (which axioms the finite triple keeps along the chain of Theorem 20.2).

**Setting.** Let A_4 = C_1 + H_2 + M_3(C)_3 + C_4 with elements (lambda, q, m, mu), V_1 = V_4 = C, V_2 = C^2, V_3 = C^3, and H_ij = Hom(V_j, V_i) with the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product. Let

    H_18 = H_13 + H_23 + H_31 + H_32,
    H_32 = H_18 + (H'_13 + H'_31 + H_43 + H_34) + (H_14 + H_41),

the primes marking copies distinct from those in H_18, of total complex dimension 18 + 12 + 2 = 32. The algebra acts by left multiplication, rho(a) X = a_i X on H_ij, and the opposite action is rho(b)^o X = J rho(b)^* J^{-1} X = X b_j, with J X = X^dagger. The grading gamma is +1 on H_13, H_32, H'_13, H_34, H_14 and -1 on H_23, H_31, H'_31, H_43, H_41 (the four bridge chiralities +, -, -, + in the displayed order and the spectator pair +, -). The Dirac operator is D_t = D_0 + t T, where D_0 is the operator of the 18-state bimodule, D_0(X, Y) = (D_v X, Y D_v) with D_v = [[0, v^dagger], [v, 0]] for a nonzero v: C -> C^2, extended by zero on the other fourteen states, and T is the normalized exchange H'_13 <-> H_43, H'_31 <-> H_34 that sends each row matrix to the same row matrix and each column matrix to the same column matrix (with V_1 = V_4 = C identified), and vanishes elsewhere. T is self-adjoint, T^2 = 1 on the bridge, and T commutes with J.

**Theorem.** For every real t:

1. (KO-6 signs) D_t is self-adjoint, J^2 = 1, J D_t = D_t J, J gamma = -gamma J, D_t gamma = -gamma D_t.
2. (Order zero) [rho(a), rho(b)^o] = 0 on H_32.
3. (First order) [[D_t, rho(a)], rho(b)^o] = 0 for all a, b in A_4.
4. (Parent orientability) With E_i = rho(e_i) and E_i^o = J E_i J^{-1}, the operator (E_1 - E_2 - E_4) E_3^o - E_3 (E_1^o - E_2^o - E_4^o) + E_1 E_4^o - E_4 E_1^o equals gamma on every block of H_32, independently of t.
5. (Heavy centralizer) ||[T, rho(a)]|| = |lambda - mu| on the row bridge and 0 elsewhere, so A_T = A_4 cap {T}' = {mu = lambda} = C + H + M_3(C), and ||[D_t, rho(a)]|| stays bounded as |t| -> infinity exactly for a in A_T.
6. (Spectral projection) With r = ||v|| > 0, the spectrum of D_t is 0 (multiplicity 8), +-r (6 each) and +-|t| (6 each). For r < Lambda < |t|, the spectral cutoff P_Lambda(D_t) is the projection onto ker T, with range H_18 + H_14 + H_41; it commutes with rho(A_4), J and gamma, and P D_t P = D_0 + 0.
7. (Faithful reduction) On the 20-dimensional light space, A_T acts faithfully: lambda on H_13, q on H_23, m on H_31.
8. (Orientability lost) On the spectator pair H_14 + H_41, every a in A_T acts as lambda_a I_2 and every opposite element as lambda_b I_2, while gamma = diag(1, -1) there. So no finite sum of left-right products equals gamma on the light space, and the operator-norm distance from gamma to the space of orientation operators is exactly 1 (the spectator pair gives the lower bound; the orientation operator of H_18 attains it).
9. (Nonzero one-forms) For a = e_1 + e_4 in A_T, [D_0, rho(a)] acts on the X-part of H_18 by left multiplication by [[0, -v^dagger], [v, 0]], which is nonzero.
10. (Distances) Before projection, d_{D_t}(omega_1, omega_4) = 1/|t| between the two scalar evaluation states, attained by e_4/|t|; after projection alone, with the full A_4 retained, P D_t P commutes with e_4 and the distance is infinite. The intersection matrix on minimal projections is Q_4 = [[0,0,2,1],[0,0,-2,0],[-2,2,0,1],[-1,0,-1,0]], with Pfaffian -2 and determinant 4.

**Proof.** (1) D_v is self-adjoint and exchanges the +-1 eigenspaces of G = diag(1, -I_2); the bridge swap exchanges opposite chiralities; J X = X^dagger is antiunitary of square one for the Hilbert-Schmidt product and commutes with D_v-multiplication and with T. (2) Left and right matrix multiplications commute. (3) On H_18, [D_0, rho(a)](X, Y) = ([D_v, L(a)] X, 0) with L(a) = diag(lambda, q), a left multiplication, which commutes with the right M_3(C) action. On H'_13 + H_43 the commutator [T, rho(a)] exchanges the two rows with the scalar coefficient (lambda - mu), and the opposite action is right multiplication by the same m_b on both rows; on H'_31 + H_34, T commutes with the common left m_a action. Hence the double commutator vanishes for every a, b. The normalization matters: with the exchange X -> X W, W = diag(1, -1, 1), and a = e_1, b_3 = E_12, X = (1, 0, 0), the double commutator is X(E_12 W - W E_12) = -2 X E_12, nonzero. (4) Each summand is a left-right product; evaluating on each of the ten blocks gives the sign of gamma there (for instance E_1 E_3^o is the identity on H_13 and H'_13, -E_3 E_1^o on H_31 and H'_31, E_1 E_4^o on H_14, -E_4 E_1^o on H_41), and no summand involves t. (5) The row bridge computation of [T, rho(a)]; the triangle inequality for the boundedness statement. (6) D_0 has eigenvalues 0 (on H_14 + H_41 and on the kernel of the D_v-multiplications, total 8 - 2 = 6 inside H_18 plus 2 spectators; count 8 in all), +-r with multiplicity 6 (three colours, two Hom directions) and T contributes +-|t| with multiplicity 6 (three row entries and three column entries); the three ranges are orthogonal, so P_Lambda(D_t) for r < Lambda < |t| is the projection off the bridge. It is a sum of block projections, hence commutes with rho(A_4), J and gamma. (7) Direct. (8) Centralization sets mu = lambda; on H_14 = Hom(V_4, V_1) the left action is lambda and the opposite action is mu = lambda, likewise on H_41; so the represented left-right products are scalar on the pair, and gamma is not. (9) Direct. (10) The upper bound uses the bridge matrix element |t| |mu - lambda|; equality takes lambda = q = m = 0, mu = 1/|t|. After projection D_0 has no entry involving e_4, so [P D_t P, e_4] = 0 and the distance is infinite. The matrix Q_4 is the trace pairing Tr(gamma rho(p_i) J rho(p_j) J^{-1}) on minimal projections. ∎

**What it says and what it does not.** The example shows one mechanism by which opposite chiralities become indistinguishable to the reduced left-right algebra: the bounded-commutator selection (A_T) and the spectral projection together keep KO-6 signs, order zero, first order, faithfulness and nonzero one-forms, and lose orientability on a gauge-neutral spectator pair. Projection alone (with A_4 retained) keeps orientability and sends the two scalar states to infinite distance; centralization carries the metric identification d = 1/|t| -> 0 into the light theory. It is an engineered finite model: nothing derives T, the gap or the selection from a Kaluza-Klein geometry, and it does not fix the Weinberg angle. The same conclusion for the real chain Pati-Salam -> Standard Model -> colour + electromagnetism of lemmas 20 and 21 is STRATEGY T17.
