# The hypercharge quotient Y_{3,2} = (mu_Y^{-1}(0) cap S^9)/U(1)_Y of the 5-bar is a smooth simply connected seven-manifold, the circle bundle of O(3, 2) over CP^2 x CP^1, with a Calabi-Yau cone and effective group (U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y; its identification with M^{111} is conditional on the recalled fibration

**Setting.** 5-bar = C^3 + C^2 with the Standard Model action on d^c in (3-bar, 1)_{1/3} and L in (1, 2)_{-1/2}; U = d^c and V = L, so 6Y = 2 on U and -3 on V. mu_Y = (2 abs(U)^2 - 3 abs(V)^2)/6 is the moment map of hypercharge. Fabbri et al. write the cone over M^{111} as the Kähler quotient of C^5 with these weights, which are six times the hypercharges of the 5-bar.

**Statement.**

1. Y_{3,2} is a smooth, simply connected seven-manifold, the circle bundle of O(3, 2) over CP^2 x CP^1 up to orientation, and its cone is the Kähler quotient C^5 // U(1)_Y.
2. The cone is Calabi-Yau (Gorenstein, sum of weights 3 x 2 + 2 x (-3) = 0) because U(1)_Y lies in SU(5).
3. The group acting effectively is (U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y; its subgroup S(U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y is PSU(3) x SO(3), and the overall phase of U(5) is the Reeb (R-symmetry) circle. So the isometry group SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) of the Witten space with Killing spinors is the centralizer of hypercharge in U(5) divided by hypercharge, and hypercharge itself is no longer an isometry.
4. The holomorphic functions are spanned by the monomials of degree (3k, 2k) in (U, V), which as functions transform as Sym^{3k}(3) x Sym^{2k}(2); for k = 1 they form the (10, 3) of Fabbri et al.

*Scope.* The identification with M^{111} uses the classification of principal circle bundles over CP^2 x CP^1 by their Euler class together with the recalled fibration. The natural quotient metric has transverse Ricci constant 10 on both factors and the Sasaki-Einstein metric needs 8, so the two agree only after the standard transverse homothety of factor 5/4; a Ricci-flat cone metric needs a separate argument. The action on holomorphic functions is dual to the action on U, and the convention must be fixed when the 10 and the 10-bar are identified.

**Proof.** Lemma 32, Theorem 2.1, and the Referee proofs there: on the level set abs(U)^2 = 3/5 and abs(V)^2 = 2/5, so the action (lambda^2 U, lambda^{-3} V) is free; the characters of T^2/U(1)_Y are generated by (3, 2); simple connectivity follows from the homotopy sequence of U(1) -> S^5 x S^3 -> Y_{3,2}.
