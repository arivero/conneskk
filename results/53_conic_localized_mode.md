# A graded Dirac operator with one zero mode localized on the flag conic surface

**Proposition 53.2.** The intrinsic spin Dirac index on the conic surface $B=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ twisted by $\mathcal O_B(k,k)$ is $k^2$. On the compact Kähler flag take the total Dolbeault complex of $\mathcal O_F(-2,0)\to\mathcal O_F$, with map multiplication by $t$ times the conic equation. Its self-adjoint Dirac operator has exactly one even zero mode for $t>0$. The squared norm of a unit zero mode outside any fixed neighbourhood of $B$ is $O(1/t)$.

The grading and the conic mass profile are part of the operator. A fixed conic preserves its stabilizer. A model using localization for a full colour multiplet must also account for the colour orbit and the physical chirality.
