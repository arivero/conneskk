# In the step-threshold model of the flat parent, differences of the inverse gauge couplings do not depend on a common bulk-fermion term, and the Sp(2) left-right difference alpha_R^-1 - alpha_2^-1 runs only logarithmically, with no power of the tower variable S

**Status.** Propositions (Propositions 26.4 and 26.5). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5. Ledger row `l26_lr`, with prior work recorded as partial: Nomura-Smith-Weiner hep-ph/0104041 (differential running) and Carena-Tait-Wagner hep-ph/0207056 (boundary terms). Cited in the section on the gauge sector of paper/boundary_condition_note.tex ("\supp{26}").

**Setting.** The conditions of Theorem 23.6 at theta = 0, S = mu/M_KK with M_KK = pi/l; a massive vector, with its fifth component and ghosts, contributes -(7/2) tr Q^2 per level. Above M_KK the model is

    alpha_i^-1(mu) = alpha_i^-1(M_Z) - (b_i/2 pi) log(mu/M_Z) - (1/2 pi) [(B_i + F) I_n(S) + C_i I_h(S)],        (4)

with b = (41/10, -19/6, -7), B = (-21/10, -21/2, -21/2), C = (-49/5, 0, -7/2), I_n(S) the sum of log(S/n) over n <= S and I_h(S) the sum of log(S/(n - 1/2)) over n - 1/2 <= S. F = 8 for three complete massless bulk generations of E on the integer tower, in the limit theta -> 0, and F = 0 with the fermions on the walls.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 26.4) In (4), the differences of the alpha_i^-1 are independent of F.
2. (Proposition 26.5) With alpha_R^-1 = (5/3) alpha_1^-1 - (2/3) alpha_3^-1, the coupling of SU(2)_R in 1/g_Y^2 = 1/g_R^2 + (2/3)/g_4^2, alpha_R^-1 - alpha_2^-1 = D_0 - (44/3)/(2 pi) log(mu/M_Z) + (7/pi) [I_h(S) - I_n(S)], with no power of S, and D_0 = 63.09 for the inputs (59.0, 29.6, 8.47).
3. For S >> 1 the difference vanishes at log(mu_LR/M_Z) = (3/23) [2 pi D_0 + 7 log(pi) - 7 log(M_KK/M_Z)]. At M_KK = 321 GeV it is 61.69 at S = 1, against 60.15 from the Standard Model extrapolation alone, and it vanishes near 2 x 10^24 GeV; it vanishes above the Planck mass for M_KK below about 2 x 10^8 GeV. Without the tower it vanishes at 5 x 10^13 GeV.

*Scope.* One loop. The cancellation concerns the difference within Sp(2), and differences involving colour keep a power of S. Model (4) omits the frequency-2 multiplets used for the vacuum (a complete five-dimensional Dirac (10, 6) has level contributions (296/15, 24, 40/3), which are not a universal F) and the boundary kinetic counterterms generated by loops (Carena, Tait and Wagner). So (4) is the running of this spectrum, and of the parent only as far as this spectrum is the parent's.

**Proof.** Lemma 26, Propositions 26.4 and 26.5; the proofs are there. The zero modes give (5/3)(41/10) + (2/3)(7) + 19/6 = 44/3, the integer tower 14 and the half-integer tower -14, and I_h - I_n ~ (1/2) log S + (1/2) log(pi) for large S.

**What it leaves open.** This is the differential running of orbifold models (Nomura, Smith and Weiner). If only a hypercharge wall term and separate bulk couplings are admitted, a low tower needs a wall term of about 60 in alpha^-1, comparable with twice the bulk value, as an estimate (lemma 26, Two regimes). The meeting point of the three couplings in (4) is 26_meeting_point_numerical.md.
