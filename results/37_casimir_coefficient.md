*Scope.* A real degree of freedom on a periodic circle of length 2l has V = -3 zeta(5)/(4 pi^2 (2l)^4), an interval tower n >= 0 half of it, and a half-integer tower the factor -15/16 relative to an integer one; fermions enter with the opposite sign and four degrees of freedom per unit of n_f.
