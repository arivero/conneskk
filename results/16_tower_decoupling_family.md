# On the doubled parent of twisted CP^3 scaled by 1/l, compression and generation give the constant finite data A_F = M_4(C), dim H_F = 16, D_F = 0, while the whole triple converges to (C, C^16, 0)

**Status.** Proposition, proved (Example 16.6 of lemma 16). Proved by Terra 6.1. Ledger row `l16_family`.

**Setting.** D_0 the Dolbeault-Dirac operator on CP^3 twisted by O(1), with V = ker D_0 = C^4; the doubled parent of 16_coarse_correspondence_order_one.md, scaled as DD_l = DD/l; the Clifford point of 16_ko6_by_clifford_point.md.

**Statement.**

1. DD_l has kernel V (x) conj(V) = C^4 (x) C^4 and a gap growing like 1/l; here P is a spectral projection.
2. Compression followed by algebra generation gives the constant finite data A_F = M_4(C), dim H_F = 16, D_F = 0.
3. The Clifford point gives dim H = 32 with the KO-6 signs.
4. Order one is automatic, since D_F = 0.
5. The whole triple converges to (C, C^16, 0) in the spectral propinquity, by Theorem 15.3.

*Scope.* Items 2 and 5 concern the truncation and the whole algebra respectively, consistently with lemma 15: the family exhibits both descriptions at once.

**Proof.** Lemma 16, Example 16.6; the argument is there.

**What it leaves open.** Nonzero mass data D_F and a parent that yields the grading are not produced (lemma 16, Consequence for T0 and T0b).
