**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 29.1) On the tube with flat fibres, T_M = T_0 + R with R Hermitian, |R| <= C_R |y|, and T_0\* T_0 = D_H^2 + Q^\* Q^ exactly, by K-equivariance of the fibre operator.
2. (Proposition 29.2) On the normal bundle, ker T_0 is one copy of the 4 and ker T_0\* = 0, with ||T_0 u||^2 >= min(4, mu_1 M) ||u||^2 on the complement and the same for T_0\*, for all representations (mu_1 = 1.02 in Python).
3. (Lemmas 29.3 and 29.4) Near the zero section ||T_M u|| >= (2 - o(1)) ||u|| off the model kernel, and ||T_M\* v|| >= (2 - o(1)) ||v||; in the bulk ||T_M w||^2 >= (M^2 f_1^2/4 - C_Phi M) ||w||^2, independently of the representation.
4. (Lemmas 29.5 and 29.6) At the Fubini-Study metric D_k^2 = C_2(L) + 3/2 - (3/8) k^2: on O(3) the kernel is one copy of the 4, in Z = +1, with D_3^2 >= 5 off the 4; on O(1) there is no kernel, with D_1^2 >= 3 on the 4 and >= 6 off it. On the collar ||T_M w||^2 >= min(m^2/4, 3) ||w||^2 on the stated domains and complement.
5. (Theorem 29.7) For delta < sqrt(3) and M large, D-hat_M has exactly eight eigenvalues in (-delta, delta). The spectral subspace splits under Gamma-hat as W_M (+) V_M, each a copy of the 4, within O(M^{-1/2}) of the cut-off core modes in the domain of T_M and of the cut-off wall modes p (x) ker D_3 in the domain of T_M\*. The gap is set by the wall at sqrt(3), with the core next at 2.

*Scope.* Spectral statements for a chosen metric family, with flat fibres near the zero section, for the sign of Proposition 28.13. Each estimate holds on all of L^2 of a fixed manifold, with boundary operators elliptic on CP^3 or S^4, so uniformity over the representations of Sp(2) needs no separate argument, and the spectral separation hypothesis of Maridakis, which fails for this mass, is never used. The window has Sp(2)-index 4 - 4 = 0 (Remark 29.12), so index theory allows lambda != 0, and the eight states come from the estimates.

**Proof.** Lemma 29, Theorem 29.7; the proof is there. It verifies Lemma 28.14 with a radial partition chi_c^2 + chi_b^2 + chi_w^2 = 1: the trial states have defect O(M^{-1/2}), and ||T_M u||^2 = sum_i ||T_M chi_i u||^2 - sum_i || |d chi_i| u ||^2 with Lemmas 29.3, 29.4 and 29.6 gives the bound (3 - o(1)) ||u||^2 on the complement.
