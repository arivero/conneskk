# On the flat four-torus with a constant connection and a constant three-form the Dirac operator is c(k + A) + i c(eta) Gamma on each momentum sector; the constant spinors are its lowest window for |A| + |eta| < pi/l, and there it is the Yukawa block c(A) + i c(eta) Gamma, with the Wilson line as the custodial half and the three-form as the splitting half, of masses m_+-^2 = |A|^2 + |eta|^2 +- 2 |A wedge eta|

**Status.** Proposition 51.1. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand. Agricola's normalization D^s = D^0 + 3sT is used. Ledger row `l51_window`. Not yet in a paper.

**Setting.** The flat four-torus A = R^4/Lambda of lemma 48 with the periodic spin structure, Sigma_4 the sheet of Proposition 49.1; D_{A,H} = D + c(A) + s i c(H) with A a constant one-form (a flat U(1) connection) and H a constant three-form, s i c(H) = i c(eta) Gamma with eta = +- s *H, s != 0 (s = 3/4 for the connection with torsion H, 1/4 for torsion H/3; Agricola); 2 pi/l the length of the shortest nonzero dual vector.

**Statement.** D_{A,H} commutes with the translations and equals Y_k = c(k + A) + i c(eta) Gamma on the sector e^{i k . z} Sigma_4, an odd Hermitian block with j-linear half c(k + A) and j-antilinear half i c(eta) Gamma, of eigenvalues +-m_+(k), +-m_-(k), m_+-(k)^2 = |k + A|^2 + |eta|^2 +- 2 |(k + A) wedge eta|. For |A| + |eta| < pi/l the constant spinors are the lowest window, with every other eigenvalue of absolute value at least 2 pi/l - |A| - |eta|, and the compression to it is exactly Y = c(A) + i c(eta) Gamma, the Yukawa block of lemma 46 with q <-> A and q' <-> eta.

*Scope.* Constant data; the constant s and the sign of eta are conventions; the compression is exact since the window is reducing.

**Proof.** Lemma 51, Proposition 51.1: constant coefficients; X^* X = |v|^2 + |eta|^2 + 2 i c(v wedge eta) on L_p with c of a simple two-form of eigenvalues +-i times its norm; the triangle inequality for the window and the lower bound m_-(k) >= |k + A| - |eta|; |Im(conj(q) q')| = |A wedge eta| from Re(conj(q) q') = q . q' and |conj(q) q'| = |q| |q'|.

**What it leaves open.** The three-generation form (flavour-dependent Wilson lines and three-forms); the E factor.
