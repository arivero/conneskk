# For two massless flavours and a reduction Sigma : S^4 -> SU(2) in the nontrivial class of pi_4(SU(2)), the spectral action is unchanged and the Weyl Pfaffian of N_c doublets, transported along connections from 0 to the pure gauge Sigma d Sigma^\*, has the relative sign (-1)^{N_c}

**Status.** Proposition (Proposition 27.5), with Remark 27.6. Proved by Claude Opus 5.5, with the main result of Witten 1982 (Eq. (6), p. 325, read on 3 Nov 2026) as a reference; checked by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand and by Terra 6.1 by hand, who corrected an error of interpretation in it (historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_27.md, items 5 and 6). Ledger row `l27_discrete` (depending on `l17_no_kinetic`). Proposition 6 of paper/chiral_limit_note.tex.

**Setting.** N_f = 2 and M = 0, with electromagnetism off, so that SU(2)_L x SU(2)_R is a symmetry of the reduced sector (Proposition 17.5); at M = 0 the admissible involutions are the U_Sigma of Theorem 17.6. For smooth Sigma(x), the pointwise reduction of Proposition 17.10 has at d = 0 the operator D_M + c^mu P_- B_mu, B_mu = Sigma d_mu Sigma\*, in independent quark and antiquark variables. Sigma : S^4 -> SU(2) generates pi_4(SU(2)) = Z_2 on the round S^4, with colour background zero, so that D_M has no kernel. Pf_-(A_t) is the square root of Witten 1982 for one Weyl doublet, transported along A_t = t B, with the endpoint fibres compared by the gauge transformation.

**Statement.**

1. On the chirality selected by P_- the operator is the massless Dirac operator with the pure-gauge SU(2) connection B, and on the other chirality it has no flavour connection; the colour connection is unaffected, and the operator is generally not self-adjoint.
2. For N_c doublets of that chirality, Pf_-(B)^{N_c} = (-1)^{N_c} Pf_-(0)^{N_c}.
3. The spectral action of the fixed operator D does not depend on Sigma. The fixed classical form is unchanged as a form, while its expression in the fixed reduced variables acquires the connection.
4. (Remark 27.6) By Witten 1982 the sign is (-1) to the number of zero modes of the five-dimensional Dirac operator on S^4 x R, whose ends compactify to the nontrivial SU(2) bundle on S^5. For isospin j the parity is that of 2 tr T^2 = 2 j (j + 1)(2j + 1)/3, odd exactly at j = 2k + 1/2. So the discrete term has the parity of N_c times the mod-two index of the doublet on that bundle, the generator of Omega_5^Spin(BSU(2)) = Z_2.

*Scope.* A relative sign for the stated Pfaffian transport and endpoint identification, at one configuration. Pulling the original measure through the pointwise R_Sigma of Theorem 17.1 preserves the value of the integral, so the sign compares that pulled-back measure with the Pfaffian continued along connections, and for N_c odd they differ between the two classes of reductions. The coupling has the sign of the discrete term (-1)^{N_c [sigma]} of the N_f = 2 sigma model (Lee, Ohmori and Tachikawa, arXiv:2009.00033, section 2.5 and Eq. (2.29)), with [sigma] in the reduced spin bordism group of SU(2) in dimension four; the anomaly is a class in Omega_5^Spin(BSU(2)), and the two agree through the anomaly comparison while being distinct groups. For a nonzero colour background the integral is complex in general, and that case is not developed. Witten 1983 was not read.

**Proof.** Lemma 27, Proposition 27.5; the proof is there: the display counts N_c Weyl doublets, to which the main result of Witten 1982 applies, and the statements on the action are item 1 of Proposition 17.10.

**What it leaves open.** Matching the sign to a pion field needs the sigma model and its anomaly-matching assumptions, and a sum over Sigma needs a sigma model that is not constructed; Proposition 17.10 supplies neither a pion action nor its measure. The identification with the S^5 index of section 6.12 of the corpus research state concerns the bundle and the index: there the S^5 is an internal space, here the compactification of spacetime and an auxiliary flow, and no relation between the two spaces or their SU(2) groups is proved, so open question 6 of the steer note stays open for that part.
