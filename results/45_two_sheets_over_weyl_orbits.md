# The 18-state orientable KO-6 bimodule of the corpus is the two-sheet construction of lemma 16 over the two orbit types of the Weyl crossed product

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 45.2), with Proposition 45.1 on the fibres of the crossed product. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 4 November 2026, by hand, from the finite content of Corollary 16.8 (row `l16_two_sheets`) and standard crossed-product facts. The complex algebra is not the complexification of A_F; the Z_2 characters enter the selection; the grading is a chosen central element. Ledger rows `l45_fibres`, `l45_two_sheets`. Addresses STRATEGY T16; it proves structurally the corpus row rs_N18 (the finite quotient) and identifies rs_N6 (the 18-state bimodule).

**Setting.** X = T^6 = A x E with the Weyl group W = S_3 x Z_2 of SU(3) x SU(2) acting, X/W = CP^2 x CP^1; B = C(X) x W. The fixed point x_0 and a three-point orbit O with stabilizer S_2 x Z_2 are the two orbit types used; the selected representations are quotients of the crossed product, never spectral windows of multiplication operators (the corpus, section 6.13, shows no finite-rank projection compresses C(T^6) multiplicatively).

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 45.1) Evaluation at x_0 maps B onto C\*(W) = C^4 + M_2(C) + M_2(C), which has six irreducible representations; restriction to O maps B onto C(O) x W = M_3(C\*(S_2 x Z_2)) = M_3(C)^4; selecting the trivial character and the standard representation of S_3, each with a character of Z_2, at x_0, and one character of the stabilizer on O, gives a surjection B -> C + M_2(C) + M_3(C), the complex algebra with M_2(C) in place of H.
2. (Theorem 45.2) The finite content of Corollary 16.8 with the sheets V_X = C + C^2 (algebra C + M_2(C), grading G = diag(1, -1, -1) as a chosen central element, d_X = D_v) and V_Y = C^3 (algebra M_3(C), d_Y = 0) gives H_F = Hom(V_Y, V_X) + Hom(V_X, V_Y), of dimension 18, with J_F(x, y) = (y^dagger, x^dagger), Gamma_F(x, y) = (G x, -y G) and D_F(x, y) = (D_v x, y D_v): the KO-6 signs, order zero, order one, nonzero one-forms and the real orientability relation Gamma_F = pi(z c) c° with c = (G, 1), z = (1, -1). This is, in Hilbert space, D, J and grading, the bimodule of the corpus, section 6.5. Over the complex algebra its intersection form on minimal projections is [[0, 0, 1], [0, 0, -1], [-1, 1, 0]], skew of rank 2 on a K_0 of rank 3 with kernel (1, 1, 0); the corpus form [[0, 0, 1], [0, 0, -2], [-1, 2, 0]] is the one over the real algebra C + H + M_3(C), where the minimal projection of H has rank two.
