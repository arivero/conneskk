# Three lepton generations with nu_R (24 states) and the complexified adjoint of the flavour SU(5) of the five light quarks are isomorphic as representations of U(1)_em, with multiplicities 12, 6, 6; the adjoint is (3, 1)_0 + (1, 8)_0 + (1, 1)_0 + (2, 3-bar)_{+1} + (2-bar, 3)_{-1} under SU(2)_u x SU(3)_d x U(1)_em, and the charged states count (up flavours) x (down flavours) as the charged leptons count (chiralities) x (generations)

**Status.** Proposition 52.3. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand (charges and dimensions). Referee pending (Claude Opus 5.5). Ledger row `l52_flavour_adjoint`. Not yet in a paper.

**Setting.** The five light quarks u, c, d, s, b of lemma 21 as the 5 of a flavour SU(5) with Q = diag(2/3, 2/3, -1/3, -1/3, -1/3); the lepton module H_l of Proposition 52.1 tensored with a generation space C^3.

**Statement.** 24 = (3, 1)_0 + (1, 8)_0 + (1, 1)_0 + (2, 3-bar)_{+1} + (2-bar, 3)_{-1}: twelve neutral real components spanning su(2)_u + su(3)_d + u(1), six complex components of charge +1 and their conjugates. H_l^{(3)} has the U(1)_em weights 0 (12), +1 (6), -1 (6), so H_l^{(3)} and sl(5, C) are isomorphic U(1)_em-modules; as left-handed Weyl fields the three generations are six neutral and six charged fields, pairing with the six neutral and six charged complex scalars of the adjoint in chiral multiplets of equal charge (the slepton counts 6, 6, 12 of lemma 21). The charged states number 2 x 3 on both sides; an isomorphism can map the generation index to the 3-bar of SU(3)_d and the pair (e_L, e_R) to the 2 of SU(2)_u (a reading). The top is absent from the 5.

*Scope.* Charges and dimensions; no supersymmetric theory and no field construction; the finer matching is a choice among isomorphisms.

**Proof.** Lemma 52, Proposition 52.3: block decomposition of sl(5) under S(U(2) x U(3)); the dictionary of the lepton states; the chiral-multiplet pairing by charge.

**What it leaves open.** A three-generation finite geometry whose generation space carries a representation of the flavour group.
