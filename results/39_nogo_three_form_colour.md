# For every homomorphism SU(3) -> SO(9), Lambda^3 R^9 (x) C and Lambda^6 R^9 (x) C have the colour content of neither family of 84, and on Y_{3,2} no tensor Kaluza-Klein mode of C_3 or of the metric is a colour triplet, so the coloured states of the 84 are not modes of C_3 there

**Status.** No-go theorem in its scope (Proposition 3.8 and Corollary 3.9). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5; proofs by Terra 6.1 are written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 39. Ledger rows `d11_colour84` (depending on `l21_counts`) and `d11_kk_triality` (depending on `d11_z6` and `d11_colour84`). Item (b) of Proposition 2 of paper/generations_note.tex, whose proof sketch cites the 11D files 03 and 04 (lemmas 39 and 33).

**Setting.** The two families of 84 of lemma 21, with colour contents 10 (3 + 3-bar) + 24 (1) and 12 (3 + 3-bar) + 12 (1) (33_nogo_colour_obstructions.md); a real nine-dimensional representation of SU(3) on the nine transverse directions of the eleven-dimensional little group; and Y_{3,2} in the quotient realization of lemma 32.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 3.8) The complexification of a real nine-dimensional representation is 9 (1), 3 + 3-bar + 3 (1) or 8 + 1, and its Lambda^3 is then only singlets, contains a 6-bar, or contains Lambda^3 8 = 1 + 8 + 10 + 10-bar + 27. So Lambda^3 R^9 (x) C, and Lambda^6 R^9 (x) C, isomorphic to it under SO(9), is the colour content of neither family.
2. (Corollary 3.9) On a background invariant under the displayed action, every tensor Kaluza-Klein mode of C_3 or of the metric on Y_{3,2} carries a representation with trivial colour centre, so none is a colour triplet, and the 60 coloured states of the first family and the 72 of the second are not such modes.

*Scope.* A linear colour action on the nine transverse directions; tensor modes on the stated quotient with the stated action, which needs only to preserve the background metric and flux. Together the two statements exclude the two direct routes, the little group and the tensor Kaluza-Klein tower, for a colour-equivariant identification.

*Escapes, as the lemma states them.* Colour acting on the internal space, outside the little group; a nonlinear or brane realization; spinor modes; a map that is not colour-equivariant, in which case the equality of the two 84s is an equality of numbers only.

**Proof.** Lemma 39, Proposition 3.8 and Corollary 3.9, and the section "Further proofs" there: the irreducibles of dimension at most nine are 1, 3, 3-bar, 6, 6-bar and 8, and a self-conjugate complexification excludes a sextet and two triplet pairs; Lambda^2 3 (x) 3-bar = 6-bar + 3, and Lambda^2 8 contains a 10. For the corollary, (omega I_3, I_2) is the quotient-circle element at lambda = e^{4 pi i/3}, so it acts trivially on every tensor bundle.

**What it leaves open.** The route left open is the brane one: wrapped M5 and M2 states, which carry triality through their hypercharge class (item 1 of 32_z6_kernel_tensor_modes.md). The complex case, Lambda^3 C^9, is 33_nogo_colour_obstructions.md.
