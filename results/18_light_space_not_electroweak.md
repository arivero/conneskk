# In the top-only limit the vanishing vector masses do not restore SU(2) x U(1) on the light space: the compressed generators on b_L fail the SU(2) relations, and the limiting coefficients have K_W != K_33 and K_3Y != 0

**Status.** Proposition, proved (Remark 15 of lemma 18). Proved by Terra 6.1. Ledger row `l18_not_unbroken` (kind claim, status proved). Item 4 of Proposition E.1 of paper/towerless_intervals.tex.

**Setting.** The top-only family of 18_top_only_w_mass_at_fixed_cutoff.md in the limit x -> infinity; P_light the projection off the top mass block. The light space retains b_L after removing t_L.

**Statement.**

1. On b_L, P_light T_1 P_light = P_light T_2 P_light = 0 and P_light T_3 P_light = -1/2, which do not obey the SU(2) relations.
2. In the limit K_W = 9/2, K_33 = 21/4 and K_3Y = -1/4, while a linearly realized unbroken action would have equal weak coefficients and no mixing.
3. The remaining complete massless doublets carry the ordinary representation, and their contribution is gauge invariant.
4. If both members of every removed doublet are heavy, the retained light space can be invariant under the full group.
5. On the light space, the five other quarks and the leptons, SU(3) x U(1)_em acts with b_L paired with b_R.

*Scope.* "The heavy fermion leaves the loop" is shorthand for the suppression in this classical trace. A full limiting theory still needs control of the remaining fields, normalizations, interactions and vacuum.

**Proof.** Lemma 18, Remark 15; the argument is there, by compressing the generators and taking the limits of Proposition 6.

**What it leaves open.** The separate role of the top (lemma 21): among single charged species only an up-type quark can be removed while keeping cos^2 theta_NC = 5/8 and the ratio 8/3 (lemma 18, after Remark 15).
