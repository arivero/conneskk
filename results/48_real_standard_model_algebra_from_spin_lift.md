# The lift of the Weyl group S_3 x Z_2 to the spinors of T^6 is (Dic_3 x Z_4)/Z_2; the spinorial part of R[Dic_3] at the orbit type (a, a, a) is C + H, complexified at the fixed point by the central lift of the inversion; the two-sheet bimodule of lemma 45 with its first sheet there carries the real Standard Model algebra C + H + M_3(C)

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 48.3), with Propositions 48.1 and 48.2. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand, from lemma 45 and the representation theory of the binary dihedral group, without consulting the corpus construction that reached the same algebra at the same orbit type (as recorded by the referee of lemma 45). **Setting.** X = A x E = T^6 with the flat metric and the periodic spin structure, W = S_3 x Z_2 acting by permutations of the z_i and by the inversion iota of E; the lift group W-tilde, the preimage of dW in Spin(6); the real crossed product C(X, R) x W-tilde and its spinorial quotient B_spin (u_{-1} = -1). The orbit types of lemma 45: the fixed point, the orbits of stabilizer S_3 (points (a, a, a), 3a = 0, with a point of E that is not 2-torsion; size two), and the three-point orbit with stabilizer S_2 x Z_2.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 48.1) Transpositions act on T A = a (x) R^2 as rotations by pi in a two-plane and lift to elements of order four (rs_N20: no homomorphic lift); three-cycles lift to order three; the lift of S_3 is Dic_3; iota lifts to a central element of square -1; W-tilde = (Dic_3 x Z_4)/Z_2, of order 24.
2. (Proposition 48.2) R[Dic_3] = R + R + M_2(R) + C + H, and its spinorial part is C + H: the conjugate pair of characters in which the lifted transposition acts as +-i, and the faithful representation, which is quaternionic. At the fixed point the spinorial part of R[W-tilde] is C + C + M_2(C), every spinorial representation being of complex type because the central lift of iota acts as +-i. At the three-point orbit it is C + C.
3. (Theorem 48.3) The fibre of B_spin at an orbit of type (a, a, a) is M_2(C + H), with corner C + H; at the three-point orbit M_3(C + C), one pair giving M_3(C). The data of Theorem 45.2 with the first sheet at such an orbit give the 18-state bimodule with the KO-6 signs, order zero, order one, nonzero one-forms and the real orientability relation over the real algebra C + H + M_3(C), with the corpus intersection form [[0, 0, 1], [0, 0, -2], [-1, 2, 0]]. The spinorial condition selects exactly C + H on the first sheet, up to the choice between the two conjugate characters.

*Scope.* Flat T^6, periodic spin structure, finite orbits; D_v and the grading as inputs; the real forms decided by the Frobenius-Schur types of the spinorial representations of the lifted stabilizers.

**Proof.** Lemma 48, Propositions 48.1, 48.2 and Theorem 48.3: rho(tau) (x) 1 = 1 - 2P with P a projection of rank two; Dic_3 from the orders 3 and 4 and the relation x a x^{-1} = a^{-1}; the character table of Dic_3 with its Frobenius-Schur types; a central element acting as +-i forces complex type; Green's imprimitivity with real coefficients and the twisted action; Theorem 16.7 is formal for the real form.
