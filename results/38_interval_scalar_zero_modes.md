**Setting.** The algebra A_l of 38_interval_connection_so30.md, with wall algebras C_0, C_l of skew parts k_0, k_l, a five-dimensional Yang-Mills action for the fluctuation on a flat product with M^4, as in Proposition 22.8, at trivial background with unrestricted endpoint variations and no wall terms, and the usual gauge action, supplied by a unital completion of pi.

**Statement.**

1. The gauge group is {g : I -> O(30), g(0) in K_0, g(l) in K_l}, with det g(0) = det g(l); the holonomy of a_x transforms as P -> g(l) P g(0)^{-1}, so the moduli are the double cosets of SO(30) under K_l on the left and K_0 on the right, the massless four-dimensional scalars from a_x form the orthogonal complement of k_0 + k_l in so(30), and the vector zero modes form k_0 cap k_l.
2. In lemma 22's algebra C_0 = A, so k_0 = so(30), and there are no such scalars, for any algebra of this kind.
3. If C_0 = C_l = M_15(C), the massless scalars are exactly Lambda^2 V + Lambda^2 V-bar, 210 real components with colour-antitriplet counts (6, 6, 3), and the massless vectors are u(15), 225 real components.
4. A generator in k_0 cap k_l has a massless vector and no massless scalar, one in the orthogonal complement of k_0 + k_l has a massless scalar and no massless vector, and all others have neither. For aligned walls the towers are n pi/l in the first two cases and (n - 1/2) pi/l for a generator in exactly one of k_0, k_l; for k_0 spanned by T_1 and k_l by cos(phi) T_1 + sin(phi) T_2 they move to k l = n pi +- phi, the mechanism of lemma 23.
5. With colour unbroken at both walls the (1, 8) of Lambda^2(30) is a vector, so Lambda^2(30) cannot consist entirely of massless scalars; in item 3 the (24, 8) + (24, 1) + (1, 8) + (1, 1) are vectors and the scalars are (15, 3-bar) + (10, 6) and conjugates.

*Scope.* Free five-dimensional Yang-Mills at trivial background, with the gauge action from a unital completion; tree-level field counts, not a quantum massless spectrum; for nonaligned walls the zero-mode statements are the subspaces above, and a nontrivial holonomy can change them. The wall algebra M_15(C) of item 3 is an input, since every f preserves the domain at D_F = 0; it says that the walls conserve quark number and the bulk does not. On a circle without walls every generator has both a massless vector and a massless scalar, and all of Lambda^2(30) appears.

*Escapes, as the lemma states them.* A circle without walls, nontrivial holonomy, brane-localized terms, further internal dimensions.

**Proof.** Lemma 38, Theorem 11.3: a gauge parameter with xi(0) in k_0 and xi(l) in k_l shifts int a_x by xi(l) - xi(0), so the linear moduli are so(30)/(k_0 + k_l); A_mu has Neumann or Dirichlet conditions at each wall by membership in k_0 or k_l, and a_x the opposite.

**What it leaves open.** Whether a wall term that conserves quark number derives M_15(C) at the walls; scalars in the slepton representations (24, 1) + (1, 1), which are vectors on an interval and need components along further internal directions, such as the base of lemma 32; the colour sextets (10, 6), which nothing removes in this construction.
