$$

**Proof.** The normal field of 104.3 is a single complex $\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbf3$ with $C_2=10/3$. Thus $T(\mathbf6)=6C_2/8=5/2$ in the convention $T(\mathbf3)=1/2$.
