# Doublet masses on the mirror plane and the rank-one condition A != 0, eta = +- J A

**Status.** Proposition 51.3. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand. In item 2 the heavier member is fixed by the sign of the oriented area <J_w A, eta>, which the orientation of A does not fix; the intrinsic form is |y_mu| = |A + J_mu eta|; the orientability relation fails at the mirror. Ledger row `l51_rank_one_pattern`. The [dictionary paper](../paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex) summarises the mirror masses in its summary tables.

**Setting.** A, eta in the mirror plane P_tau with the complex structure J = 1 (x) J_E of A; phi the angle between them; the finite data of Corollary 16.8 with V_X = Sigma_4, A_{F,X} = span_R(1, x), d_X = Y, V_Y = C^3, M_3(C).

**Statement.** m_+-^2 = |A|^2 + |eta|^2 +- 2 |A| |eta| sin phi. (1) Equal masses exactly when A wedge eta = 0 (the custodial case). (2) m_+ = |A| + |eta|, m_- = ||A| - |eta|| exactly when eta is perpendicular to A, equivalently eta in R J A for A != 0; then for |eta| <= |A| the Wilson line is the mean mass of the doublet and the torsion half its splitting, m_up = |A| + |eta| and m_down = |A| - |eta| when eta is a positive multiple of -J_w A, exchanged for the opposite sign (the up-type member on the Majorana line w, an input). (3) The block has rank one exactly when A != 0 and eta = +- J A; then m_+ = 2 |A| and Y = {c(2A), P_mu} with J_mu = -+J for eta = +-J A, the block c(h) P_w of lemma 50 with h = 2A when the Majorana line is mu. (4) On each weight line mu of x the coupling has modulus |A + J_mu eta|; with the up-type member on w, y_u = z(A) + i z(eta) and conj(y_d) = z(A) - i z(eta) in a common complex coordinate z on (P_tau, J_w), so |y_u|^2 = |A|^2 + |eta|^2 - 2 <J_w A, eta>. The finite data at the mirror stratum are a 24-state bimodule over C + M_3(C) with the KO-6 signs, order zero and order one, with every one-form zero, explicit masses (Proposition 17.3) and the first-sheet mirror algebra equal to the centralizer of the block in C + H for Y != 0 (Proposition 50.4). At A = eta = 0 the full first-sheet algebra commutes with Y. These mirror data give the label 9; the orientability relation fails, since the sums of products a b° lie in span_R(1, x) (x) M_3(C) while Gamma_F takes opposite signs on the two +i weight lines.

*Scope.* One generation, one Wilson line and one three-form; no potential selects eta = +- J A.

**Proof.** Lemma 51, Proposition 51.3: the formula in a two-plane; for A != 0 the perpendicular of A in P_tau is R J A; m_- = 0 forces |A| = |eta| and sin phi = 1; comparison with Proposition 50.2 (3); Proposition 49.4 for c(A) = L_a and Proposition 49.3 for the couplings; Theorem 16.7 formally; [Y, x] = 0 kills the one-forms.

**What it leaves open.** Three generations; a relation between H and A from an action or a quantization condition.
