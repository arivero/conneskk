## Vortex-neutral spectators

**Proposition 90.1.** A six-dimensional spin theory with three copies of the ordinary chiral bundles can cancel its local anomaly with a nonchiral Green–Schwarz two-form and additional fermions having **no four-dimensional zero mode** at finite smooth weak sphere. $$
 \mathcal X=v^2+B,\qquad

 B=c_2(E)-2l^2,\quad l=c_1(E),\quad\lambda=p_1(T)/2 .
$$
Here $E$ is the defining bundle of $U(3)=(SU(3)_c\times U(1)_{6Y})/\mathbb Z_3$, and $v=c_1(V)$ is the vortex line. The net perturbative anomaly is $I_8=\mathcal X\mathcal Y$. Only the stated local group and its spin background enter the claim.

Add for every species an opposite six-dimensional Weyl fermion of the same colour and hypercharge with $N=0$. Its internal Dirac operator has spin line $\mathcal O(-1)$ and no kernel on the weak sphere. The anomaly of these fields follows by expanding $\widehat A(T)\sum\chi\,\mathrm{ch}_R e^{6Yx}(e^{Nv}-1)$ through form degree eight. Per family the signed colour-weighted sums of $N$ and $(6Y)N$ vanish. The terms $v\,c_3^c$, $xv\,C$, $x^3v$, $xv^3$ and $xv\,p_1$ all vanish. The coefficients of $Cv^2,x^2v^2,v^4,p_1v^2$ per family are $-1,-12,7/3,-1/6$. For three families one thus has
$$
 I_8^{(0)}=v^2[7v^2+C_0-\lambda],\qquad C_0=-3C-36x^2 .

This already factorizes with the pure vortex Bianchi class $v^2$. For the K3 choice the first factor includes $B=C-15x^2$. The coefficient of $x^2$ comes from $y^2/2=-15$; $C$ is fixed by a colour coroot and $h\cdot y=0$. We need to add $B(C_0-7B-\lambda)$ without changing the light spectrum.
