# On the disc bundle with mass M_eps proportional to log(1/eps) and dilation R_eps = eps^{-alpha}, the operator keeps exactly eight eigenvalues, of size at most C eps, below delta eps^alpha, its compression converges to the endpoint maps, and its count at fixed energy has seven-dimensional Weyl growth C_X E^7 eps^{-7 alpha}

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 30.5). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5, new in lemma 30 and resting on lemma 29; the dilation of Remark 29.13 is from the audit of 3 November 2026 by Astra 6. Ledger row `l30_disc` (depending on `l29_window_b`, `l29_window_a`, `l29_tunnelling` and `l29_compression`). Theorem 5 of paper/joint_limit_note.tex; cited in the section on the massless limit of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex.

**Setting.** A shape as in Setting 29.0, with connection (b), or with connection (a) at rho = 1, and delta as in Theorem 29.7 or 29.11. G_0 = int_e^{l - a} f dr, 0 < alpha < beta < 1, E_\* > 0, eta in (0, 1), M_eps = (2 (1 - alpha)/((1 - eta) G_0)) log(1/eps), R_eps = eps^{-alpha} and D_eps = D-hat_{M_eps}/R_eps. By the dilation of Remark 29.13, exact under the canonical spinor identification and the L^2 unitary factor R^{-7/2}, D_eps is the operator of the metric R_eps^2 g with potential (M_eps/R_eps) f(s/R_eps) n . J in the physical radial coordinate s = R_eps r, of size M_eps/R_eps -> 0.

**Statement.** For eps small:

1. D_eps has exactly eight eigenvalues in (-delta eps^alpha, delta eps^alpha), equal to +-m_eps with multiplicity 4, or zero with multiplicity 8, where m_eps <= C eps. The next level is at most C log(1/eps) eps^alpha, so it tends to zero.
2. For E_eps = E_\* eps^beta, N_eps(E_eps) = #{|D_eps| <= E_eps} = 8.
3. The compression converges to the endpoint maps, with error C ||a||_{C^1} (log 1/eps)^{-1/2} in shape coordinates.
4. For fixed E > 0, N_eps(E) ~ C_X E^7 eps^{-7 alpha}, with C_X = 64 omega_7 Vol_g(X)/(2 pi)^7 the Weyl constant of D-hat_0 on X, where omega_7 is the volume of the unit ball and 64 = 8 x 4 x 2 counts the spinors, E and the doubling.

*Scope.* Statements about operators, compression maps and spectra along a family given by hand, which no dynamics selects (Remark 30.8). Item 1 is an upper bound on m_eps, since a lower bound on lambda is unproved, and only the fermion window is treated, so the relation between lambda and M_W is unproved on the disc bundle. The constants of Proposition 29.8 are fixed once the shape and eta are fixed, so no uniformity in a varying shape is used. The pointed limit at fixed energy is flat at a fixed interior point and a half-space at a fixed boundary point, and nothing here identifies it with a complete Bryant-Salamon manifold or with a solution of field equations. A family with l_eps growing at fixed M has the same form, with the rate of Proposition 29.8.

**Proof.** Lemma 30, Theorem 30.5; the proof is there. Theorem 29.7 or 29.11 applies since M_eps -> infinity, Proposition 28.19 gives the multiplicity, and Proposition 29.8 gives lambda <= C eps^{1 - alpha}, hence m_eps = lambda/R_eps <= C eps; item 3 is Theorem 29.9. For item 4, D-hat_M = D-hat_0 + M V with V bounded by c = (3/2) sup f, so min-max gives N(D-hat_0, Lambda - c M) <= N(D-hat_M, Lambda) <= N(D-hat_0, Lambda + c M), and with Lambda = E R_eps, M_eps/Lambda -> 0 and the Weyl law for D-hat_0 apply.

**What it leaves open.** Remark 30.8 lists, in order: the vector sector on this family, through the kinetic matrix and mass Hessian H_eps u = M^2 K_eps u and then the momentum-dependent kernel, with the aim M_W, M_Z ~ c lambda/R at finite nonzero couplings; the gauge normalization, since a bulk Yang-Mills field with fixed microscopic coupling and constant profile has g_4^-2 = Vol(X)/g_11^2, so plain dilation gives g_4 proportional to R^{-7/2}, which must be separated from restoration at finite coupling; an action in the radius and the electroweak order parameter, where a toy potential a r^-p + b eps^2 r^q would select alpha = 2/(p + q) and none has been derived; the connector of Proposition 28.20 and the origin of the quaternionic grading of Proposition 28.19.
