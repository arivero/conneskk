**D0 threshold and radius.** Conditional on the unique-relative-singlet result for $SU(N)$ and the spacelike M-theory dictionary $R=g_s\ell_s=\ell/\pi$, each **positive** D0 charge $N$ gives one threshold multiplet of mass $N/R$. Sixteen real centre-of-mass fermions quantize to $2^8=256$ states, with the two Clifford chiralities branching as $44+84$ and $128$. At $N=1$ no relative factor is required. For energies at most $E$ the multiplicity across positive charge sectors is $256\lfloor RE\rfloor$; including negative momenta and an independently supplied zero multiplet gives $256(2\lfloor RE\rfloor+1)$. These are threshold single-particle counts. The spacelike signed channel of Lemma 31 has count $2rRE+O(r)$; its $H_F$ would need a physical assignment of momentum. The original BFSS infinite-momentum prescription sends both $R$ and $N/R$ to infinity. A fixed longitudinal momentum path is a separate limit. **Proposition (Landau representation).** Let $p>0$, and on the unit square choose $A=2\pi p x\,dy$ and boundary multipliers $e^{2\pi i(py+\beta_x)}$, $e^{2\pi i\beta_y}$ for translation by one in $x,y$ respectively. The $\beta$ include the flat line and spin signs. Define $U\psi=e^{-2\pi i y}\psi(x+1/p,y)$ and $V\psi=\psi(x,y+1/p)$. Direct composition gives $UV=e^{2\pi i/p}VU$, $U^p=e^{2\pi i\beta_x}$, $V^p=e^{2\pi i\beta_y}$. Both commute with the magnetic Dirac operator. Riemann–Roch gives a $p$-dimensional zero space and $V$ cycles the $p$ distinct $U$ weights, proving irreducibility at this central character. The commutative bifundamental $\operatorname{Hom}(1,L_p)$ has index $p$, and $\operatorname{End}L_p$ has degree zero. At $\Theta=1/p$ a CDS projective class $(n,m)=(1,p)$ would have trace $n-m\Theta=0$. Faithfulness excludes a nonzero projective module with this trace. The Morita matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}p&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ sends $\Theta$ to zero and the class to $(0,1)$, of rank zero in $K$-theory. The finite representation is a fibre representation of the rational torus. Every positive $p$ works; a choice $p=3$ is extra. $\square$ [CDS, §§3.2, 6.3 and 6.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9711162) · [Landau window](../40_doubled_torus_landau_window.md)

**Potential, flux and shift.** On $A^4\times S^1$ a three-form splits into four all-$A$ and six two-$A$ components with a circle leg. As Dirac flux the former give the odd $i c(\eta)\Gamma_A$; the latter give an even two-form term and can break the grading charge. As a constant eleven-dimensional potential the former are $C_{ijk}$ moduli; the lightlike-circle components give the CDS parameter and can be flat, $G=dC=0$. Brane gauge fields see the resulting two-form through the invariant $B+2\pi\alpha'F$. The two signed interval progressions agree arithmetically with $(n-m\Theta)/R$ for $m=\mp1$ and $\Theta=\theta/\pi$; positive DLCQ momentum, a membrane charge assignment and the lightlike limit are additional hypotheses. Integer shift requires $n\mapsto n+m$; reversing orientation gives $\Theta\mapsto-\Theta$. The rational endpoints are the commutative torus and $A_{1/2}$ with two-dimensional irreducible fibres. Morita inversion is an additional duality. For the separate NS–NS model on $T^3$ with integral $H=k\,dx^3\wedge dx^1\wedge dx^2$, the $T^2$-dual has stabilized fibres $A_{k x^3}$. Flux measures the winding of the circle-valued parameter. The origin phase can be arbitrary; periodicity of a constant parameter does not quantize its value. An NS–NS $H$ obtained by reduction is basic on the actual M-to-IIA circle. General backgrounds with three *dualized* fibre legs require different hypotheses.
