# The Berry connection of a moving Dirac kernel

**Theorem.** Let $D_s(b)$ be a differentiable family of self-adjoint fibre Dirac operators on a smooth Hilbert bundle. Use a fixed Hilbert identification (including the fibre density), common graph domain or differentiable resolvents, and an isolated constant-rank zero eigenbundle with a locally uniform gap. Let $P=P_0$, $Q=1-P$, $R=Q(D_0|_Q)^{-1}Q$. Include the density correction in the unitary ambient horizontal connection $\nabla^s$. Set $B_X=Q[\nabla^0_X,P]P$ and $S=Q\dot P P$. With the kernel identified by Kato transport its connection varies by
$$
S=-R\dot D P,\qquad
\dot\nabla^K_X=P\dot\nabla_XP+S^*B_X-B_X^*S .
$$
This fixes the gauge of comparison. A different moving frame adds the covariant derivative of its infinitesimal change of frame.

**Proof.** Differentiating $D_sP_s=0$ and $P_s^2=P_s$ gives the first formula and $\dot P=S+S^*$. If $U_s$ is an isometric frame transported by $\partial_sU_s=[\dot P_s,P_s]U_s$, then $\dot U=SU$. Differentiate $U_s^*\nabla^s_XU_s$. The term from $\dot U^*$ is $U^*S^*B_XU$. Differentiate $U^*SU=0$ covariantly in $X$ to obtain $U^*\nabla_X(SU)=-U^*B_X^*SU$. The middle term is the compressed change of ambient connection. A purely horizontal perturbation fixing $D$ recovers Proposition 43.1. A vertical coefficient perturbation has $\dot D$ given by vertical Clifford contraction. A zero eigenvalue which splits requires an isolated cluster formulation instead. [Horizontal theorem](../43_berry_connection_criterion.md)

**Sign test.** In the northern frame $u=(\cos(\theta/2),e^{i\varphi}\sin(\theta/2))$ use the anti-Hermitian convention $A=u^*du$. Then $F=dA=(i/2)\sin\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\varphi$. Move $P=uu^*$ by the flow of $V=\sin\theta\,\partial_\theta$ and take $D=1-P$. This frame is Kato-parallel along that flow. Direct substitution gives $\dot A_\varphi=i\sin^2\theta/2$, $\dot A_\theta=0$ and $\dot F=i\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\varphi=\mathcal L_VF$. The integrated first Chern class stays fixed.

**The wall of the disc bundle.** The four eigenlines of the angular endomorphism $q$ are fixed as equivariant subbundles on the homogeneous $\mathbb{CP}^3$ wall. After requiring their preservation, each line has exactly one invariant unitary connection there. Two such connections differ by an invariant imaginary one-form, and the isotropy representation has no trivial tangent summand. Equivalently the direct sum of the four projected connections is this connection. On the same base and metric the unsplit pullback connection also exists; its two vertical ladder maps between adjacent weights are nonzero. Continuous invariant interpolations from pullback in the core to the split connection at the wall can be made with arbitrary radial cutoff profiles. The family perturbation acts in $\dot D$ through those angular directions. The base and its symmetries therefore determine the split connection **conditional on preserving the four lines**; line preservation and radial interpolation are additional data of case (b). The fixed-background Berry theorem cannot infer these from the base. [Setting 29.0 and the off-diagonal wall term](../../lemmas/29_window_on_the_disc_bundle.md#case-a)
