# What spectral projection retains of an unpaired charged multiplet

**Theorem.** Let a self-adjoint mass operator $D$ commute with a unitary action of unbroken $SU(3)\times U(1)$, and take $P=\mathbf1_{[-E,E]}(D)$. Each isotypic space is $R\otimes M_R$, $D=1_R\otimes D_R$ there, and the window contains exactly $R\otimes\mathbf1_{[-E,E]}(D_R)M_R$. In particular a retained $\bar3_{4/3}$ has its original colour and electromagnetic couplings. Preserving the unital algebra gives $P1P=1_{PH}$ for every nonzero window. The absence of an opposite-chirality mass partner supplies no spectral cutoff.

**Proof and finite parent.** The decomposition is Schur's lemma and spectral functional calculus. For a chiral mass matrix let the left multiplicity of $R$ be $k+3$, the right multiplicity $k$, and pair the first $k$ by $m1_k$. For its self-adjoint off-diagonal double $D=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&M\\M^\dagger&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $0<E<|m|$, precisely three left copies survive, with zero eigenvalue. Take $R=\bar3$ as the right dual of the defining colour module, and let the electric group act by character $4$ in the integral normalization $3Q$. Adjoin the conjugate sector if a real structure is wanted (the conjugate does not add independent left Weyl fields). The order-zero representation is realized by $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb C^3,\mathbb C)$ and its conjugate over $\mathbb C\oplus M_3(\mathbb C)$. One may also take the direct sum with a known gapped factorized window. These operations leave the spectator kernel and its charges intact; anomaly-free gauging of the whole parent would need further matter or inflow. The factorization criteria of Lemma 06 concern real structures on specified modules and impose additional pairing hypotheses. If the retained modes genuinely carry these charges, their covariant derivative contains the gluon term in $\bar3$ and $ie(4/3)A_\mu$. The three independent left Weyl antitriplets contribute $-3,\ 2,\ 64/3,\ 12$ to the cubic colour, colour-colour-charge, cubic charge and mixed gravitational charge traces, respectively, in $T(3)=1/2$. Majorana self-mass requires a real gauge representation. The symmetric/flavour counting in Lemma 21 alone specifies a multiplicity and representation label under the groups chosen there. To reinterpret the same count as colour and electric singlets in a different vacuum requires the parent action of the actual massless gauge generators and a mechanism changing that action. The completely projected-out subspace has no states in $PH$.

For a separate singlet surviving in a parent with gap $\Delta$, write $D=D_{\rm diag}+V_{\rm off}$ in light/heavy blocks. At spectral parameter $|z|<\Delta$ the induced term is $-PVQ(QDQ-z)^{-1}QVP$, bounded by $\|PVQ\|^2/(\Delta-|z|)$ when $\mathrm{dist}(0,\sigma(QDQ))\ge\Delta$. Heavy vector exchange can instead give current-current coefficients $g_1g_2/M^2$ when both vertices are present. These formulas require the parent mixing or currents and supply no universal dark coupling. The scalar reading gives zero Weyl anomaly even for charged scalars, while the separate singlet-fermion reading has zero traces through its different gauge representation. Constructing a parent with the proposed dark singlets and residual interactions remains open. $\square$ [Fixed compression and heavy-sector term](../13_nogo_fixed_compression_not_covariant.md)
