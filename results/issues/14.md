# Charges and the charged-meson Hessian in five-flavour SQCD

**Proposition (conditional on the massive SQCD effective superpotential).** Use the dimension-two meson $M=Q\widetilde Q$, a diagonal invertible quark mass matrix $m$, and the holomorphic scale $\Lambda$ of electric $\mathcal N=1$ $SU(3)$ with five flavours. On the invertible-meson patch
$$W=-2(\det M/\Lambda^4)^{1/2}+\mathrm{tr}(mM),\quad
S^3=\Lambda^4\det m,\quad M_0=S\,m^{-1}.$$
There are three supersymmetric vacua. In each, for $i\in\{u,c\}$ and $j\in\{d,s,b\}$, the quadratic superpotential contains $(m_i m_j/S)\,\delta M^i{}_j\delta M^j{}_i$. Thus six charged Dirac pairs have a factorized $2\times3$ array of holomorphic coefficients, with rank six for the map from all positive charges to the negative charges.

**Proof.** Set $S=(\det M/\Lambda^4)^{1/2}$. Differentiation gives $mM=S1$. Taking determinants gives the cubic equation and each solution obeys the local square-root equation on its branch. With $T=M_0^{-1}\delta M$ the quadratic term is $S[\mathrm{tr}\,T^2/2-(\mathrm{tr}\,T)^2/4]$, giving the stated off-diagonal masses. For diagonal Kähler coefficients the physical masses are $|m_i m_j/S|/\sqrt{Z_{ij}Z_{ji}}$. Taking different positive $Z$ while keeping the same holomorphic action and unbroken charges changes these values. Their row and column ratios survive as masses only under corresponding restrictions on $Z$. Rescaling the holomorphic meson changes $\mu$ in $\Lambda^4\widetilde\Lambda=\mu^5$; setting $\mu=F_\pi$ supplies no equation for these wavefunction factors. The relations $\sum m_\ell=2M_N$ and $m_\pi^2-m_\mu^2=F_\pi^2$ thus require extra nonholomorphic dynamics, supersymmetry breaking and an identification of ordinary QCD observables. $\square$ [Intriligator–Seiberg, §§5.4–5.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9509066)

**Gauge assignments.** Normalize electric baryon number to $B(Q)=1$. The charge on the five flavours is $t+B/15$, $t=\mathrm{diag}(3/5,3/5,-2/5,-2/5,-2/5)$. The magnetic quarks are antifundamentals of flavour with $B=3/2$, so their charges are $-Q_i+1/6=(-1/2,-1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2)$; their partners have opposite charges. The $25$ mesons carry $Q_i-Q_j$ with multiplicities $(13,6,6)$ for charges $(0,+1,-1)$. Removing the singlet gives the character of Lemma 52. The complex adjoint supplies 24 independent Weyl components, twice the all-left lepton content with right neutrinos. The superpotential pairs equal and opposite charges. Electroweak couplings are spectator couplings in this matching.

The cubic and gravitational anomalies of the gauged vector subgroup $SU(2)_u\times SU(3)_d\times U(1)$ vanish by conjugate pairing and the reality of the meson representation. The $SU(2)_u$ mod-two counts are even (6 electric, 10 magnetic). As a normalization check the mixed $R Q^2$ trace is $2\cdot3(-3/5)[2(2/3)^2+3(1/3)^2]=-22/5$ electrically, and $2\cdot2\cdot5(-2/5)/4+12(-1/5)=-22/5$ magnetically. $R\,SU(2)_u^2$ and $R\,SU(3)_d^2$ both give $-9/5$ on either side. This uses the fermion $R$ charge one below the scalar charge. [Seiberg duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9411149)

**The two candidate weak groups.** $SU(2)_u$ mixes equal-charge up flavours, hence it is horizontal. If the magnetic $SU(2)$ supplies $T_3$, denote the preceding vectorial charge by $Y$ and take $Q'=T_3+Y$. The ten magnetic doublets have five of each hypercharge sign, no electric colour, and even Witten parity. One electric mass gives $\langle q_k\tilde q^k\rangle=-\mu m_k$; the magnetic doublet vacuum preserves the diagonal $Q'$ for the corresponding weight choice. Starting with six flavours the magnetic group is $SU(3)$ and the top-mass deformation Higgses it to $SU(2)$, with $\Lambda_5^4=m_t\Lambda_6^3$ in the electric holomorphic convention. Alternatively a spectator chiral $SU(2)_W\subset SU(5)_L$ acts on $(u,d),(c,s)$; a top completing $(t,b)$ supplies three more colour doublets. With three lepton doublets the count is twelve and the Standard Model anomalies cancel. The two dual gauge groups do not act simultaneously on the same quark doublet. The conformal-window inequalities and beta coefficients $4,1$ establish no quantitative weak-coupling expansion at $N_f=5$. The group names at the mirror and symmetric strata of the torus agree with two entries of this construction. A supersymmetric Hamiltonian, baryon map, anomalies and matching correlators along the torus motion would be needed to promote this agreement to a duality of the geometries. ## A mass deformation between the proposed endpoints

**Proposition (conditional on the SQCD mass dictionary).** Let the colour sector be ordinary four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ $SU(3)$ SQCD with five flavours and no soft breaking. Treat the electroweak sources as probes. At the proposed symmetric point take all five quark masses zero. At the mirror let their matrix have rank $r>0$. The two endpoints have different spaces of supersymmetric vacua for every such $r$. There is a Seiberg pair at each fixed value of the mass source, with different infrared theories at the two ends.

**Proof.** After holomorphic changes of flavour frames and decoupling the $r$ massive flavours, put $k=5-r$. The electric scale obeys $\Lambda_k^{9-k}=\Lambda_5^4\prod_{i\ {\rm heavy}}m_i$ in matched holomorphic conventions. The exact SQCD moduli spaces and superpotentials give the following tests. The dimensions refer to generic complex Higgs branches in the absence of gauged spectators.

| Massless flavours $k$ | Supersymmetric vacuum data |
|---|---|
| 5 | Dimension $2\cdot3\cdot5-8=22$, with the conformal theory at the origin. |
| 4 | Confining theory with mesons and baryons and superpotential $(B M\widetilde B-\det M)/\Lambda_4^5$. The moduli space has dimension 16. |
| 3 | Quantum constraint $\det M-B\widetilde B=\Lambda_3^6$. The moduli space has dimension 10 and excludes the classical origin. |
| 1 or 2 | The Affleck–Dine–Seiberg superpotential $(3-k)(\Lambda_k^{9-k}/\det M)^{1/(3-k)}$ has a runaway and no finite supersymmetric vacuum. |
| 0 | Pure $SU(3)$ has three isolated gaugino vacua. |

These distinctions are independent of the nonholomorphic meson normalization. The electric baryons $Q^{[i}Q^jQ^{k]}$ map to the complementary magnetic two-quark baryons using the five-flavour epsilon tensor. The magnetic superpotential contains $\mathrm{tr}(m M)$ for the same mass matrix as in the electric theory. Holding the deformation fixed is part of this operator correspondence. [Seiberg's magnetic theory](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9411149) · [Quantum-deformed moduli spaces](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9402044) · [Intriligator and Seiberg, §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9509066)

For an explicit local family choose $m_i(z)=a_i z$ with all $a_i\ne0$, treating $z$ as a background parameter in a chart around the symmetric point. On each local vacuum branch
$$S=(\Lambda_5^4\textstyle\prod a_i)^{1/3}z^{5/3},\qquad
 M_{ii}=S/(a_i z),\qquad h_{ij}=a_i a_j z^2/S.$$
The exponents of the meson expectation and of the charged holomorphic Hessian are $2/3$ and $1/3$. A circuit about zero permutes the three vacua. The mirror Wilson block of Lemma 51 at vanishing three-form has mass proportional to the absolute value of its local complex coordinate. Identifying such entries with superpotential spurions requires assigning flavours and choosing their complex phases. The geometric block by itself supplies neither the electric SQCD action nor the magnetic superpotential. Under the mass identification the construction describes a family of mass deformations, and the table settles the proposed identification of the two endpoints with one infrared theory. A geometric duality at fixed mass would require physical data other than this change of mass rank. $\square$ [Mirror Wilson block](../../lemmas/51_torus_wilson_line_and_torsion_yukawa.md)

**Which measured masses could follow.** At a nondegenerate diagonal mass spurion, let $P_i^L,P_j^R$ be its left and right spectral projectors for the squared singular values. A locally positive kinetic metric on the meson fluctuations can have the terms $Z_{ij}\|P_i^L\,\delta M\,P_j^R\|^2$, with $Z_{ij}>0$. These are covariant with the spurion transformed and invariant under its centralizer when it is fixed. For any six positive targets $b_{ij}$ choose
$$Z_{ij}Z_{ji}=\frac{|m_i m_j/S|^2}{b_{ij}^2}$$
on the six oppositely charged pairs. The canonically normalized quadratic masses are precisely the targets with the same holomorphic action, scales and charges. Fixing one such physical reference mass still leaves five choices. The Kähler metric of a specified microscopic theory is determined by its dynamics. The family of admissible metrics proves that the holomorphic and anomaly data in the issue cannot determine these mass ratios. In ordinary chirally broken QCD with electromagnetism switched off, $m_\pi^2\to0$ whereas $F_\pi^2\to F^2>0$ in the two-flavour chiral limit. Thus $m_\pi^2-m_\mu^2=F_\pi^2$ cannot be a leading chiral-limit identity for a real muon mass. Comparison at the measured quark masses remains a separate numerical question.
