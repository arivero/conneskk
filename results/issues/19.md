These are three different definitions. A common-scale matching claim needs its own finite corrections. The measured dimensionless target is $m_N/v=0.00381334$, alongside $3\alpha(0)B/v=0.00383161$. Keeping $m_N$ fixed along a heavy path while imposing exact equality would require $\alpha B/v\propto v^{-1}$. The fixed-$\Lambda_5$ trajectory with spectral coupling ratios instead gives logarithmic suppression of the matching quantity, under the screening and threshold hypotheses of issue 18. Writing the three square roots as a mean plus a real first harmonic on $\mathbb Z_3$ gives a phase about $0.22222$ radians for the ordering with positive $\delta$ and the largest lepton at $g=0$. The fractional amplitude is $\sqrt2$ exactly only at $K=2/3$; setting $\delta=2/9$ is another input.
