# The electromagnetic and strong scales on the one-coupling interval

**Proposition (tree matching and one-loop effective theory).** In the flat one-coupling interval of Theorem 23.6, impose orthogonal grading axes, $\theta=\pi/4$, keep the five-dimensional gauge coefficient $g_5^2>0$ fixed and vary the length $\ell$. Use the prescribed connector in the vector wall condition, the universal no-bulk-mass fermions, and no extra light matter. The weak scale here is the **charged pole** $\mu_F=M_W$, not a value inferred from $G_F$. Even with both walls and their relative angle specified there is one continuous parameter before any absolute electromagnetic, weak or strong scale fit.

**Proof.** In generator normalization $\mathrm{tr}\,T_aT_b=\delta_{ab}/2$ on each defining factor the full bulk form is the common multiple of $\mathrm{Tr}_W+\mathrm{Tr}_V$ on $\mathfrak{sp}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(4)$. Constant fields have inverse squared coupling $\ell/g_5^2$ in this normalization. Restricting to colour and the electroweak generators gives $g_3^2=g_2^2=g_5^2/\ell$, $g_Y^2=3g_2^2/5$, zero neutral kinetic mixing, $K_{\rm EW}=\mathrm{diag}(g_2^{-2},g_Y^{-2})$. Along $Q=T_3+Y$ this gives $\alpha_*=3g_5^2/(32\pi\ell)$ and $\cos^2\theta_{\rm kin}=5/8$. Boundary projection gives $(M_W,M_Z)=(\pi/4,\arctan 2)/\ell$; the pole cosine squared is $(\pi/(4\arctan2))^2$. The only massless vectors are the photon and eight gluons. The charged and neutral towers obey $\sin^2(k\ell)=1/2,4/5$ respectively; the universal fermion towers have $k\ell=|n\pi\pm\pi/4|$. All nonzero masses scale as $\ell^{-1}$. [Exact poles and wall content](../23_exact_w_z_masses.md)

Choose a fixed $\xi>0$ below the smallest positive dimensionless charged or coloured mass and match at $\mu_*=\xi/\ell$ directly onto Maxwell and pure $SU(3)$ Yang–Mills. Set finite matching shifts to zero, neglect higher-dimensional operators, and use one-loop $\overline{\rm MS}$ with $b_0=11$. Then $\alpha_{\rm low}=\alpha_*$ at this order and
$$
\Lambda_0={\xi\over\ell}e^{-8\pi^2\ell/(11g_5^2)},\quad
{\Lambda_0\over\alpha_{\rm low}M_W}={128\xi\ell\over3g_5^2}e^{-8\pi^2\ell/(11g_5^2)},\quad
{\Lambda_0\over\alpha_{\rm low}M_W\cos^2\theta_{\rm kin}}={8\over5}{\Lambda_0\over\alpha_{\rm low}M_W}.
$$
The matching convention specifies the absolute prefactor. A finite shift $c$ of $g_3^{-2}$ rescales $\Lambda_0$ by $\exp(-8\pi^2 c/11)$; a UV tower calculation would be needed to fix that shift. A pure-glue observable such as the lightest glueball mass requires its own nonperturbative conversion $m_{0^{++}}/\Lambda_0$. This theory has no nucleon. The given weak and photon observables and the strong parameter all follow along the same curve. At $\ell/g_5^2\gg1$ it is weakly matched and the ratio tends to zero with increasing length. Continuing to $\ell\downarrow0$ at fixed $g_5^2$ leaves perturbative control. $\square$ [QCD running conventions](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-qcd.pdf)

**Which geometric input could fix the length?** Orthogonality of the two specified wall axes imposes $\mathrm{tr}(\Gamma_0\Gamma_\ell)=0$ in the isoclinic family and fixes the angle. It is independent of $\ell$. A potential $\ell^{-4}U(\theta)$ from scale-free massless fields has $\partial_\ell V=-4\ell^{-5}U$ and $\partial_\theta V=\ell^{-4}U'$. A stationary interior length therefore requires $U=U'=0$, in which case all lengths are stationary. Adding a length-dependent input can change these equations. [Flat-direction example](../31_counterexample_flat_direction.md)

**Other endpoint convention.** If five quarks remain light and a top with $\Lambda_5<m_t<\mu_*$ is added, running is different. Continuous one-loop matching gives $\alpha_3^{-1}(\mu_*)=(7/2\pi)\log(\mu_*/\Lambda_5)+(1/3\pi)\log(m_t/\Lambda_5)$. With fixed $\Lambda_5$, spectral normalization and nonnegative net screening this forces infrared $\alpha$ to zero as $\mu_*\to\infty$. The pure-glue interval above instead computes its strong scale as an output. A finite point for the physical QCD spectrum, a Fermi constant extracted from currents, and quantum normalization of the tower remain open.
