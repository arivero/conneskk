# Triality transport of the three-family frame

**Proposition.** In a common Cartan of the two involutions $\theta$ (Chevalley) and $\sigma$ (root parity), a product of two specified root reflections lifts to an element $g$ exchanging them. Transport of the 96-dimensional subspace together with its colour action and $\mathrm{SO}(9)_\theta$ preserves the projected counts and places the projected top in the three-form.

**Proof.** The fixed algebra of both involutions is $D_4\oplus D_4$ of full rank. In its Cartan use the $E_8$ lattice obtained by gluing equal discriminant words $0,v,s,c$ of the two $D_4$ lattices. The three 64-dimensional spaces have the three nontrivial joint characters. Label the standard orthonormal coordinates so that exchanging $v,s$ exchanges the characters of $\theta,\sigma$. This simultaneous choice of triality labels is a choice of frame. Put
$$u=(-1,1,1,1)/2,\quad \alpha=(u,u),\quad \beta=(u,-u).$$
These are orthogonal roots in the glued lattice. With $n_\gamma=\exp(E_\gamma)\exp(-E_{-\gamma})\exp(E_\gamma)$ for a normalized root triple, take $g=n_\alpha n_\beta$. Its Weyl action is $(H,H)$ with $H=1-2uu^T$, since the two reflection cross terms cancel. $H$ sends $e_1$ to $(1,1,1,1)/2$ and exchanges the vector and positive half-spin cosets. The two involutions act trivially on this Cartan and by characters on the root spaces; the verified permutation gives $g\theta g^{-1}=\sigma$ and $g\sigma g^{-1}=\theta$. Torus phases of the root representatives do not enter these equalities. To reach a common Cartan from the original integer-root frame one can take Cayley rotations in the eight commuting root $\mathfrak{sl}_2$'s for $e_{2i-1}\pm e_{2i}$, $1\le i\le4$. Their $\exp[i\pi(E_\gamma+E_{-\gamma})/4]$ rotate the compact coroot directions to multiples of $E_\gamma-E_{-\gamma}$ in the joint fixed algebra.

In the adapted frame the image of **each** state $X$ is $\mathrm{Ad}(n_\alpha)\mathrm{Ad}(n_\beta)X$, extended linearly from the original root vectors after the Cartan change. Writing $P_{\tau,\pm}=(1\pm\tau)/2$ gives $P_{\sigma,\pm}\mathrm{Ad}(g)=\mathrm{Ad}(g)P_{\theta,\pm}$. Within the transported odd space apply the transported projectors onto the $44$ and $84$. The original gravity root pairs supply three even-under-root-reversal combinations in the $44$ at $t=+1/2$, fifteen at $-1/2$; the remaining 45 or 33 combinations are in the $84$. The other 48 dimensions are in the transported even subspace. The top root pairs lie entirely outside the original gravity grading at either sign, and their odd projections remain in the transported $84$. This calculation compares the conjugated states; holding the old weights fixed while moving only the subgroup defines a different relative placement. **Colour.** In the Chevalley frame the $44$ is the symmetric traceless matrices in $\mathfrak{sl}_9$. Their commutators span $\mathfrak{so}_9$ (commute an off-diagonal symmetric matrix with a diagonal one having unequal entries). Every connected stabilizer of the $44$ in $E_8$ normalizes this $\mathfrak{so}_9$. Its derivations are inner and its Lie centralizer vanishes by $248=36+44+84+84$. Thus the connected stabilizer is exactly $\mathrm{SO}(9)$, also after conjugation. A nontrivial colour action on the real nine has complexification $3+\bar3+3\mathbf1$ or $8+\mathbf1$. Its traceless symmetric square is respectively $6+\bar6+8+3(3+\bar3)+6\mathbf1$ or $27+2\cdot8+\mathbf1$. Neither occurs as a subrepresentation of the root table's $248=8+78\mathbf1+27(3+\bar3)$; the former also has only three copies of each triplet where the whole fourth family with conjugates already needs four. Consequently no placement in this class preserves the specified colour and puts the top with the fourth family in this $44$. This resolves item (i) of the generations note for transported states and specifies the obstruction for fixed colour. The arithmetic $44=32+12$ and the unrestricted four-family flip remain as in Lemma 34.
