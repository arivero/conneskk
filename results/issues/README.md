# Calculations for the GitHub research questions

Numbers correspond to the original discussions. Each calculation states its inputs, observable and scope. The linked issue retains its original questions and comments.

| Issue | Calculation | Outcome |
|---|---|---|

| [#4](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/4) | [Triality transport of the three-family frame](04.md) | An explicit two-reflection conjugator transports the 96 states. The colour stabilizer excludes the proposed 44. |

| [#6](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/6) | [From elliptic holonomy centralizers to the spin-stabilizer algebras](06.md) | Weyl extraction gives the three strata. The Cartan pairing gives a degree-nine map to flat lines on the four-torus. |
| [#7](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/7) | [Quark mirror removal and the charge of the retained wall mode](07.md) | A same-type wall gives one quark generation. Jointly with the periodic Majorana zero sector it has four weak doublets. |

| [#9](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/9) | [Quantized torus torsion below a descending tower](09.md) | An integral flux gives a mass of order length to the minus three and a residual radius. The flat source-free field equation requires zero flux. |
| [#11](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/11) | [Stationary points of the flat one-loop wall potential below the critical count](11.md) | All real stationary counts and the unique fold are classified. The physical integer cases have no interior minimum. |
| [#12](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/12) | [The hierarchical Gaussian law for left mixing](12.md) | Limit coefficients, conditional Gaussian median integrals, and quadratic small-ball probabilities. |
| [#13](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/13) | [The squashed twistor wall near the symmetric radius](13.md) | Eight states on an explicit open interval, with a smaller interval of nonzero fibre weight. |
| [#14](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/14) | [Charges and the charged-meson Hessian in five-flavour SQCD](14.md) | Six holomorphic mass pairs and matched anomalies. Flavour data and the Kähler metric leave physical lepton and hadron scales free. |
| [#15](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/15) | [What spectral projection retains of an unpaired charged multiplet](15.md) | Equivariant compression preserves the charge of each retained triplet. A concrete massless spectator survives the window. |
| [#16](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/16) | [D0 thresholds, the Spin(9) frames and magnetic translations](16.md) | At charge two the relative threshold state is unique. The cubic rotation sector has infinitely many normalizable positive levels. Two protected superspins, 9 and 16, remain. Their [common vector polarization](../56_two_d0_mass_certificate.md) reduces the protected Planck test to one ground energy. The [three-variable vector reduction](../58_two_d0_vector_spectrum.md) has 9,834 channels. A light vector needs at least one fifth of its squared norm in orbital degrees three and higher. Embedded states of even one-row superspin remain open. The finite-charge matrix-radius bound also applies. |
| [#17](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/17) | [The slab regulator, circle eta invariant and the flavour anomaly](17.md) | Total-spin-holonomy eta formula, common-wall anomaly cancellation, and a conditional Clifford-mass theta term. |
| [#18](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/18) | [The electromagnetic and strong scales on the one-coupling interval](18.md) | A residual length with fixed wall angle and kinetic normalization, and a matched pure-glue scale. |
| [#19](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/19) | [Electromagnetic, hadronic and charged-lepton mass comparisons](19.md) | Three empirical comparisons and the edition history. The on-shell hypercharge comparison differs by 5.65 experimental standard deviations. |
| [#20](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/issues/20) | [The hypercharge projection at a stationary spectral vacuum](20.md) | At a fixed stationary polynomial vacuum, a free lepton determinant moves the one-loop electromagnetic benchmark. The exact flat trace gives a separate vacuum test. |
