# Calculations for the GitHub research questions

Numbers correspond to the original discussions. Each calculation states its inputs, observable and scope. The linked issue retains its original questions and comments.

Sixteen issues have answers within their stated hypotheses. The table links each proof and identifies where a spectrum, coupling or field was supplied as input. Four questions still have an outstanding calculation.

- [#1](01.md) has a flux-stabilized nine-dimensional endpoint, a chiral monopole example and a real boundary domain. A single parent connecting them while breaking the gauge group to colour and electromagnetism remains open.
- [#10](10.md) needs the transmission coefficient of the coupled disc-bundle radial system. The cutoff-dependent lower exponent proposed in the issue is incompatible with the upper bound.
- [#16](16.md) needs the bottom of the low-spin positive point spectrum, control of higher D0 charge and a dynamical identification of the circle.
- [#20](20.md) needs a charged-current vacuum and low-energy matching to predict the 313 MeV comparison. On the calculated family an independent Higgs quadratic coefficient can move the observable through the benchmark.
