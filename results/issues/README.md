# Calculations for the GitHub research questions

Numbers correspond to the original discussions. Each calculation states its inputs, observable and scope. The linked issue retains its original questions and comments.

Issue 20 explicitly allowed a residual family. Its one-loop calculation varies the lepton mass product at fixed classical spectral coefficients and stationary Higgs norm. The observed nucleon mass sets the comparison scale. A microscopic prediction of the observed 313 MeV relation remains open.

- [#1](01.md) realizes a chiral generation, colour and weak rotations, and breaking to the photon on a projective product. Its light space has an explicit KO-six completion. The tree matching fixes two radii and leaves the interval length free. A [two-interval family](../54_chiral_family_over_the_five_sphere.md) now realizes a chiral upper endpoint, a KO-six cylinder and the vectorlike Berger spectrum with one classical action. Two windings select the critical area and the scaling of the boundary angle. The weak action there is on coefficients. A smooth capped sphere now gives both local interval domains from its magnetic connection. At degree two with its separately prescribed northern half flux, one mode extends and the other localizes. In the [gravitating vortex family](../55_gravitating_vortex_cylinder.md) the charge-two connection is the square of the singlet line. Both doublet modes now localize, one on each cap. The singlet gap is asymptotic to $\pi/P$. Custodial symmetry fixes the missing inertia of the existing doublet phase and gives $\rho=1$ with a regular current coefficient. An exact circle dual realizes the twisted Berger operator on projective winding forms. In a [helical metric](../57_helical_weak_sphere.md) local Einstein kinetics give an exact photon, a relatively matched scalar limit and a near-round custodial vector spectrum at a calculable tilt. Ordinary null-energy matter requires negative external curvature for this unwarped helix. A common supporting solution and chiral domain remain open. The one-generation monopole parent has quantized spin inflow, and a purely fermionic completion adds explicit vectorlike light pairs.
- [#16](16.md) gives the positive-charge count $256\lfloor RE\rfloor$ under the periodic spacelike BPS dictionary, with an independent index at charge two and a source-level check of the massive index and recovery at higher charge. The confined total vector and cubic each have infinitely many positive charge-two levels at unit matrix coupling. The vector levels also occur embedded in total $\boldsymbol{128}$. The enclosure $720/1819\le E_{\boldsymbol9}<9249/500$ straddles $E_P$. The [radius-17 reduction](../58_two_d0_vector_spectrum.md#a-high-orbital-degree-gap-on-the-radius-17-ball) bounds the orbital complement $l\ge59$ and leaves a retained Schur form to certify. The protected candidates are superspins $9,36,16$, and possible embedded eigenvalues with even one-row superspin remain open. BMN transverse minimization leaves the circle radius free.
