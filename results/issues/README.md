# Calculations for the GitHub research questions

Numbers correspond to the original discussions. Each calculation states its inputs, observable and scope. The linked issue retains its original questions and comments.

Seventeen issues have answers within their stated hypotheses. Issue 20 explicitly allowed a residual family. Its one-loop calculation varies the lepton mass product at fixed classical spectral coefficients and stationary Higgs norm. The observed nucleon mass sets the comparison scale. A microscopic prediction of the observed 313 MeV relation remains open.

- [#1](01.md) realizes a chiral generation, colour and weak rotations, and breaking to the photon on a projective product. Its light space has an explicit KO-six completion. The tree matching fixes two radii and leaves the interval length free. A [two-interval family](../54_chiral_family_over_the_five_sphere.md) now realizes a chiral upper endpoint, a KO-six cylinder and the vectorlike Berger spectrum with one classical action. Two windings select the critical area and the scaling of the boundary angle. The weak action there is on coefficients. A common realization with geometric weak rotations and the conic sphere collapse remains open.
- [#10](10.md) factors the light singular value at every mass into an exponential, a product over complex zeros and a polynomially bounded prefactor computed from imaginary mass. A choice of mass schedule realizes every small target fermion mass. A uniform rate without the zero factor and a constant-times-power law for a general interior remain open.
- [#16](16.md) proves confinement in the two-D0 rotation sectors absent from the separated-particle ground channel, including an infinite cubic-sector spectrum. The Planck estimate leaves 52 protected superspin labels and possible embedded levels in the even symmetric scattering family. The lowest positive mass and a dynamical circle remain open.
