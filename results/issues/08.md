# Three mirror-plane Yukawa blocks from a flavour endomorphism

**Proposition.** Fix a mirror plane on the flat four-torus, its unit covector $e$, its quarter turn $J_w$ fixed by the Majorana line, and a parallel Hermitian flavour space of rank three. One parallel normal endomorphism $Z$ gives commuting Hermitian coefficients $T=(Z+Z^\dagger)/2$ and $S=(Z-Z^\dagger)/(2i)$. Use the unitary connection one-form $i e\,T$ and the three-form coefficient determined by $s*H=-J_we\,S$, with the Clifford normalization $s\ne0$ of Lemma 51. Then the two mass operators on the weight lines are unitarily equivalent, up to the conjugation convention of the second line, to $T+S$ and $T-S$. On the $g$th joint eigenline their singular values are $|t_g+s_g|,|t_g-s_g|$. This construction uses a flavour-dependent Clifford three-form; ordinary metric torsion is a scalar on flavour.

**Proof.** The spectral theorem diagonalizes $Z$ by one constant basis and both forms lie in the chosen mirror. Theorem 51.3 applied to each eigenline gives $A_g=t_ge$, $\eta_g=-s_gJ_we$ and $J_w\eta_g=s_g e$. The block commutes with the lifted transposition on both spin chiralities. For any positive diagonal masses $u_g,d_g$ choose $t_g=(u_g+d_g)/2$, $s_g=(u_g-d_g)/2$. Work in units and at a length for which $\max_g(|t_g|+|s_g|)<\pi/\ell$. This isolates the three constant-spinor blocks. A negative first $s_g$ and positive second and third give the observed reversal. In the perpendicular branch the required ratios are $|u_g-d_g|/(u_g+d_g)$, approximately $0.37,0.84,0.97$ in **first-to-third** order for the within-doublet running ratios of Lemma 51. Use masses renormalized in the same scheme and at the same scale for each comparison. These numbers specify flavour data. They do not follow from the choice of mirror. [Torus block and mass conventions](../../lemmas/51_torus_wilson_line_and_torsion_yukawa.md)

There is a geometric multiplicity realizing these three eigenlines on $A\times E$. Equip $E$ with a positive degree-three line $L$ and the periodic spin structure. Riemann–Roch and $H^0(E,L^{-1})=0$ give three positive-chirality zero modes. A lift $U$ of a nonzero three-torsion translation, normalized so $U^3=1$, has the three simple eigenvalues $1,\omega,\omega^2$: the other magnetic translation $V$ satisfies $UV=\omega VU$ and cycles the eigenspaces. With prescribed complex coefficients $z_j$ use $Z=\sum_{j=0}^2z_j U^j$ on the zero sector. Discrete Fourier inversion realizes any three complex numbers $t_g+i s_g$. Magnetic translations commute with the vertical Landau Dirac operator, so these operators preserve its kernel exactly. The rank-three bundle on $A$ gives a local coefficient model; the realization using magnetic translations on $E$ is an operator on sections and uses extra flux, a marked translation and the specified coefficients. One can put the higher Landau modes above the finite window by reducing the area of $E$ at fixed degree. The index fixes the multiplicity independently of the fitted eigenvalues. $\square$ [Landau window](../40_doubled_torus_landau_window.md)

**Restrictions on simpler data.** The masses satisfy $|u_g-d_g|/2\le|\eta_g|\le(u_g+d_g)/2$ by the two triangle inequalities for $A_g\pm J_w\eta_g$. The first and third allowed intervals are disjoint. With a single weight multiplying both forms the oriented splitting is proportional to the square of that weight, precluding reversal. The untwisted elliptic spin fibre supplies a doubling. The three Weyl mirrors are conjugate for invariant data, and a nonzero Wilson covector in one mirror breaks the other transpositions. Finally the invariant endomorphisms of an irreducible flavour $\bar3$ are scalar. The constructed $Z$ is precisely the flavour breaking required to distinguish its three weights. A dynamical selection of its eigenvalues or of an intersector mixing matrix remains open.
