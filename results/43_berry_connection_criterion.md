# Changing the coefficient connection by a horizontal alpha changes the Berry connection on a constant-rank kernel bundle by the fibrewise compression P alpha P, exactly, and every connection on the kernel bundle is a Berry connection of such a change exactly when the compression Gamma(F_b, u(E)) -> u(K_b) is surjective at every point, that is, when the products s_i s_j^\* of a kernel frame are linearly independent

**Status.** Theorem (Proposition 43.1 and Theorem 43.2). Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 4 November 2026 by hand. Refereed by Claude Opus 5.5 (effort max, 4 November 2026; report in historical/reviews/2026-11-04_opus_check_41_45.md): correct with three amendments, applied: the mean-curvature term of the unitary Berry connection, the transposed adjoint, and the exclusion of vertical components of alpha, which change the fibre operators and move the kernels. Ledger row `l43_berry`. The table row on the connection on the window of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex.

**Setting.** pi: P -> B a fibre bundle with compact fibres F_b and a horizontal distribution; E -> P a Hermitian vector bundle with a unitary connection nabla; D = (D_b) a smooth family of fibrewise elliptic self-adjoint operators whose kernels K_b have constant dimension k, with projections P_b. The Berry connection is nabla^K_X s = P_b nabla_{X^h} s, with X^h the horizontal lift; it is unitary when horizontal transport preserves the fibre measure, and in general the unitary connection adds (1/2) k(X) with k the mean-curvature form of the fibres (Bismut; Berline-Getzler-Vergne, chapter 9), a term common to all the connections compared. C_b(X) = P_b X P_b on Gamma(F_b, End(E)) has the adjoint C_b^\*(Y) = sum_{ij} Y_{ij} s_i s_j^\* in a unitary frame s_1, ..., s_k of K_b.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 43.1) For nabla' = nabla + alpha with alpha in Omega^1(P, u(E)) vanishing on vertical vectors, the Berry connection is nabla^K + beta with beta_b(X) = C_b(alpha(X^h) restricted to F_b), exactly; a fibrewise twisted Dirac operator uses only the vertical part of the coefficient connection, so such an alpha leaves the family and its kernels unchanged. A vertical component changes D_b, moves the kernels and changes the Berry connection at first order through the second fundamental form; such components are excluded.
2. (Theorem 43.2) The following are equivalent: every unitary connection on K -> B is the Berry connection of nabla + alpha for some horizontal alpha; C_b : Gamma(F_b, u(E)) -> u(K_b) is surjective for every b; for every b and a unitary frame of K_b, the k^2 sections s_i s_j^\* of End(E) over F_b are linearly independent.
3. When these hold, alpha(X^h) = C_b^\*(C_b C_b^\*)^{-1} beta_b(X), extended by zero on vertical vectors, is a smooth preimage.

*Scope.* Constant-rank kernels, compact fibres, unitary connections with volume-preserving horizontal transport or the mean-curvature term; changes of the connection with no vertical component. The theorem is linear algebra fibre by fibre with a smoothness remark.

**Proof.** Lemma 43, Proposition 43.1 and Theorem 43.2: with the kernels unchanged, P_b applied to alpha(X^h) s is C_b(alpha(X^h)) s; C_b is surjective on u(K_b) exactly when C_b^\* is injective, its kernel being closed under the adjoint; and C_b C_b^\* is positive, invertible and smooth in b.
