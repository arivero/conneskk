# While the quaternions remain in the algebra no doubling exists for any D, and with particle preservation required an unpaired left neutrino prevents doubling

**Status.** No-go theorem in its scope; two statements of lemma 06, No-go theorem 8 and the no-go theorem under the extra hypothesis [U, Q] = 0 in Result 6. Proved by Astra 6 (job 06); the escapes are listed below. Ledger row `l06_nuL`; the particle-preserving form is also stated in Theorem 12 of lemma 08 (row `l08_pairing`). Item 2 of Theorem D of paper/towerless_intervals.tex.

**Setting.** The representation of lemma 06, Result 5, for g >= 1 generations, with the full algebra A = C + H + M_3(C); doubling involutions as in 06_doubling_criterion.md. Q is +1 on particles and -1 on antiparticles.

**Statement.**

1. (No-go theorem 8) With A = C + H + M_3(C), no involution satisfying the factorization criterion exists, for any admissible D; in particular at v = 0, when only the Majorana block remains. The central projection e_H = (0, 1_H, 0) has dim rho(e_H) H_+ = 8g and dim rho(e_H) H_- = 0, and an algebra-linear grading-reversing unitary would identify these spaces. Alternatively, with V_L = (3, 2)_{1/6} + (1, 2)_{-1/2} and V_R = (3, 1)_{2/3} + (3, 1)_{-1/3} + (1, 1)_{-1} + (1, 1)_0, the character [H_+] - [H_-] = I_G - conj(I_G) is nonzero, the coefficient of (3, 2)_{1/6} being g.
2. (No-go under [U, Q] = 0) An unpaired nu_L prevents doubling. Such a U maps particle states of positive grading to particle states of negative grading within the same bimodule, no particle nu_R exists, and the neutral involution of Result 5 maps nu_L to its conjugate, violating [U, Q] = 0.

*Hypotheses.* Item 1 needs H in the represented algebra with this representation. Item 2 needs [U, Q] = 0 and no particle bimodule of opposite grading matching nu_L.

*Known escapes (Terra 6.1).* For item 1: a change of algebra or representation, the even description, and boundary or defect realizations of chirality. For item 2: a matching particle bimodule of opposite grading.

**Proof.** Lemma 06, No-go theorem 8 and the no-go theorem in Result 6; the proofs are there.

**What it leaves open.** The obstruction points to the reduced algebra after the heavy projection, where doublings exist (06_heavy_limit_ko7_only.md), and to chirality carried by boundary conditions (lemmas 09 and 12). Physical chirality and doubling of the complete real triple are different tests (lemma 06).
