# With a bulk prealgebra P and a grading at each wall, a P-valued function preserves the domain iff it commutes with the grading at each wall, and aligned walls compress P to its even part, with the grading inherited from the parent operator, when Gamma_0 P Gamma_0 = P

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 23.2). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5; checked by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand and by Terra 6.1 by hand (historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_23.md). Ledger row `l23_aligned`. Theorem 5 of paper/boundary_condition_note.tex.

**Setting.** D(Gamma_0, Gamma_1) as in 23_masses_as_principal_angles.md. P is a \*-subalgebra of End(H) that need not commute with Gamma_0, and A(Gamma_0, Gamma_1) = {f in C^infinity([0, l], P) : [f(0), Gamma_0] = 0, [f(l), Gamma_1] = 0}, acting pointwise and identically on both spin components. E_0(Z) = (Z + Gamma_0 Z Gamma_0)/2 is the conditional expectation onto the even part.

**Statement.**

1. A function f in C^infinity([0, l], P) preserves the domain exactly when it lies in A(Gamma_0, Gamma_1). Then [D, f] = E f', and Gamma^ commutes with every f, including those with values odd for Gamma_0.
2. Under item 2 of Theorem 23.1, with W the isometry of Theorem 22.1, Q(T) = l^-1 int_0^l E_0(R(x)\* T(x) R(x)) dx.
3. (Aligned walls) If Gamma_1 = Gamma_0, then D_F = 0, the window consists of constant L-values in H_+ and constant R-values in H_-, and Gamma^ restricts on it to Gamma_0. For an ambient constant a in P, Q(a) = E_0(a), and the image of the boundary algebra is E_0(P), by compactly supported profiles. If Gamma_0 P Gamma_0 = P, then E_0(P) is the even part of P, so the compressions of the prealgebra are exactly its even part, with the grading inherited from Gamma^. Without that hypothesis this can fail: for H = C^2, Gamma_0 = sigma_z and P generated by sigma_x + sigma_z, E_0(P) = span{1, sigma_z}, while the even part of P is C.
4. If P° = {J_1 b\* J_1^-1 : b in P} commutes with P, with J_1 as in Theorem 23.1, the parent has order zero and order one for A(Gamma_0, Gamma_1).

*Scope.* Item 3 is the step that Example 16.9 had to add by hand; for M_2(H) + M_4(C) it gives H + H + M_4(C) (paper/boundary_condition_note.tex, after Theorem 5). It evades the inherited-grading obstruction of Result 11 of lemma 20 (16_nogo_grading_from_even_parent.md), because hypothesis 4 there fails: odd a give Q(a) = 0 here, while there the coordinates are retained as Q(x_a) = Gamma_a/5. So, for a matrix-valued parent, the prealgebra is the bulk algebra, the even algebra is what the window sees, and the grading is a boundary condition. By Remark 23.3, J_1 = Gamma_0 J incorporates the grading automorphism on the W-factor of the opposite representation, a structural analogy with the twisted triples of the grand symmetry, whose automorphism is different.

**Proof.** Lemma 23, Theorem 23.2; as in Theorems 22.3 and 22.4, using that the conditions at a wall put L- and R-values in the eigenspaces of Gamma_i; item 3 is the case R = 1, and item 4 is pointwise commutation.

**What it leaves open.** The commutative-parent and retained-coordinate questions. For a commutative parent, scalar functions compressed directly give R + R on the S^4 factor, and H + H needs the matrix algebra generated at the intermediate stage (24_two_stage_compression.md).
