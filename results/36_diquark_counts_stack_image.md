# Under U(15), the adjoint of SO(30) is Lambda^2 V + Lambda^2 V-bar + V (x) V-bar and that of USp(30) is S^2 V + S^2 V-bar + V (x) V-bar, and the colour antitriplets of Lambda^2 V and S^2 V carry the charge counts (6, 6, 3) and (3, 6, 1)

**Status.** Proposition (Proposition 9.2), conditional on the recalled orientifold projections for its reading as strings. Proved by Claude Opus 5.5 from representation theory; a proof of items 2 and 3 by Terra 6.1 is written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 36. Ledger row `d11_diquark_strings` (depending on `l21_so30` and `d11_wilson_top`). Cited in the section on the sign of an odd factorization of paper/real_structure_note.tex ("\supp{36}, Proposition 9.2") for the decomposition behind its table of counts.

**Setting.** V = C^5 (x) C^3, five light flavours in three colours, with Q = 2/3 on u, c and -1/3 on d, s, b; the 30 of lemma 21 is V + V-bar. Fifteen D8-branes on an O8 plane carry the Chan-Paton space 30, with SO(30) on an O8^- plane, USp(30) on an O8^+ plane and U(15) off the plane (recalled).

**Statement.**

1. The adjoint of SO(30) is Lambda^2 V + Lambda^2 V-bar + V (x) V-bar and that of USp(30) is S^2 V + S^2 V-bar + V (x) V-bar; the first two summands are the strings between the stack and its image, and V (x) V-bar the strings with both ends on the stack.
2. Lambda^2 V = (S^2 5, 3-bar) + (Lambda^2 5, 6) and S^2 V = (S^2 5, 6) + (Lambda^2 5, 3-bar), of dimensions 105 and 120.
3. In the colour antitriplet Lambda^2 V has the charge counts (-2/3: 6, +1/3: 6, +4/3: 3) of lemma 21, and S^2 V has (-2/3: 3, +1/3: 6, +4/3: 1).
4. With the angles theta_k = l m_k, a string from channel i to the image of channel j has the spectrum (n pi +- (theta_i + theta_j))/l, and from channel i to channel j (n pi +- (theta_i - theta_j))/l.
5. A single brane has no string to its own image on an O8^- plane, since Lambda^2 of a line vanishes, and one on an O8^+ plane.

*Scope.* Items 4 and 5 concern the field-theory Kaluza-Klein momenta of the specified gauge-field channels for the assumed Wilson-line action; oscillator states, the strings between the top brane and the other branes, the orientifold projections and global tadpole conditions are outside them, and identifying the Chan-Paton modes with composites is an additional construction. The window of the interval parent sees the counts (3, 6, 1) also at s = +1, through the commutator with the masses (38_window_shadow_of_connection.md).

**Proof.** Lemma 36, Proposition 9.2, and the section "Further proofs" there: the swap of V (x) V is the product of the swaps of the two factors, so Lambda^2(A (x) B) = (S^2 A (x) Lambda^2 B) + (Lambda^2 A (x) S^2 B); splitting 5 = 2 + 3 by charge gives the counts; 2 x 105 + 225 = 435 and 2 x 120 + 225 = 465.

**What it leaves open.** The reading that the seesaw sign of Proposition 8.1 and these counts select the same orientifold type, O8^-, is a proposal, qualified by lemma 38: parent scalars with the counts (6, 6, 3) need M_15(C) at both walls (38_interval_scalar_zero_modes.md). The spin of these states, one real scalar per nine-dimensional vector multiplet, is the gap shared with Proposition 6.1 of the 11D line.
