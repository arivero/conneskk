**Setting.** One generation with Dirac neutrinos in the representation of lemma 06, Result 5, with dim H = 32, J(p, p-bar) = (K p-bar, K p) and gamma = +1 on p_L and p-bar_R. The chain A_PS = H_R + H_L + M_4(C) -> A_SM = C + H + M_3(C) -> B = C + M_3(C), with A_SM the centralizer under the heavy Majorana block (Theorem 20.2, rank one) and B acting on the 18-dimensional light block of d, e and nu_L with their conjugates; the minimal projection of C inside B is (1, Q_1, 0) = p_1 + p_2. One minimal projection per summand, and the trace form Q_ij = Tr(gamma rho(p_i) rho(p_j)^o), with b^o = J b^\* J^{-1}.

**Statement.**

1. (Lemma 41.1) For a finite real triple with J^2 = +-1, J gamma = -gamma J, order zero and an even algebra, Q_ij = -Q_ji.
2. (Theorem 41.2) Q_PS = [[0, 0, -2], [0, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]], Q_SM = [[0, -2, -2], [2, 0, 2], [2, -2, 0]] and Q_B = 0, of ranks 2, 2 and 0, with kernels spanned by p_R' + p_L' and by p_1 + p_2 - p_3. So the trace-form Poincaré duality fails at each step: at the first two because a skew form on a K_0 of odd rank is singular, at the third because the form vanishes.
3. (Remark 41.3) Q_ij is the graded dimension of the range of rho(p_i) rho(p_j)^o, and it vanishes identically exactly when every such range has equal numbers of states of the two gradings, the bimodule balance condition behind the doubling of lemma 06. This is weaker than physical vectorlikeness: the light block keeps the unpaired nu_L, with [V_L] - [V_R] = (1)_0 on SU(3) x U(1)_em, while Q_B = 0, because the form sees the full real space, where the neutral class cancels against its conjugate and lambda and conj(lambda) share one projection of the real summand C.

*Scope.* One generation with Dirac neutrinos; with three generations the matrices multiply by three and nothing else changes. The test is the trace form on minimal projections, and a real Kasparov duality is a different question. Lemma 41.1 is the observation of Ćaćić for KO-dimension 6, recorded in the corpus as rs_N7.
