# For the heat cutoff at leading order in small masses, a vector of constant transported profile on the interval has M^2 = ((3 - d_eff)/2) ||[D_F, T]||^2/||T||^2, with d_eff the spectral dimension of the unfolded circle at the cutoff, so that M_W^2 = ((3 - d_eff)/24) sum N_f m_f^2 and m_t/M_W runs from sqrt(8/3) to 2

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 25.2), with Corollary 25.3. Proved by Claude Opus 5.5; checked by Claude Opus 5.5 in Python (the kernel of lemma 18 summed over the tower with explicit 4 x 4 level blocks, independently of the unfolding: C_0/C_1 against 3/2 - <k^2> to six digits for s from 0.3 to 10, the top-only family and the neutral direction) and by Terra 6.1 by hand (historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_25.md). Ledger rows `l25_mass` (depending on `l25_unfold`) and `l25_sum_rule`, with prior work recorded as partial: m_t = 2 M_W in flat gauge-Higgs models with suitable representations (hep-ph/0510366, section 5; arXiv:1804.06012, Eq. (3.23)), and the coefficient (3 - d_eff)/24 and its crossover not found in the records inspected. Theorem 9 of paper/boundary_condition_note.tex; cited in the table of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex and in paper/joint_limit_note.tex.

**Setting.** A_mu(y) = a_mu(y) T on R^4, with T even and constant along x in the transported frame, and D = -i gamma^mu (d_mu + i A_mu) + gamma_5 V, V = -i d/dx (x) 1 + 1 (x) D_F, whose eigenvalues are k_n + mu_i (25_unfolding_to_circle.md). The heat cutoff t = 1/Lambda^2, s = l Lambda, S(s) = sum_{n in Z} exp(-n^2 pi^2/s^2), and d_eff = d log S/d log s.

**Statement.**

1. With d_ij = mu_i - mu_j and I_t as in lemma 18, the kernel over the tower is C_t(q^2) = (1/(8 pi^2)) sum_n sum_ij |T_ij|^2 [(2/t + d_ij^2 + q^2) I_t((k_n + mu_i)^2, (k_n + mu_j)^2; q^2) - (e^{-t (k_n + mu_i)^2} + e^{-t (k_n + mu_j)^2})/t]; the sum converges, with no expansion in D_F.
2. At fixed s and for fixed nonzero T, M^2 = C_0/C_1 = ((3 - d_eff)/2) ||[D_F, T]||^2/||T||^2 + O(||D_F||^4/Lambda^2), in Hilbert-Schmidt norms. d_eff tends to 0 for s << 1 and to 1 for s >> 1, and the factor (3 - d_eff)/2 is 1.5000, 1.4990, 1.3935, 1.1414, 1.0240, 1.0022, 1.0000 at s = 0.3, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 5.
3. (Corollary 25.3) For the three-generation Standard Model representation, with the literal particle and antiparticle trace, Dirac masses small compared with Lambda and 1/l, and T a charged generator of SU(2)_L, M_W^2 = ((3 - d_eff)/24) sum_f N_f m_f^2: 8 M_W^2 = sum N_f m_f^2 at d_eff = 0, the CCM relation, and 12 M_W^2 = sum N_f m_f^2 at d_eff = 1. For the top alone m_t/M_W = (8/(3 - d_eff))^{1/2}, from sqrt(8/3) = 1.633 to 2, that is y_t from 2g/sqrt(3) to sqrt(2) g, and M_W^2/M_Z^2 = 5/8 at all s to this order.
4. (Remark) For positive Laplace cutoffs f(z) = int e^{-tz} dnu(t) as in lemma 18, M^2 = ((3 - d_nu)/2) ||[D_F, T]||^2/||T||^2, with the same endpoints and a crossover that depends on nu.

*Scope.* A Taylor quotient for a prescribed constant-profile vector, at leading order in D_F, about a prescribed background and not a demonstrated vacuum; Taylor and pole masses differ in general, and a lowest physical gauge branch needs an admissible gauge field, a positive kinetic form and an isolated lowest branch. Flat profiles on one interval, no bulk masses, a matching scale and no running; the top-only statement counts all twelve doublets on the same interval, and already in the towerless top-only family M_W^2 = (3/8) m_t^2 - (23/128) m_t^4/Lambda^2. The value at d_eff = 1 is the one five-dimensional covariance requires on the unfolded circle, where A_5 = D_F is constant, and the unfolding removes the boundary heat-kernel terms for this operator and these generators. Vectors that are Dirichlet at a strict wall, as in Proposition 22.8, are a different boundary problem.

**Proof.** Lemma 25, Theorem 25.2 and Corollary 25.3; the proofs are there. Equation (1) of lemma 18 applies block by block, justified by Gaussian bounds. To second order in mu the bracket at q = 0 is d_ij^2 e^{-k^2} (1 - (2/3) k^2) and at order q^2 it is (2/3) e^{-k^2}; terms odd in k cancel between n and -n, and the sum gives 3/2 - <k^2> with <k^2> = (1/2) d log S/d log s. The corollary uses ||T||^2 proportional to Q_11 = 12 and ||[D_F, T]||^2 to K_11 = sum N_f m_f^2.

**What it leaves open.** In this constant-profile calculation the factor 3/2 of Proposition F of paper/towerless_intervals.tex is the ratio of the two endpoints. Priority for the coefficient and its crossover is open pending a wider search. The comparison with measured values in lemma 25 (formally d_eff = 1.27, or 1.06 with a running top mass) draws no conclusion, since no running from a matching scale has been computed; with bulk masses for the light fields the kinetic coefficient must be recomputed.
