# With only an up-type Dirac mass the finite heat trace is 84 + 12 e^{-x}, and the 96 states of H_F split as 84 + 12 in two ways, by the top and by the neutrinos

**Status.** Propositions (Propositions 21.2 and 21.3); the ledger records them as one row of kind claim. Proved by Claude Opus 5.5. Ledger row `l21_counts`. The split 96 = 84 + 12 is stated in the introduction of paper/generations_note.tex ("\supp{21}").

**Setting.** Three generations with nu_R, so dim H_F = 96 (Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli). The heat cutoff and the constant-background density of lemma 18, with only an up-type Dirac mass m, no Majorana mass, and x = m^2/Lambda^2. For the counts, four states per Dirac fermion and colour, with Dirac neutrinos.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 21.2) Tr exp(-V_0^2/Lambda^2) = 84 + 12 e^{-x}. So the coefficient of the vacuum density moves from 96 to 84 along the radial direction, with no finite nonzero stationary point on this free radial ray (No-go theorem 9 and Proposition 11 of lemma 18). At m = 0 the derivative is zero.
2. (Proposition 21.3) 96 = 84 + 12 as five quarks and all leptons (60 + 12 + 12) plus the top (12), and 96 = 84 + 12 as all electrically charged fermions (72 + 12) plus the neutrinos (12). The two families of 84 overlap in 72 states, and each is the other with the top and the neutrinos exchanged. Without nu_R the first family has 78 states and the second stays at 84.
3. In eleven dimensions the three-form C_3 has C(9, 3) = 84 on-shell components, and its dual C_6 has C(9, 6) = 84.

*Scope.* Item 1 concerns the literal flat-space action with no added potential or constraint. Compact support can give a plateau; sign-changing cutoffs, additional terms, constraints and paths with an additional fixed noncommuting mass lie outside it. The top-only family is the endpoint of this descending direction only, since a radial direction giving other species masses descends further. The 84 of H_F is a complex dimension and the count of C_3 a real bosonic polarization count, so an identification needs a map of representations and degrees of freedom.

**Proof.** Lemma 21, Propositions 21.2 and 21.3; the proofs are there. The top block has 3 colours, 2 chiralities and charge conjugates, so 12 states, each with V_0^2 = m^2; the rest is counting.

**What it leaves open.** Lemma 21 proposes, with no derivation, relating the first family to C_3 or to an M2 or M5 brane and the second to the dual object, with the exchange of the top and the neutrinos as the content of the duality on the Standard Model side; equality of the number is insufficient for this. What the eleven-dimensional line proves about this exchange is collected in paper/generations_note.tex.
