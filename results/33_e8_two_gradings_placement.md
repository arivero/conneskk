# Two commuting gradings of order three on e8, by SU(9) and by the family SU(3), place the left-handed 10 and H_u of a generation in the C_3 sector and (d^c, L), N and (D^c, H_d), nu^c in the C_6 and gravity sectors according to a sign t, so that the 96 states split as 45 + 45 + 6 for t = +1/2 and 33 + 33 + 30 for t = -1/2

**Status.** Theorem (Lemma 4.A and Theorem 4.4). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand from branching rules; a proof of the table and of the grades by Terra 6.1 is written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 33. Ledger row `d11_e8_grading`. Lemma 3 and Theorem 4 of paper/generations_note.tex ("The proofs are in \supp{33}").

**Setting.** e8 = sl(9) + Lambda^3 C^9 + Lambda^6 C^9 (80 + 84 + 84), the grading b = 0, 1, 2 of an inner automorphism of order three; the names gravity, C_3 and C_6 for the three pieces follow West's level decomposition of e8(8), recalled. The family grading a = 0, 1, 2 is given by the centre s_f of the SU(3) of (SU(3) x E6)/Z_3 in E8, with C^9 = (3, 1)_{-2} + (1, 6)_1 under SU(3) x SU(6) x U(1). SU(2)_I is the commutant of the Georgi-Glashow SU(5) in E6, with Cartan T_3, and t = +-1/2.

**Statement.**

1. (Lemma 4.A) s_f commutes with the SU(9) grading, its centralizer is (SU(3) x E6)/Z_3, and b = a + 2 T_3 mod 3; in the row a = 1 the pieces have dimensions 18 (b = 0), 45 (b = 1) and 18 (b = 2).
2. (Theorem 4.4) For the generation 16 in the 27 of family grade a = 1, in the left-handed basis, the 10 = (Q, u^c, e^c) and the 5 = (D, H_u) lie in g_{1,1}, the C_3 sector; (d^c, L) and N lie in g_{1,1+2t}, and (D^c, H_d) and nu^c in g_{1,1-2t}; antiparticles lie in g_{2,-b}. The two signs of t differ by the Weyl reflection of SU(2)_I.
3. With conjugates, the top block has (C_3, C_6, gravity) = (6, 6, 0) and the neutrino block (3, 3, 6), for both signs of t, and the 96 states of H_F split as 45 + 45 + 6 for t = +1/2 and 33 + 33 + 30 for t = -1/2.

*Scope.* t_L and t^c lie in b = 1 in the left-handed basis, and t^c is an antitop field, so the two chiralities of the top particle lie in different sectors. The names C_3 and C_6 express an algebraic grading, and a physical M2/M5 identification requires a field construction. Selecting t = +1/2, where nu^c is the only state in the gravity sector, is a proposal.

**Proof.** Lemma 33, Lemma 4.A and Theorem 4.4, and the section "Further proofs" there: Lambda^3 of (3, 1)_{-2} + (1, 6)_1 is (1, 1)_{-6} + (3-bar, 6)_{-3} + (3, 15)_0 + (1, 20)_3; the column a = 0 sums to 8 + 78 and the row a = 1 to 3 x 27; in the E6 charge normalization, T_3 = (psi + chi)/8 vanishes on the 10 and the 5 and is +-1/2 on the doublet members.

**What it leaves open.** A finite real spectral triple on the graded e8 with D_F = ad(Higgs), its order zero and order one, and its comparison with C + H + M_3(C); the E11 level decomposition beyond E8. The couplings that the gradings allow are 33_yukawa_grades.md, and the exchange of the two blocks within this structure is excluded by 33_nogo_graded_e8_exchange.md.
