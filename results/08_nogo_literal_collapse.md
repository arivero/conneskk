# Literal collapse of the massless chiral interval in its product with a finite KO-6 triple leaves an empty light sector at every fixed cutoff

**Status.** No-go theorem in its scope (No-go theorem 8 of lemma 08). Proved by Astra 6 (job 08). Ledger row `l08_nogo`, depending on `l05_limits`. Part of Proposition A.1 of paper/towerless_intervals.tex.

**Setting.** The heavy-limit model with exactly the product Dirac operator of Result 5, D_F = D_0 (x) 1 + Gamma_0 (x) D_I, with the massless chiral interval or its minimal truncation and m = pi/(2l); a fixed energy cutoff Lambda.

**Statement.**

1. Anticommutation of D_0 and Gamma_0 gives D_F^2 = D_0^2 (x) 1 + 1 (x) D_I^2.
2. The chiral interval and its minimal truncation satisfy D_I^2 >= pi^2/(4l^2), so |D_F| >= pi/(2l).
3. Hence 1_{[0,Lambda]}(|D_F|) = 0 for l < pi/(2 Lambda): at every fixed cutoff the light subspace is zero for small l.
4. The same holds for the alternative product convention, by unitary equivalence.

*Hypotheses and escapes.* The product operator exactly as in Result 5, with a massless interval and no boundary coupling. The lemma states that retaining a light sector requires additional dynamics or a modified limiting prescription. The tuned bulk mass of lemma 09 (Theorem 9.9; 09_two_limits.md) and the scaled boundary coupling of lemma 12 (Theorem 12.7; 12_light_spectrum_towerless_limit.md) are such prescriptions.

**Proof.** Lemma 08, No-go theorem 8; the proof is there.

**What it leaves open.** The exact product supplies an algebraic model at each finite l, and neither 9 nor 11 is established by it as the dimension of a surviving heavy-W/Z limit (lemma 08, consequence for 9 versus 11).
