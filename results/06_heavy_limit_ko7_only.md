# The strict heavy-limit light spectrum of the Standard Model with one or three unpaired left neutrinos admits the KO-7 reduction and excludes the KO-5 reduction

**Status.** Proposition (Propositions 5, 7 and 9 of lemma 06). Proved by Astra 6 (job 06); checked by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand and by Terra 6.1 by hand (historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_nogo_19.md). Ledger row `l06_nuL`, of kind no_go; the row also records No-go theorem 8, filed as 06_nogo_doubling.md. Item 2 of Theorem D of paper/towerless_intervals.tex.

**Setting.** The Standard Model representation of lemma 06, Result 5: F = C + C^3, H_p = (C^2 (x) F)_L + (C^2 (x) F)_R, H = H_p + H_pbar, J(p, pbar) = (K pbar, K p), gamma = diag(I_8, -I_8, -I_8, I_8), Dirac block S_p = [[0, Y^\\*], [Y, 0]] (CCM, Proposition 2.4 and Theorem 2.21). The strict projection of canonical section 3 removes nu_R, J nu_R and the complete up-type Dirac sector, restricts to B = {(lambda, Q_lambda, m)} = C + M_3(C), and keeps D_light = PSP on d_L, d_R, e_L, e_R, nu_L and their conjugates. In three generations, removing the three right-neutrino Majorana pairs and the top Dirac sector leaves 96 - 6 - 12 = 78 complex dimensions, and removing all up-type sectors leaves 54.

**Statement.**

1. In one generation, and in three generations under either removal, with the surviving left neutrinos unpaired and D_light = PSP, the light triple admits a KO-7 reduction and excludes KO-5.
2. The charged sectors (d and e, with any masses and with mixing within the species blocks) admit both reductions. On N = span(nu_L, J nu_L) both algebra actions are the character lambda and PSP|_N = 0, and gamma_N = sigma_3, J_N = sigma_1 K, U_N = sigma_1 is a KO-7 reduction; KO-5 is excluded by the neutral-sector criterion, and the descended spaces would have odd dimensions 9, 39 or 27.
3. Dropping the neutral sector, or retaining right neutrinos with purely Dirac masses, permits both reductions.
4. (Proposition 9) The heavy projection keeps KO-degree six, so the product with spacetime has degree 4 + 6 = 10 mod 8. A KO-5 reduction gives the formal representative 4 + 5 = 9; it exists for the charged truncation and for the pure-Dirac completion, which also admit KO-7. For the strict light spectrum only 4 + 7 = 11 is available among the proposed reductions. The odd reductions use J_{4+5} = (J_M gamma_M) (x) j_5 and J_{4+7} = J_M (x) j_7.

*Scope.* A statement about that finite triple; it concerns no parent manifold (ledger row). No finite-dimensional operation here changes heat dimension, so the loss of one dimension is at most a chosen Clifford-factor removal (Proposition 9).

**Proof.** Lemma 06, Results 5, 7 and 9; the proofs are there. Charged sectors reduce to copies of Result 3 by singular-value decomposition with bimodule unitaries; the neutral bimodule is inequivalent to the charged ones, so every permitted U preserves it, and Lemma 4 with M_N = 0 and odd r excludes KO-5.

**What it leaves open.** With particle preservation required, the strict light spectrum admits neither reduction (06_nogo_doubling.md, item 2). A proved metric collapse realizing the loss of one dimension is not supplied.
