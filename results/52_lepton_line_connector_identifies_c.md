# The lepton sub-bimodule of the two sheets has eight states and contains the connector; the connector's order-one condition reduces C + H + C' + M_3(C) to the Standard Model algebra, identifying the two copies of C, exactly when the Majorana line is a weight line of the lifted transposition; the trace-form pairing of the lepton generation over C + H is nondegenerate

**Status.** Proposition 52.1. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand, from Theorems 16.7, 20.1 and 20.2 and Propositions 49.2, 49.5 and 50.3. The dictionary needs the eigenline condition; the pairing is nondegenerate over Q, with the matrices of Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli, Proposition 2.30 (3). Ledger row `l52_lepton_line`. Not yet in a paper.

**Setting.** V_X = Sigma_4 with A_{F,X} = C + H (Proposition 49.2), V_Y = Cv + C^3 with A_{F,Y} = C' + M_3(C); H_F = Hom(V, Sigma_4) + Hom(Sigma_4, V), 32 states; D_F = D_Y + S_{T_kappa} with D_Y the split block of Proposition 50.3 and T_kappa the connector on the lines w in Sigma_R and v.

**Statement.** (i) H_F = H_l + H_q with H_l = Sigma_4 (x) conj(Cv) + Cv (x) conj(Sigma_4) (8 states) and H_q the quark generation of Proposition 49.5 (24 states), both invariant under the algebra, J_F, Gamma_F, D_Y and the connector, whose vectors e, f lie in H_l; H_l carries nu_R, e_R (the weight lines of x on the lepton line, w the Majorana line, under the eigenline condition), the left doublet and their conjugates. (ii) The largest subalgebra of C + H + C' + M_3(C) with order one for D_F (kappa != 0) is A_SM = {(lambda, q, lambda, m)} exactly when Cw is a weight line of x, the two copies of C identified, and R + H + M_3(C) otherwise; on A_SM, H_l is the summand H_F^(1) of Chamseddine, Connes and Marcolli with the Majorana block on nu_R. (iii) The trace-form pairing on H_l for p_1 = (1_C, 0, 1_{C'}, 0), p_2 = (0, 1_H, 0, 0), p_3 = (0, 0, 0, e_11) is [[0, 2, 0], [-2, 0, 0], [0, 0, 0]]: nondegenerate over Q over C + H (determinant 4), the matrices of Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli, Proposition 2.30 (3), whose Corollary 2.31 obtains nondegeneracy over the three summands from the two classes; on H_q it is [[0, 0, 2], [0, 0, -2], [-2, 2, 0]] and on H_F the sum, skew of rank two (Theorem 41.2 up to sign).

*Scope.* One generation; the axis, connector and blocks are inputs; the sign convention of lemma 49.

**Proof.** Lemma 52, Proposition 52.1: P_v commutes with A_{F,Y}; the rank-one defect formula of S_{T_kappa}, which gives A(T_kappa) as the largest *-subalgebra, intersected with the four-summand algebra, a matrix diagonal in two different bases being scalar; the traces tr(Gamma a_p), tr(b_p) on the lepton line.

**What it leaves open.** Three generations; the orientability failure with nu_R (Proposition 41.4) is unchanged.
