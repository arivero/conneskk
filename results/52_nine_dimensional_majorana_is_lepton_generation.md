# A Majorana spinor of nine-dimensional Minkowski space is, under Spin(1, 3) x Spin(5), the complex space (2, 1) (x) 4: four left-handed Weyl spinors in the spinor fibre of the five-dimensional internal space, the sheet W = H^2, which under K_p is the left doublet and the right-handed pair, one lepton generation with nu_R; on its self-conjugate sector the masses are the torsion |eta| for both members, the sectors of nonzero momentum pairing into a generation and a mirror, and a Majorana mass for nu_R alone is wall data in the scope of constant bulk data

**Setting.** Spin(1, 8) with the Dirac spinor C^16; M^4 x K_5 with K_5 a spin five-manifold, structure group Spin(1, 3) x Spin(5) = Spin(1, 3) x Sp(2); for K_5 = A x S^1 the fibre is the sheet of Proposition 49.1 with c(e_5) = +-Gamma.

**Statement.** (i) C^16 = (2, 1) (x) 4 + (1, 2) (x) 4 under Spin(1, 3) x Spin(5); a Majorana conjugation C (antilinear, C^2 = 1, commuting with the Clifford action) exists and exchanges the summands, so the Majorana spinor R^16 is the complex space (2, 1) (x) 4. (ii) The 4 is the spinor fibre of K_5, the sheet W = H^2; under K_p = Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b it is (2, 1) + (1, 2), under Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b it is (2)_0 + (1)_{+1} + (1)_{-1}: one lepton generation with nu_R, with weak isospin Sp(1)_a and the T_3R circle; the quark generation is the same fibre tensored with C^3. (iii) The nine-dimensional mass bilinear vanishes (rs_F3), the masses are the internal Dirac operator on the fibre; a Majorana field has no U(1) charge and no Wilson line, so on A x S^1 its momenta lie in the dual lattice shifted by the spin structure; it admits the torsion of lemma 51 (C Gamma^{(3)} antisymmetric), and the mass operator on the sector (k, k_5) is c(k) + i c(eta) Gamma + k_5 Gamma with squared masses m_+-(k)^2 + k_5^2; the self-conjugate sector (k, k_5) = 0 carries exactly one generation, with the purely anti-quaternionic block i c(eta) Gamma and the equal masses |eta|, and a pair of sectors +-(k, k_5) != 0 carries a generation and a mirror, four Dirac fermions; a Majorana mass for nu_R alone is absent from Lorentz-invariant and constant bulk data (constant fluxes with a leg on S^1 give nu_R^c and e_R^c entries of equal modulus), wall data in that scope (Remark 44.4), and selects the KO-7 factorization for one generation (lemma 06, Lemma 4), with Dirac masses only both factorizations existing (Proposition 7 with R = 0).

*Scope.* Fibres at a point; no vacuum, gauge group or action constructed; the Wilson-line statement is the absence of a U(1) charge on a Majorana field.

**Proof.** Lemma 52, Proposition 52.2: Cl(1, 8) as the graded tensor product of Cl(1, 3) and Cl(0, 5); conj(4) = 4 and conj(2, 1) = (1, 2); B conj(B) = 1 in signature (1, 8), C symmetric, C Gamma^{(3)} antisymmetric, with the antilinear Majorana conjugation exchanging the gamma_5-eigenspaces; the real form projects isomorphically onto the first summand; Proposition 49.1 for the fibre; Proposition 51.1 for the spectrum.
