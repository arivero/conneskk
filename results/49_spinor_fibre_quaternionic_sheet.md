**Setting.** The flat four-torus A = a (x) R^2 of lemma 48 with the periodic spin structure; Sigma_4 = Sigma_+ + Sigma_-, Spin(4) = Sp(1)_+ x Sp(1)_-, the quaternionic structure j, the Clifford multiplication c(v) and the chirality Gamma; Gamma_a = c(e_a), Gamma_5 = Gamma; the lifted stabilizer Dic_3 = <a, x> of the orbit type (a, a, a).

**Statement.**

1. (49.1) (Sigma_4, Gamma_1, ..., Gamma_5, j) is the sheet W = H^2 of Example 16.9 with axis p = e_5: Gamma_p is the chirality, L_p and L_p^perp are the two chiralities, K_p = Spin(4), and the bidoublet Hom_H(L_p, L_p^perp) = T_p S^4 is the Clifford action of the tangent space of A, a Spin(4)-equivariant isomorphism R^4 -> Hom_H(Sigma_+, Sigma_-). The even algebra is End_H(Sigma_+) + End_H(Sigma_-) = H_R + H_L.
2. (49.2) The three-cycles act trivially on one chirality Sigma_R and by order three on the other Sigma_L; x acts with eigenvalues +-i on both. The real algebra generated by Dic_3 is C + H with C = span_R(1, x|_{Sigma_R}) acting as (lambda, conj(lambda)) on Sigma_R, the Cartan of H_R, and H = End_H(Sigma_L): the Standard Model subalgebra of the Pati-Salam pair. The circle of the lifted transposition is the T_3R torus of Sp(1)_R, the same for the three transpositions; it is lemma 42's U(1)_b exactly when the connector's line is one of its weight lines, and Corollary 46.4's torus exactly when the mass eigenlines are its weight lines.
3. (49.3) With x acting as L_i on both lines, the odd blocks commuting with x (the circle on both chiralities, whose span is the C_F of Chamseddine, Connes and Marcolli, their photon condition on D_F) are X = diag(y_1, y_2), complex dimension two, the Y_u, Y_d of the spectral Standard Model; none is invariant under the circle on Sigma_R alone; X = L_a + i L_c with a = (y_1 + conj(y_2))/2, c = (y_1 - conj(y_2))/(2i); the quaternionic ones have y_2 = conj(y_1). No nonzero odd block commutes with all of Dic_3.
4. (49.4) The stabilizers in S_3 of the points of A are S_3 (nine points), <tau> (mirror planes) and trivial; the lifted stabilizer algebras on Sigma_4 are C + H, C (the circle of x on both chiralities, the C_F restricted to the first sheet and the unbroken circle of Corollary 46.4) and R, with unitary groups U(1) x SU(2), U(1) and {+-1}. The Clifford element of a displacement along a mirror is a quaternionic block commuting with x.
5. (49.5) Corollary 16.8 with V_X = Sigma_4, A_{F,X} = C + H, gamma_X = Gamma (for the orientation of the Setting), a nonzero d_X, V_Y = C^3, M_3(C) gives a 24-state bimodule over C + H + M_3(C), the quark summand H_F^(3) of Chamseddine, Connes and Marcolli for one generation with the grading the chirality of A, with the KO-6 signs, order zero, order one, nonzero one-forms and the orientability relation, and the intersection form [[0, 0, 2], [0, 0, -2], [-2, 2, 0]] they print; the 18-state Hilbert space of Theorem 48.3 is the sub-bimodule on one weight line, preserved by D_F exactly when X vanishes on the other.

*Scope.* The flat A with the periodic spin structure; the convention on which chirality is Sigma_R depends on the orientation and the lift; the E factor and the inversion are outside; the motion of the point along A is a reading, the proved content being the stabilizer algebras.

**Proof.** Lemma 49, Propositions 49.1 to 49.5: Cl(4) with its chirality is Cl(5) on C^4; isoclinic rotations lie in one Sp(1) factor and d tau is a rotation by pi in one plane, so x has two imaginary components; the image of R[Dic_3] is its spinorial part C + H (lemma 48); a map commuting with L_i preserves its eigenlines; the three-cycles have no fixed vectors on Sigma_L; the stabilizers of points of A by the coordinates; Theorem 16.7 formally for the real subalgebra.

**What it leaves open.** A second independent reading. The lepton generation, which needs a connecting operator identifying the two copies of C (the order-one condition of Chamseddine, Connes and Marcolli, Proposition 2.11; the connector of lemma 20); the Dirac operator of the torus at a point of a mirror with a constant three-form term, the candidate that supplies both halves of an x-invariant Yukawa block (the anti-quaternionic half is i times the Clifford multiplication by a three-form, which no displacement supplies); the E factor, which doubles the fibre.
