# On a flat torus with a line bundle of degree p, the doubled Dirac operator with J(psi, chi) = (C chi, -C psi) has the signs of KO-dimension six, a kernel C^p + conj(C^p) below Landau levels of order (p/A)^{1/2}, and compressions of the functions generating M_p(C), so the window carries the unital finite datum M_p(C) + C

**Status.** Proposition (Proposition 7.3), with the unitization A = M_p(C) + C of the nonunital displayed action (ledger status conditional). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand, with Berezin-Toeplitz quantization of the torus recalled; proofs of items 1 and 2 and of the generation step by Terra 6.1 are written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 40. Ledger row `d11_torus_flux`, with prior work recorded as partial (the fuzzy torus is known; the KO-6 doubling with restricted algebra follows lemma 16). The section on the algebra and the table row on M_p(C) of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex ("\supp{40}").

**Setting.** T^2 a flat torus of area A with a spin structure, S_0 its flat spin line, L a line bundle of degree p >= 1 with a connection of constant curvature and L-bar = L^{-1}. H = L^2(S (x) L) + L^2(S (x) L-bar), D = D_L + D_{L-bar}, gamma = gamma_S on both summands, J(psi, chi) = (C chi, -C psi) with C the charge conjugation of the spin structure (antiunitary, C^2 = -1, C D = D C, C gamma_S = -gamma_S C), and C^infinity(T^2) acting by multiplication on the first summand.

**Statement.**

1. J^2 = 1, J D = D J and J gamma = -gamma J, the signs of KO-dimension six, and the algebra satisfies order zero.
2. ker D = H^0(L (x) S_0) + H^1(L^{-1} (x) S_0), isomorphic to C^p + conj(C^p), with gamma = +1 and -1 on the two summands; the nonzero levels are sqrt(2 abs(B) n), n >= 1, with B = 2 pi p/A, so they go to infinity as A -> 0 at fixed p.
3. The compressions of the characters to the lowest Landau level are nonzero Gaussian multiples of clock-and-shift operators U^m V^n with U V = e^{+-2 pi i/p} V U, and these span M_p(C).
4. With the algebra M_p(C) + C represented by rho(a, lambda) = a on C^p and lambda on conj(C^p), the opposite action J rho(b, mu)^\* J^{-1} is mu on C^p and b^T on conj(C^p), which commutes with rho; this is a unital finite KO-6 datum with D = 0 and order zero.

*Scope.* At p = 3 the window has the form of the colour sector, M_3(C) on the quarks and its opposite on the antiquarks. The representation generated by the functions alone is degenerate on the second summand (40_counterexample_nonunital_torus_window.md), and the compression map is not multiplicative on the function algebra.

**Proof.** Lemma 40, Proposition 7.3, and the section "Further proofs" there: Riemann-Roch in genus one and Serre duality give the kernels; with the position split into guiding-centre and oscillator parts, P e^{ik.x} P = e^{-abs(k)^2/(4 abs(B))} e^{ik.R} P; tr(U^m V^n) = 0 unless m = n = 0 mod p, so the p^2 products are orthogonal.

**What it leaves open.** The quaternionic factor H from a torus with even flux and a real structure of quaternionic type, and the full A_F from several tori or a rank-three bundle with flux. The reading for duality 1, with p counting IIB momentum on the nine-dimensional circle and SL(2, Z) acting by Morita equivalence, is a proposal, and whether p = 3 for colour has a reason there is open.
