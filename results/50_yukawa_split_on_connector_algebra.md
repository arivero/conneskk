# A sheet-diagonal Yukawa block split along projections of the second sheet keeps order one exactly when the projections commute with the second-sheet algebra: universal on the Pati-Salam M_4(C), with the lepton Yukawas equal to the quark ones, and separable exactly on the connector's algebra C + M_3(C), with the lepton line the connector's line

**Status.** Proposition 50.3. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 5 November 2026 by hand. The top-only block alone keeps order one on H_R + H_L + C + M_3(C), of real dimension 28; the connector reduces the sum to A_SM. Krajewski's first-order rule is the general criterion; the split block lies outside the compressed form d_X (x) 1. Ledger row `l50_split_after_connector`. Not yet in a paper.

**Setting.** The data of Corollary 16.8 with V_X = W, A_X an even algebra containing the grading Gamma_p, V_Y = V = C^4 with A_Y in M_4(C); projections P_1, ..., P_r on V summing to 1 and odd Hermitian blocks Y_i on W; D x = sum Y_i x P_i, D y = sum P_i y Y_i.

**Statement.** D is self-adjoint, odd, J_F-invariant and of order zero, with order-one defect sum_i [Y_i, a_X] x [b_Y, P_i]. (1) Equal Y_i: order one for every A_Y. (2) P_i commuting with A_Y: order one for every Y_i. (3) Linearly independent Y_i: order one exactly when every P_i commutes with A_Y; for Y_l (x) P_v + Y_q (x) (1 - P_v), exactly when Y_l = Y_q or [A_Y, P_v] = 0. (4) On A_Y = M_4(C) the block is universal: the Dirac neutrino and the up quark, the charged lepton and the down quark, share their masses in each generation (the unified Yukawas k^nu = k^u, k^e = k^d of the spectral Pati-Salam model); the commutant of P_v in M_4(C) is C + M_3(C), the second-sheet algebra of a connector on v (Theorem 20.2), on which the split block keeps order one. (5) If a split block on a line v' and a connector on v both keep order one with colour End(v^perp) retained, then Cv' = Cv. In general, with the grading in A_X, order one holds exactly when D commutes with the right action of A_Y (Krajewski's rule). So the top-only family of lemma 21 (Y_l = 0, Y_q = {c(h), P_w} (x) P_g) is a sheet-diagonal operator on the bimodule of Corollary 16.8, outside its compressed form d_X (x) 1, with order one exactly when the second-sheet algebra lies in the commutant C + M_3(C) of P_v, all of H_R + H_L being allowed on the first sheet; with the connector on the same line added, order one of the sum holds exactly on A(T_kappa) = C + H + M_3(C).

*Scope.* Sheet-diagonal operators with projections on the second sheet; the three-generation form is the same computation.

**Proof.** Lemma 50, Proposition 50.3: block computation of [[D, a], b°]; with a_X = Gamma_p the first factors -2 Gamma_p Y_i are independent; the commutant of a rank-one projection in M_4(C); a rank-one projection commuting with End(v^perp) vanishes on v^perp.

**Prior work.** Krajewski (arXiv:hep-th/9701081, sections 3.4, 4.1 and 4.2): the first-order rule of which the criterion is a case; Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli, Theorem 2.21; Chamseddine and Connes (arXiv:0706.3688, section 4); Chamseddine, Connes and van Suijlekom (arXiv:1304.8050, sections 4, 5 and 8): the unified case k^nu = k^u, k^e = k^d, the decoupling of Sigma, and the Standard Model vacuum aligning Delta, Sigma and phi on the lines of Propositions 50.1 and 50.3; the forcing of the shared lepton line (item 5) was not found (paper/LITERATURE.md, section P).
