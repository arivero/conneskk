# On the interval with D_F = eps D_\* and length l_\* eps^{-alpha}, the finite data stay exact in a window of r = dim H_F states below E_\* eps^beta, while the tower gap closes as eps^alpha and the count at fixed energy grows as eps^{-alpha}

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 30.1), with Corollaries 30.2 and 30.4. Proposed in an audit of commit b2ddf98 by Astra 6 (historical/reviews/2026-11-04_outside_audit_assessment.md), with the proofs redone by Claude Opus 5.5 against the source lemmas; checked by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand and by Terra 6.1 by hand, in a report on lemma 30 together with the joint-limit note, whose corrections are applied and have not been read again (historical/reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_30_note.md). Ledger rows `l30_interval` (depending on `l22_family`), `l30_poles` and `l30_cylinder` (both depending on `l30_interval`). Theorem 1 of paper/joint_limit_note.tex, with the vector poles and the cylinder stated after it ("\supp{30}").

**Setting.** (H_F, D_\*, J_F, Gamma_F) satisfying the hypotheses of Theorem 22.1, with A_F represented by pi as in Theorem 22.4 and r = dim H_F. For 0 < alpha < 1, l_\* > 0 and small eps > 0, D_F(eps) = eps D_\*, l_eps = l_\* eps^{-alpha}, and D_eps is the interval operator D_X of Theorem 22.1 for these data. Delta_eps is the smallest element of spec |D_eps| outside spec |eps D_\*|, and N_eps(E) = #{|D_eps| <= E}.

**Statement.** For eps small:

1. W_eps\* D_eps W_eps = eps D_\*, with J_F and Gamma_F exact.
2. Delta_eps = (pi/l_\*) eps^alpha - eps ||D_\*||.
3. ||Q_eps(a) - pi(a)|| <= (2/3) l_\*^2 ||D_\*||^2 eps^{2(1 - alpha)} ||a||.
4. For alpha < beta < 1, E_\* > 0 and E_eps = E_\* eps^beta, N_eps(E_eps) = r.
5. For fixed E > 0, N_eps(E) ~ (2 r l_\* E/pi) eps^{-alpha}.
6. (Corollary 30.2) In the five-dimensional Yang-Mills toy of lemma 23, with universal mass m_eps = eps m_\* and theta_eps = l_eps m_eps -> 0, M_W = arcsin(sin(2 theta_eps)/sqrt(2))/l_eps and M_Z = arcsin(2 sin(2 theta_eps)/sqrt(5))/l_eps, so M_W^2 = 2 m_eps^2 (1 + O(theta_eps^2)) and M_Z^2 = (16/5) m_eps^2 (1 + O(theta_eps^2)), and the vector poles follow the hierarchy.
7. (Corollary 30.4) On the cylinder of lemma 24, with finite data on V = C^4 with gradings of type (2, 2), so r = 4, and r_eps = r_\* eps^{-gamma}, 0 < gamma < 1: for max(alpha, gamma) < beta < 1 the cutoff E_\* eps^beta isolates the window, the next level being at least min((Delta_K/r_\*) eps^gamma, (pi/l_\*) eps^alpha - eps ||D_\*||), and at fixed E the count is proportional to l_eps r_eps^6 E^7, a seven-dimensional Weyl growth, by Dirichlet and Neumann bracketing. For gamma = alpha all seven directions expand homothetically.

*Scope.* These families have the hierarchy m_weak << E_eps << Delta_KK -> 0, which alone does not make the count at fixed energy diverge (the signed spectrum 0, +-eps^alpha, +-1, +-2, ... has it with a bounded count), so items 5 and 7 are proved separately. The finite data are supplied, the parent algebra is matrix-valued, and the wall data change with eps by hand; a fixed Majorana block does not scale as eps D_\*, so an application to an electroweak Dirac block needs a reducing sector or a treatment of the heavy neutral sector. The constants 2 and 16/5 belong to the five-dimensional action, and the small-mass pole coefficients of the fully massive finite spectral action are 3 and 24/5; lemma 25 connects the two pairs at the level of the leading coefficient at fixed s = l Lambda, and a joint-limit use needs a remainder uniform in s, since s -> 0 along Lambda = E_eps and s -> infinity at fixed Lambda. Varying the cutoff of the action is an extra prescription. In item 7 the window is prescribed by the spectral walls of lemma 24, and other values of r need an enlarged tangential bundle, which is not constructed.

**Proof.** Lemma 30, Theorem 30.1 and Corollaries 30.2 and 30.4; the proofs are there. l_eps ||D_F(eps)|| = l_\* ||D_\*|| eps^{1 - alpha} < pi/2 for small eps, so Theorems 22.1 and 22.4 (2) apply; eps ||D_\*|| << eps^beta << eps^alpha; the signed spectrum (n pi +- theta_k)/l_eps consists of r arithmetic progressions of spacing pi/l_eps. Corollary 30.2 is Theorem 23.6, and Corollary 30.4 uses bound (5) of lemma 24.

**What it leaves open.** No dynamics selects these families (lemma 30, Remark 30.8). A window that comes from a local operator, with seven-dimensional growth, is the disc bundle (30_joint_limit_disc_bundle.md), and the localized interval family is 30_localized_family.md.
