Near roundness, the change in $B,L,a$ induced by the two fixed gauge norms and fixed meridian length has logarithmic derivative $O(\epsilon)$ in $x$. One-loop continuous decoupling into the six-flavour colour theory gives $\partial_x\log\Lambda_6=\mathcal D/(4b_6)$, $b_6=7$. Corrections include higher-order matching and the mass anomalous dimensions when moving between a common renormalization convention and masses at their running thresholds. **Condition for stationarity in the shape direction.** In this leading model a stationary $\Xi$ in $x$ would require
$$
 \mathcal D_*=7\,\frac{2-1/\rho-F_N}{1-F_N}\simeq7.09 .
$$
In the custodial limit $\rho=1$ this is exactly seven independently of the sigma terms. If every extra threshold scales with the same power as the ordinary quark links, $\mathcal D$ is the total removed matter contribution. At $\rho=1$ stationarity then corresponds to a vanishing high-energy one-loop colour coefficient. At the fitted $\rho>1$ it would place that coefficient slightly on the infrared-free side. This is a conditional relation between a specified infrared derivative and threshold dynamics. It makes no assumption that Nature imposes stationarity. Thresholds with other mass exponents, a moving strong boundary condition or genuinely interacting nonperturbative degrees of freedom change the criterion.
