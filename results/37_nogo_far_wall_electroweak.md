# In the chamber 15 + 1 with the top's brane on the far wall, the D0 states massless there at infinite coupling satisfy M_D0 R = R^2 - R_\*^2, so W bosons of this kind are massless at the string-scale radius and grow heavier as R grows, and a neutral Z cannot be of this kind

**Status.** No-go theorem in its scope (the section on the far-wall electroweak group of lemma 37, unnumbered, with no ledger row of its own). Derived by Claude Opus 5.5 on 4 November 2026 from equations (windingmass) and (D0mass) of Bergman, Gaberdiel and Lifschytz (hep-th/9711098, section 3, read from the arXiv LaTeX). The equation is the full D0 mass in the heterotic frame; the enhancement states with heterotic winding +-1 contain half a D0-brane, so for them M_{W,h} R_h = (1/2)(R_h^2 - 7/4), with the same growth; the identification with the W stays conditional. Stated in the section on the string dualities of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex among the negative statements in the scope of that dictionary.

**Setting.** The chamber 15 + 1 of 37_radius_fifteen_plus_one.md with n = 1 brane, the top's, at the strong far wall, placed at x^9 = 0 by reflecting the interval; N' = 8 - n, A = (B + 2 pi N')^{2/3}, b = B^{2/3}, alpha' = 2. Remark 8.3 of lemma 35 places SU(2) x U(1) at this wall at infinite coupling, conditional on the recalled type I' rules.

**Statement.**

1. M_D0 R = (N'/2) b/(A - b) = R^2 - R_\*^2, which vanishes at B = 0, where R = R_\*, and grows like R^2; for the enhancement states with heterotic winding +-1, which contain half a D0-brane, M_{W,h} R_h = (1/2)(R_h^2 - 7/4).
2. Only W^+ and W^- can be such states: a neutral Z is a Cartan combination, and the rank of a nine-dimensional theory with sixteen supercharges is fixed, so no massive neutral vector arises this way.
3. If the W^+- are these D0 states, they are massless at the string-scale radius with the tower fixed, and M_W l rises with l, the direction opposite to l ~ 1/M_W. So on one path, in this scope, the dictionary can place the electroweak group at the far wall, or the opening of H on the circle of the SO(32) Wilson line, and not both.

*Scope.* The chamber 15 + 1, the classical background and the identification of the W with the D0 states at the wall. This is the fixed-tower path of the Astra 6 audit (its section 2.2), on which masslessness opens no dimension.

*Escapes, as the lemma states them.* Other chambers; the W as a Wilson-line string between D8-branes, as in lemma 23; the top's brane in the bulk, with the far wall giving E_1.

**Proof.** Lemma 37, section on the far-wall electroweak group: the winding mass and the D0 mass of Bergman, Gaberdiel and Lifschytz combined, with R_\* of 37_radius_fifteen_plus_one.md.

**What it leaves open.** The result points to the escapes above. The physical status of the far-wall group itself rests on the recalled enhancement rule (Remark 8.3, ledger row `d11_e_walls`, conjectural), which has no file.
