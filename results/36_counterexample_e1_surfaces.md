# The two del Pezzo surfaces of degree eight have different lattices orthogonal to the canonical class: P^1 x P^1 has a class of square -2 there and dP_1 has none, so the empty far wall with E_1 cannot be assigned to either surface indifferently

**Status.** Counterexample, to the unqualified identification "dP_1 or P^1 x P^1" for the empty far wall, a recalled proposal (ledger row `d11_dp_walls`, kind conjecture). Lemma 36, section 5, item 1, now reads: the empty far wall E_1 corresponds to P^1 x P^1, while dP_1 = F_1 gives the distinct E~_1 theory. Constructed by Terra 6.1 and written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 36. No paper cites it.

**Setting.** dP_1 = F_1 with H^2 = 1, e^2 = -1, H . e = 0 and K = -3H + e; P^1 x P^1 with ruling classes R_1, R_2, R_1^2 = R_2^2 = 0, R_1 . R_2 = 1 and K = -2R_1 - 2R_2; dP_2 with exceptional classes e_1, e_2 and K = -3H + e_1 + e_2.

**Statement.**

1. On dP_1 a class D = aH + b e orthogonal to K has b = -3a and D^2 = -8 a^2, so there is no integral class of square -2.
2. On P^1 x P^1 the class R_1 - R_2 is orthogonal to K and has square -2.
3. On dP_2 the class e_1 - e_2 is orthogonal to K with square -2, and the orthogonal lattice has rank two, consistent with an A_1 factor and an additional abelian direction.

*Scope.* In the usual local engineering (recalled), P^1 x P^1 gives E_1 and dP_1 gives the distinct theory E-tilde_1. The computation constructs neither the five-dimensional field theory nor the type I' duality, and the map from branes to geometry stays conditional on the cited dualities, whose primary sources were not available for this computation.

**Proof.** Lemma 36, section "Further proofs", Counterexample to the unqualified E_1 surface identification: D . K = -3a - b = 0 and D^2 = a^2 - b^2.

**What it leaves open.** The local dP_2 of the wall with the top's brane, and its relation to the Witten base CP^2 x CP^1; the occurrence of CP^2 and CP^1 in that six-dimensional base does not by itself identify the compactification with a local dP_2.
