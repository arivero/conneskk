# The two del Pezzo surfaces of degree eight have different lattices orthogonal to the canonical class: P^1 x P^1 has a class of square -2 there and dP_1 has none, so the empty far wall with E_1 cannot be assigned to either surface indifferently

**Status.** Counterexample, to the unqualified identification "dP_1 or P^1 x P^1" for the empty far wall in section 5, item 1, of lemma 36, a recalled proposal (ledger row `d11_dp_walls`, kind conjecture, with the referee amendment). Constructed by Terra 6.1 in the referee report on the 11D line (historical/reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_11d.md, section on the del Pezzo wall correspondence), written out in the Referee proofs of lemma 36; the ledger row records checks by hand by Terra 6.1 and Claude Sonnet 5.5. No paper cites it.

**Setting.** dP_1 = F_1 with H^2 = 1, E^2 = -1, H . E = 0 and K = -3H + E; P^1 x P^1 with ruling classes F_1, F_2, F_1^2 = F_2^2 = 0, F_1 . F_2 = 1 and K = -2F_1 - 2F_2; dP_2 with exceptional classes E_1, E_2 and K = -3H + E_1 + E_2.

**Statement.**

1. On dP_1 a class D = aH + bE orthogonal to K has b = -3a and D^2 = -8 a^2, so there is no integral class of square -2.
2. On P^1 x P^1 the class F_1 - F_2 is orthogonal to K and has square -2.
3. On dP_2 the class E_1 - E_2 is orthogonal to K with square -2, and the orthogonal lattice has rank two, consistent with an A_1 factor and an additional abelian direction.

*Scope.* In the usual local engineering (recalled), P^1 x P^1 gives E_1 and dP_1 gives the distinct theory E-tilde_1. The computation constructs neither the five-dimensional field theory nor the type I' duality, and the map from branes to geometry stays conditional on the cited dualities, whose primary sources were unavailable to the referee.

**Proof.** Lemma 36, Referee proofs, Counterexample (referee) to the unqualified E_1 surface identification: D . K = -3a - b = 0 and D^2 = a^2 - b^2.

**What it leaves open.** The local dP_2 of the wall with the top's brane, and its relation to the Witten base CP^2 x CP^1; the occurrence of CP^2 and CP^1 in that six-dimensional base does not by itself identify the compactification with a local dP_2.
