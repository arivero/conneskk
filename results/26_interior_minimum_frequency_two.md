# In the flat family with pure frequency-1 and frequency-2 channels at one loop, an interior global minimum exists for n_2 >= n_c = 2.8855..., at theta = pi/4 with M_KK = 4 M_W when the frequency-2 channels act alone, and in (pi/4, pi/2) when 0 < n_1 < n_\* = (4/3)(4 n_2 - 54/5)

**Status.** Theorem (Theorem 26.2, with Lemma 26.1), with Proposition 26.2b and Corollary 26.3. Theorem 26.2 and Corollary 26.3 proved by Claude Opus 5.5, Proposition 26.2b by Terra 6.1, the referee. Theorem 26.2 checked by Claude Opus 5.5 in Python (11600 pairs, n_2 from 3 to 60 and n_1 from 0 to 199, on 20001 angles, no failure) and by Terra 6.1 by hand; Corollary 26.3 by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand and by Terra 6.1 in Python (independent roots and table rows); Proposition 26.2b by Claude Opus 5.5 by hand. The first report on lemma 26 found three errors and two gaps as first written, and the second one error in the summary, all corrected (historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_26.md, historical/reviews/2026-11-03_terra_check_27.md). Cited in the section on the gauge sector of paper/boundary_condition_note.tex ("\supp{26}").

**Setting.** The flat interval with the gauge content, couplings and connector wall of Theorem 23.6. A fermion channel is pure of frequency q when its two wall gradings differ by a rotation through q theta; its spectrum is +-(n pi + q theta)/l and its tower product 1 + sin^2(q theta)/sinh^2 x. E = Hom(V, W) is pure with q = 1. n_q >= 0 is twice the number of Dirac species in such channels, each colour counted once. With t = sin^2(theta), u = t(1 - t) and F, J as in Results 8 and 9 of lemma 24, assuming precisely these towers and multiplicities,

    8 pi^2 l^4 V = 3 F(2u) + (3/2) F((16/5) u) - n_1 F(t) - n_2 F(4u).

**Statement.**

1. (Lemma 26.1) R(u) = [6 J(2u) + (24/5) J((16/5) u)]/(4 J(4u)) is strictly increasing on (0, 1/4], with R(0+) = 27/10 and R(1/4) = n_c = 2.8855033... (numerical quadrature).
2. (n_1 = 0) If n_2 <= 27/10, theta = 0 and pi/2 are the global minima, degenerate at n_2 = 27/10, and pi/4 is the maximum. If 27/10 < n_2 < n_c, there are exactly two global minima, at theta_\* and pi/2 - theta_\*, theta_\* in (0, pi/4). If n_2 >= n_c, theta = pi/4 is the unique global minimum.
3. (n_1 > 0, n_2 >= n_c) If n_1 < n_\*, there is exactly one stationary point in the open interval, the strict global minimum, in (pi/4, pi/2); if n_1 >= n_\*, V decreases strictly and theta = pi/2 is the unique global minimum. The range n_1 > 0, n_2 < n_c is not classified.
4. (Proposition 26.2b) For n_1 > 0 and 0 < t < 1/2, V(1 - t) - V(t) = -n_1 [F(1 - t) - F(t)] < 0, so no point of (0, pi/4) is a global minimum; with item 2 this covers all integer counts.
5. (Corollary 26.3) At theta = pi/4, M_KK = pi/l = 4 M_W, m_1 = M_W, m_2 = 2 M_W, tan(M_Z l) = 2 and M_W^2/M_Z^2 = 0.5032. From pi/4 to pi/2, m_1/M_W increases from 1 to infinity and m_2/M_W decreases strictly from 2 to sqrt(2); M_KK >= 4 M_W for all theta in (0, pi/2).

*Scope.* One interval, flat profiles, this gauge content, one loop, no bulk masses, no wall terms. With M_W = 80.36 GeV the minimum at pi/4 has M_KK = 321 GeV, and a frequency-1 top with m_1/M_W = 2 needs theta = 1.13774, on the line n_1 = 2.71387 n_2 - 7.71725, with M_KK = 444 GeV (Python, recomputed by the referee). For n_1 > 0 and n_2 = 1, 2 a scan to n_1 = 200 (Python) finds no interior local minimum, which is not proved, and metastable minima at small angle in the cases left to scans are not excluded. A sector with tower product 1 + sin^2(2 theta)/(2 sinh^2 x), counted by n_d >= 27/5, also makes theta = pi/4 the unique global minimum at n_1 = 0.

**Proof.** Lemma 26, Lemma 26.1, Theorem 26.2, Proposition 26.2b and Corollary 26.3; the proofs are there. For n_1 = 0, dV/du = 4 J(4u) (R(u) - n_2); for n_1 > 0 and t > 1/2, dV/dt = J(t) [Psi(t) - n_1] with Psi increasing strictly from 0 to n_\*. Lemma 26.1 uses Lemma 8 of lemma 24 with the strict covariance argument of the referee amendment there.

**What it leaves open.** Frequency-2 channels live in multiplets beyond E = (4, 4bar), such as the (10, 6) or the (5, 15), whose displayed channels carry light coloured states (26_wall_mass_light_coloured_state.md). The potential, the wall-mass spectrum, the wall kinetic term and the running of lemma 26 use different simplified spectra and have not been combined into one model (lemma 26, Outstanding), so T13 stays open; priority for the classification with n_c is open.
