# For the core copy of the 4 in the disc-bundle window, every bounded real observable compresses to within 2 eta_t of a quaternionic operator and products of k of them to within 2 k eta_t, and the defect eta_t is O(M^{-1/2}) in general and exponentially small in M when the quaternionic structure of E is parallel

**Status.** Propositions (Propositions 30.6 and 30.7). Proposition 30.6 was proposed in the Astra 6 audit of commit b2ddf98 (historical/reviews/2026-11-04_outside_audit_assessment.md), with the proof redone by Claude Opus 5.5; Proposition 30.7 is by Claude Opus 5.5, new in lemma 30. **Setting.** The window of Theorem 29.7 or 29.11. For its copy of the 4 at the core, B(r) = F(r)\* F(r) = diag(b_a I_2, b_+, b_-) as in Proposition 28.16, j is the quaternionic antiunitary commuting with Sp(2), P_H(A) = (A + j A j^-1)/2, Q is the normalized compression of this mode, and eta_t = int |b_+(r) - b_-(r)| dr / int tr B(r) dr.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 30.6) ||Q(f) - P_H Q(f)|| <= 2 eta_t for every real f with ||f||_infinity <= 1.
2. If X_1, ..., X_k are such compressions, ||X_1 ... X_k - P_H(X_1 ... X_k)|| <= 2 k eta_t.
3. (Proposition 30.7) eta_t = O(M^{-1/2}).
4. If the quaternionic structure j_E of E is parallel on all of X, as for the pull-back connection and for the real affine interpolation (1 - s(r)) nabla^pull + s(r) nabla^line, then for every eta in (0, 1) there are M_eta and C_eta with eta_t <= C_eta exp(-(1 - eta) M int_{2e}^{l - a} f dr) for M >= M_eta, the exponent of Proposition 29.8 doubled, with the integral starting at 2e.

*Scope.* Bounded expressions become quaternionic as eta_t -> 0, while an unrestricted generated algebra may use coefficients of size 1/eta_t and retain the other directions, as in Counterexample 28.17. The parallel hypothesis can fail for other invariant interpolations allowed by (H3). A bound in terms of lambda itself would need a lower bound on lambda, which is open. Under the added hypothesis the defect comes from the wall, which breaks C = C_7 (x) j_E (Proposition 28.18); for an operator with the same interior and no wall, on a complete manifold, Hom_G(4, ker T) carries a real structure, and if it is one-dimensional the core block is exactly quaternionic. The audit samples (Python, eta_t/lambda^2 between 0.5 and 0.9, rows reproduced by Claude Sonnet 5.5) vary the length at fixed mass with profiles outside Setting 29.0, so they are a qualitative comparison that does not test item 4.

**Proof.** Lemma 30, Propositions 30.6 and 30.7; the proofs are there. Schur's lemma writes Q(f) as an average of lambda(g)^-1 B(r) lambda(g), j exchanges b_+ and b_-, so ||B - P_H B|| = |b_+ - b_-|/2, and telescoping gives the product bound. For item 4, C commutes with T_M when j_E is parallel, F -> C F j^-1 is a real structure on the one-dimensional Hom_G(4, ker T_0), and zeta = u - C u satisfies T_M zeta = lambda nu in the interior with Pi(chi_c zeta) = 0, so the Agmon weights of Proposition 29.8 and a weighted Cauchy-Schwarz inequality give the doubled exponent.

**What it leaves open.** The exact generated algebra at finite mass, and a lower bound on lambda. The wall block generates M_4(C), which needs the loss of the inherited symmetry at the wall or a different real correspondence (Proposition 28.18).
