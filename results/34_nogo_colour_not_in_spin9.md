# The colour SU(3) of the root table lies in no Spin(9) in SO(16) with 128 -> 44 + 84, so the split 44 + 84 of the four-family spinor is not colour-covariant and the fit 44 = 32 + 12 is numerical

**Status.** No-go theorem in its scope (Reading 3b, part (b), of lemma 34: a proved statement inside a reading, after an observation of the user in part (a)). Proved by Claude Opus 5.5 on 4 November 2026 by hand. Ledger row `d11_spin9_colour` (depending on `d11_colour84`). Proposition 12 of paper/generations_note.tex, with the remark after it on the so(9) of e8.

**Setting.** Four families: the 128 half-integer roots of E8, the chiral spinor of SO(16), with colour the regular SU(3) of the root table, under which the vector 16 of SO(16) is 3 + 3-bar + 10 (1). Spin(9) is embedded in SO(16) through its spinor, in the class with 128 -> 44 + 84, the graviton and the three-form of eleven-dimensional supergravity (recalled; the other chirality of the embedding gives an irreducible 128).

**Statement.**

1. Under such a Spin(9) the vector 16 of SO(16) is the spinor of Spin(9), which restricted to an SU(3) inside Spin(9) is 16 (1), 2 (3 + 3-bar) + 4 (1) or 8 + 8, by the three real nine-dimensional representations of 39_nogo_three_form_colour.md. So colour lies in no such Spin(9).
2. The stabilizer of the split 44 + 84 in so(16) is spin(9), so the split is not colour-covariant, and no set of 84 roots spans the 84.
3. Colour, with 248 = 8 + 78 (1) + 27 (3 + 3-bar), lies in no so(9) of e8 with 248 = 36 + 44 + 84 + 84, that of 33_e8_two_gradings_placement.md included, since the 44 contains a 6 or a 27 under any SU(3) in such an so(9).

*Scope.* The regular colour SU(3) of the root table, in this class of Spin(9).

*Escapes, as the lemma states them.* Other colour embeddings; identifications that are not equivariant.

**Proof.** Lemma 34, Reading 3b (b): the restrictions in item 1 differ from 3 + 3-bar + 10 (1); a subalgebra between spin(9) and so(16) would be a submodule of 36 + 84, and so(16) is irreducible on the 128; a partition of roots has the rank-8 Cartan subalgebra in its stabilizer.

**What it leaves open.** With four families 128 - 84 = 44 = 32 + 12, so removing either family of 84 leaves the fourth family with the top block or with the neutrino block (part (a)); M of 34_colour_family_transposition.md carries neither set of 44 onto the other, and no isometry does (34_nogo_44_sets_not_isometric.md). How the 96 states of the three-family frame sit against the Spin(9) conjugate to the so(9) of e8 (34_spin9_conjugate_so9.md) has not been worked out.
