# Under SU(16) x U(1) the complex adjoint of SO(32) is 120 + 120-bar + 255 + 1, and under SU(5) x SU(3) the 105 in the 120 is (15, 3-bar) + (10, 6), whose antitriplet has the charge counts (6, 6, 3)

**Status.** Proposition (section 1 of lemma 36). Stated by Claude Opus 5.5; a proof by Terra 6.1 is written out in the section "Further proofs" of lemma 36. Ledger rows `d11_so32_algebra` (depending on `d11_diquark_strings`) and `d11_so32_comparison`, the comparison with the EPJC paper read from its LaTeX source by Claude Sonnet 5.5, with prior work recorded as known. Cited in the introduction of paper/real_structure_note.tex ("\supp{36}, section 1") and in the introduction of paper/generations_note.tex ("\supp{36}, Section 1").

**Setting.** 32_C = 16_{+1} + 16-bar_{-1} under U(16) in SO(32); 16 = 15 + 1 under SU(15), and 15 = 5 (x) 3 under SU(5) x SU(3).

**Statement.**

1. so(32)_C = Lambda^2 32 = 120_{+2} + 120-bar_{-2} + 255_0 + 1_0.
2. Under SU(15), 120 = 105 + 15, and 105 = Lambda^2(5 (x) 3) = (15, 3-bar) + (10, 6), with the antitriplet charge counts of 36_diquark_counts_stack_image.md.
3. 15 (x) 15-bar = (24, 8) + (24, 1) + (1, 8) + (1, 1).

*Scope.* Lie-algebra shorthand; global embeddings involve finite central quotients. Matching representations does not identify physical composites, and assigning the colour-neutral SO(2) plane to a top brane is a label of the proposed dictionary.

**Proof.** Lemma 36, section "Further proofs", the branching of section 1: Lambda^2(16 + 16-bar) = Lambda^2 16 + Lambda^2 16-bar + 16 (x) 16-bar, with the plethysm of Proposition 9.2.

**What it leaves open.** The comparison of section 1 with A. Rivero, Eur. Phys. J.
