# For a marked complex line l in the quaternionic sheet W = H^2 with quaternionic line L over p in S^4, T_L S^4 = (2, 2) = Hom_H(L, L^perp) and T_l CP^3 = (2)_{+-1} + (1)_{+-2} under Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b: the Higgs doublet of hypercharge 1/2 along the base and a charged singlet of hypercharge 1 along the twistor fibre

**Status.** Proposition (Proposition 42.2). Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 4 November 2026 by hand; the representation theory is standard and agrees with the isotropy representation stated in lemma 28. Ledger row `l42_tangent`. The table row on the Higgs doublet of paper/connes_kk_dictionary_note.tex.

**Setting.** G = Sp(2) acting on W = H^2 = C^4; K = Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b is the stabilizer of a quaternionic line L, with Sp(1)_b acting on L and Sp(1)_a on L^perp, and H = Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b the stabilizer of a complex line l in L, so G/K = S^4, G/H = CP^3 and K/H = S^2 is the twistor fibre (lemma 28). U(1)_b charges are normalized to +-1 on the doublet of Sp(1)_b, and Y = charge/2 as in the first-stage note of the corpus.

**Statement.**

1. The G-orbit of l = Cw is CP^3 = G/H and that of L = l + jl is S^4 = G/K.
2. T_L S^4 is the (2, 2) of Sp(1)_a x Sp(1)_b, identified with Hom_H(L, L^perp) with the action t -> q_a t q_b^{-1}.
3. T_l CP^3 = (2)_{+-1} + (1)_{+-2} under Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b, where (2)_{+-1} is the horizontal part, the restriction of T_L S^4, one complex doublet of the weak Sp(1)_a with hypercharge 1/2, and (1)_{+-2} is the tangent of the twistor fibre at l, one complex singlet of hypercharge 1 and electric charge +1.

*Scope.* The window of lemma 29 with its Sp(2)-equivariance, the connector of Theorem 20.2 as a marked pair of lines and a coupling, and the normalization Y = charge/2. Nothing here makes the marked lines dynamical or computes a potential.

**Proof.** Lemma 42, Proposition 42.2: sp(2) = sp(1)_a + sp(1)_b + m with m = (2, 2), the isotropy representation of S^4 (so(5) under so(4)); sp(1)_b = u(1)_b + (1)_{+-2} under its torus; and (2, 2) restricted to Sp(1)_a x U(1)_b is (2)_{+1} + (2)_{-1}.

**What it leaves open.** Whether the fibre direction (1)_{+-2} is a dynamical scalar, a gauge direction of U(1)_b, or frozen by the collar connection of lemma 29, which preserves the line summands; this is the question of the grading axis of Proposition 28.19, which marks L. A parent in which the lines of the connector vary along S^4, a Higgs field as a section over the base; lemma 43 covers horizontal changes of the connection and leaves the kernel-moving collar connection open.
