# Along D_t = t T_R + t^alpha V_nu + S, the algebra retained by bounded commutators is C + H + M_3(C) for a bounded Dirac coupling (alpha = 0) and C + M_3(C) for a growing one (alpha > 0), so a finite seesaw mass and the Standard Model algebra belong to two different limits of the family

**Status.** Proposition (Proposition 44.1), with Corollary 44.3. Proved by Claude Fable 5.1 on 4 November 2026 by hand; checked by its author by hand. No independent check yet: the ledger marks the row referee_pending. Ledger row `l44_algebra` (depending on `l20_rank_one`). No paper cites it.

**Setting.** One generation in the representation of lemma 06, Result 5, with the Pati-Salam algebra A_PS = H_R + H_L + M_4(C) and J(p, p-bar) = (K p-bar, K p). T_R = |nu_R><J nu_R| + |J nu_R><nu_R| is the Majorana block, of rank two, J-real and odd; V_nu = y (|nu_L><nu_R| + |nu_R><nu_L|) plus its conjugate is the neutrino Dirac block; S collects the remaining Yukawa blocks. The family is D_t = t T_R + t^alpha V_nu + S with t -> infinity and 0 <= alpha < 1, and the retained algebra is the set of a in A_PS with sup_t norm([D_t, rho(a)]) finite, the bounded-commutator criterion behind Theorem 20.2 and lemma 06.

**Statement.**

1. (Proposition 44.1) The retained algebra is A_PS cap {T_R}' cap {V_nu}' for alpha > 0, and A_PS cap {T_R}' = C + H + M_3(C) for alpha = 0.
2. For alpha > 0 it equals {(lambda, Q_lambda, m)} = C + M_3(C), the algebra of colour and electromagnetism: the centralizer of the top block of lemma 06 and the commutant C_F of lemma 22 for the Standard Model data.
3. (Corollary 44.3) The algebra C + H + M_3(C) needs alpha = 0, along which the light neutrino mass is -y^2/t -> 0, and a finite light mass needs alpha = 1/2, along which the retained algebra is C + M_3(C) (44_seesaw_light_mass.md). The two coincide only at finite t, where all commutators are bounded by t^{1/2} y, so the seesaw limit at fixed light mass is a limit of the broken theory, in the sense of Proposition 5 of lemma 06 (06_heavy_limit_ko7_only.md).

*Scope.* One generation, the representation of lemma 06 and the strict limit t -> infinity. With three generations y becomes the Dirac matrix Y_nu and the commutant condition holds on each generation, with the same conclusion as long as Y_nu has no zero column. The lemma does not decide the sign s of the odd factorization; Lemma 4 of lemma 06 does (06_neutral_sector_criterion.md). At finite t the bound t^{1/2} y is the Dirac neutrino mass, of the order of the electroweak scale.

**Proof.** Lemma 44, Proposition 44.1 and Corollary 44.3: comparing the coefficients of t and t^alpha forces [T_R, rho(a)] = 0 and, for alpha > 0, [V_nu, rho(a)] = 0. The first commutant is C + H + M_3(C) by Theorem 20.2 (20_rank_one_block_classified_algebra.md), with lambda acting on nu_R; then rho(a) V_nu nu_R = y (q_11 nu_L + q_21 e_L) against V_nu rho(a) nu_R = lambda y nu_L gives q_11 = lambda and q_21 = 0, so q = Q_lambda.

**What it leaves open.** An independent check by a model other than its author. The direction lemma 44 names: a seesaw that keeps the Standard Model algebra needs the Dirac coupling bounded and the light mass generated at a lower scale, or a second heavy neutral state pairing with nu_L, the escape that lemma 06 lists from the KO-7-only conclusion. Remark 44.4 places the Majorana block in nine dimensions in the wall data X = tan(l D_F) of lemma 22, since a single Majorana field in dimension 8k + 1 has no scalar mass term, so the scaling here is a scaling of the wall coupling.
