These are correct geometry with no lemma resting on them. They stay as reference, with their status labels, and no result is withdrawn.

**Open ideas to carry forward.**

1. *Engineered connections* (§9). The construction of a horizontal unitary connection whose Berry connection on the vertical kernel is a prescribed one, through a surjective fibrewise Toeplitz map with off-diagonal components between flux summands, is the same device as the collar connection preserving the line summands in lemma 29, case (b), and the interpolation of 11D/11. The corpus had it on N over S^5 with the flux (1, 1, 2) giving the dual five-sphere spinor bundle S_5^*; the project used it on the disc bundle over S^4. The general statement, which connections on a coefficient bundle can be prescribed on a given vertical kernel, has been used twice and proved nowhere in general. Decision: a strategy item (T15 below), small.

2. *The integer Wilson-line condition* (§9, "A concrete Wilson-line compatibility test"). On the Berger family of S^5, with the engineered connection and the specified spin lifts, all three coefficient lines keep a circle zero mode exactly when eta^2 is an integer; eta^2 = 2 is nonround with U(3) isometry. A quantization of a metric parameter by a holonomy condition is an idea the project has not used; it is conditional on the stated lifts, and its Python check covers only the phase arithmetic. Decision: record under T15 as a test case, no claim.

3. *Seesaw as a scaling limit of the algebra chain* (§2.2, the Schur correction -t^{2 alpha - 1} B C^{-1} B^*). The remark that finite seesaw scaling with alpha = 1/2 need not retain the algebra of a bounded-mixing limit bears on STRATEGY T2c (the Majorana mass and the choice between 9 and 11), where lemma 08 stopped. Decision: add to T2c as the first computation to make: the retained algebra along the seesaw scaling, by the Schur formula, for the Standard Model data.

4. *The Weyl-group origin of the finite algebra* (§10). CP^2 x CP^1 = T^6/(S_3 x Z_2), with S_3 x Z_2 the Weyl group of SU(3) x SU(2); the spin-twisted real crossed product gives, on two orbit types, a corner C + H (from the binary dihedral group Dic_3 in the spin sector) and a central M_3(C) (from a three-point orbit), so A_F = C + H + M_3(C) is a corner of a finite-orbit quotient. It has a demonstrated order-zero failure (the opposite action), and the corner is chosen rather than derived. No lemma touches it. The idea that H comes from a spin lift of a reflection, and M_3 from an orbit, is of the same kind as the project's reading of gamma as the reflection of a circle (11D/01, Proposition 1.1). Decision: a strategy item (T16 below): whether the finite triple with its J can be built as a crossed product by the Weyl group with a regular left-right bimodule, which the corpus names as the repair and never tried.

5. *The second parent* (§15, the next test). The corpus asks for one real equivariant correspondence on the selected seven-manifold N_{1,-1} with the kernel S_5^*; lemma 28 did this for the disc bundle over S^4 instead. The two parents share the twistor fibration CP^3 -> S^4 (N -> F -> CP^2 and CP^3 -> S^4 are different fibrations of different spaces, so this is a parallel, not an identification). Decision: record as an alternative parent for T0, lower priority than the vector sector on the disc bundle.

6. *The six-to-five circle limit with doubling* (§12). D_tau = D + tau Gamma L with D_tau^2 = D^2 + tau^2 L^2 is the device of lemma 24's spectral walls and of the Clifford doubling of lemma 16; the corpus applied it to the circle quotient CP^3 -> S^5 with two fixed CP^1 strata and compensated the four O(3) zero modes by a coefficient doublet. The 11D line's Y_{3,2} is a circle bundle over CP^2 x CP^1, so the corpus's singular quotient and the 11D line's smooth bundle are two circle structures on related spaces, never compared. Decision: one line in STRATEGY T14 as a question, no item.

**Errors or overstatements found on reading.** None beyond those the ledger already records (the heavy-W prescription corrected in lemma 11; the CP^3 index statement corrected in lemma 01; the 5/8 from p = 5 withdrawn in §14.3 itself). The file's own status labels are consistent with its content.

**Open ideas to carry forward.**

7. *A single 9D Majorana field has no scalar mass* (§5.2, F3; Alvarez-Gaumé-Witten, dimension 8k + 1). In nine dimensions the charge-conjugation bilinear is symmetric, so a Lorentz-invariant mass needs an even number of real spinors; a background that breaks the 9D symmetry can still give a 4D Majorana mass. This bears directly on STRATEGY T2c (the Majorana mass and the choice between 9 and 11), where lemma 08 stopped: in D = 9 the right-handed neutrino mass must come from the wall data or from a pairing, never from a bulk mass term. Decision: add to T2c together with item 3 (the Schur seesaw); the two are the same computation seen from the bulk and from the finite side.

8. *A finite contact term without a W* (§5.1). A 9D four-fermion operator of dimension -7 gives C_beta ~ G_9 / V_5 on constant modes, allowed by SU(3) x U(1)_em; keeping G_F finite as M_W -> infinity at tree level needs g_2 ~ M_W, inconsistent with e = g_2 sin theta_W. The project's electroweak limit (lemmas 06, 12) sends the W to the wall; the record says what survives at low energy, a contact term with an undetermined coefficient, and that nothing in the geometry fixes it. Decision: one sentence in STRATEGY T2, no item: the limit removes beta decay unless a contact term is derived, and no derivation exists.

9. *A central Dirac mass forbids the orientability cycle* (§5.4, N9; the QED triple of van den Dungen-van Suijlekom as the example). If D_F is nonzero and commutes with A_F, then no cycle sum rho(a_i) J rho(b_i)^* J^{-1} equals gamma_F. Joined with §6.6 (orientability lost under the heavy centralizer while first order survives) and N7 (Poincaré duality fails for every three-summand KO-6 bimodule), this asks which axioms the finite triple of the main paper keeps along the chain of Theorem 20.2: Pati-Salam -> Standard Model -> colour + electromagnetism. The main paper proves the Connes-Lott data (order zero, first order, KO-6 signs) and says nothing about orientability or Poincaré duality at the later steps. Decision: a strategy item (T17 below), checkable by hand from the data of lemmas 20 and 21; its answer belongs in the dictionary note as one row.

10. *The Weyl quotient has no equivariant spin lift* (§6.13, N20; with §2.5 and N18). At the transposition fixed point, either preimage in Spin(6) squares to -1; a spin-c or projective lift is needed. This is a condition on item 4 (T16): the crossed product by the Weyl group with a real left-right bimodule must be built with a spin-c or twisted lift, which is also what §6.11 found on the flag, where E_2 = Q^* admits no antilinear automorphism and the quaternion structure lives on su(E_2), a non-constant bundle. Decision: fold into T16 as its first obstacle.

11. *Strong coupling at the literal collapse* (§6.10, last paragraph). In an unwarped Einstein-Hilbert reduction with fixed internal shape, g_a^{-2} ~ M_4^2 R^2, so R -> 0 at fixed M_4 drives the gauge couplings to strong coupling. The project's second scale (11D/10, the dilaton ruled out in scope) is the same question from the other side: what keeps the couplings finite when the tower opens. Decision: one line in STRATEGY T14 as a constraint on the vector sector; no item.

12. *The selection of a shape by a potential* (§4.1, D1, open). The record asks for one action and background that fix xi, with E3 as the counterexample in the simplest class, and names a quantized flux with density n / Vol as the kind of term that could depend on p. Lemma 26 (the interior vacuum at theta = pi/4 from frequency-2 channels) is a selection of this kind in the flat family. Decision: note in STRATEGY, under lemma 26, that D1 of the record is answered there for one family; the Berger family remains open and is low priority.
