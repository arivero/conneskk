The final example has one arrow preserving the right character and the other preserving the left character; it satisfies order one although \(B\) is not contained in \(\mathcal A(T)\). No correction is needed.

**2. Correct.** For the displayed rank-one \(T_\kappa\), with \(\kappa\ne0\), the two selected lines must reduce their respective actions and carry the same scalar. This gives (6), of real dimension \(2+4+18=24\). The self-adjoint off-diagonal operator has complex rank \(2\).

Its compression \(K:\mathbb C^2\to\mathbb C^4\) has rank one or two. Rank two gives dimension at most \(4+4+8=16\); rank one gives dimension \(24\), uniquely up to automorphism. For the displayed block, \(T_\kappa x=\kappa KxK\). For a general admissible connector, the paper’s \(K\) is obtained from an auxiliary intertwiner and need not equal the Dirac block. No correction is needed.

**4. Correct.** In [Result 5](../lemmas/20_connecting_operator.md), (18) and (19) preserve the boundary domain. The equality \(cX=X^*c^{-1}\) is the required compatibility for \(J\).
