Finite spectral windows with an opening tower in seven-dimensional parents

Draft, 4 November 2026

Abstract

The statement that a Kaluza-Klein dimension opens as the WW and ZZ become massless needs a family, since along a general path to MW=MZ=0M_W=M_Z=0 no tower opens. We give families with the hierarchy m≪Eε≪Δ→0m\ll E_\varepsilon\ll\Delta\to0 in which a moving low-energy window keeps a fixed finite number of states and the count at fixed positive energy diverges. On an interval this follows from exact formulas, and in a five-dimensional toy the vector poles follow the same hierarchy. On the disc bundle of Λ−2T*S4\Lambda^2_-T^*S^4, a connected seven-dimensional commutative parent with boundary ℂℙ3\mathbb{CP}^3, we treat a fermion window: for a chosen class of metrics, a local Dirac operator with a Callias potential has exactly eight light states, one copy of the pseudoreal 𝟒\mathbf 4 of Sp(2)Sp(2) near each end, with exponentially small mixing, and real functions compress to the two-sheet finite maps generating M2(ℍ)⊕M4(ℂ)M_2(\mathbb H)\oplus M_4(\mathbb C). Along a family with logarithmically growing potential and expanding metric, the eight states stay separated at the moving scale, and the count at fixed energy has seven-dimensional Weyl growth. The proofs rest on exact sums of squares given by Sp(2)Sp(2)-equivariance. On the disc bundle no calculation identifies the tunnelling scale with MWM_W or MZM_Z: the vector poles, the gauge couplings, the connector of the real bimodule, the chirality of the physical spectrum and any dynamics selecting the family remain open.

0.1 Introduction

In the towerless reading of almost-commutative geometry proposed in (“Finite Spectral Triples as Towerless Intervals” 2026), a finite spectral triple is the low-energy window of a parent with an internal geometry, and the labels 9 and 11 are limits of an even interior at finite Fermi constant, where the physical point would lie. In that proposal, the limit towards 11 is the one in which the WW and ZZ become massless and a dimension opens. This note asks what that statement can mean.

It needs a path. On the interval of (“The Finite Dirac Operator as a Boundary Condition on an Interval” 2026), the vector masses obey sin2(MWl)=12sin22θ\sin^2(M_Wl)=\tfrac12\sin^2 2\theta, so θ→0\theta\to0 at fixed ll makes them vanish with the tower spacing fixed. The criterion we use is a hierarchy along a family indexed by ε→0\varepsilon\to0, mε≪Eε≪Δε→0,m_\varepsilon\ll E_\varepsilon\ll\Delta_\varepsilon\to0,(1) with mεm_\varepsilon the light scale and Δε\Delta_\varepsilon the first discarded level. Then a window at the moving scale EεE_\varepsilon holds a fixed number of states. The hierarchy alone does not make the count at fixed E>0E>0 diverge, so we require that as a separate property, and prove it for each family below. For such a family the two observations have different limits, which reconciles a finite description near the weak scale with a tower at fixed energy.

The results of Sections 2 and 3, and the formulation of (1), follow an audit of this project (see the Provenance). The new content is Section 4 and what rests on it: a window obtained from a local operator on a connected seven-dimensional parent, rather than prescribed.

0.7 Relation to prior work

The mechanisms have precedents, which we distinguish from the combination studied here. Chiral localization on defects and boundaries goes back to (Kaplan 1992; Shamir 1993), and gauge symmetry breaking by interval boundary conditions to (Csáki, Grojean, Hubisz, et al. 2004; Csáki, Grojean, Murayama, et al. 2004); a tree-level mt=2MWm_t=2M_W for a suitable representation is in (Cacciapaglia, Csáki, and Park 2006). The square identity with a zero-order cross term is in (Maridakis 2015), in a concentrating-pair setting, and a horizontal and vertical factorization of the Dirac operator with a curvature contraction is in (Kaad and Suijlekom 2017), under other hypotheses. Index statements of Callias type in families are in (Callias 1978; Kottke 2009), and the finite boundary window is a separate question. Kaluza-Klein modes that become massless during gauge restoration are followed in (Iso, Kitazawa, and Suyama 2021), with a different mechanism and geometry. Targeted searches found these and did not establish whether the eight-state theorem has appeared elsewhere; they were not exhaustive.

0.8 What is missing

The vector sector. On the family of Theorem 5 one needs the kinetic matrix and the mass Hessian of the vector fluctuations, then the momentum-dependent kernel, with the aim MW,MZ∼cλ/RM_W,M_Z\sim c\lambda/R. The tunnelling eigenvalue alone does not establish this, so the words “massless electroweak limit” apply to the disc bundle only as a target.

The couplings. For a bulk Yang-Mills field with a constant profile, g4−2=Vol(X)/g112g_4^{-2}=\mathrm{Vol}(X)/g_{11}^2, so with fixed microscopic coupling a plain dilation gives g4∝R−7/2g_4\propto R^{-7/2}, which must be separated from restoration at finite coupling. Gauge fields from isometries have Killing overlap integrals instead, and non-Killing fields behave differently again (Baptista 2026).

The dynamics. A potential ar−p+bε2rqa\,r^{-p}+b\,\varepsilon^2r^q has its minimum at r∝ε−2/(p+q)r\propto\varepsilon^{-2/(p+q)}, so it would select α=2/(p+q)\alpha=2/(p+q) if p+q>2p+q>2. Nothing has derived such a potential, and adding it by hand derives nothing.

The finite structure. The window gives the graded eight-dimensional space and the limit algebra. The connector of the bimodule of complex dimension 32 with a real structure, the quaternionic grading and the chirality of the physical spectrum remain inputs. The product of two copies of the parent has heat exponent t−7t^{-7}, so the bimodule should come from a finite-rank correspondence, to keep seven dimensions.
