The finite Dirac operator as a boundary condition on an interval

Draft, 3 November 2026

Abstract

Every finite Dirac operator DFD_F with the signs of KO-dimension six is exactly the lowest spectral window of the pure derivative on an interval of length ll, for l∥DF∥<π/2l\|D_F\|<\pi/2, with no mass term. The boundary data are a grading at each wall, and DF=(2l)−1Γ0log⁡(Γ1Γ0)D_F=(2l)^{-1}\Gamma_0\log(\Gamma_1\Gamma_0), so the singular values of DFD_F are principal angles over ll and the masses are quantized momenta. Preserving the domain restricts the observables at a wall to the commutant of its grading. Order one holds in this parent for any finite data with order zero. The compression of an observable is its average under eixDFe^{ixD_F}, |x|≤l|x|\le l, which tends to the finite representation with rate l2l^2. Aligned walls compress a bulk prealgebra stable under the grading to its even part, with the grading inherited. Even scalar fluctuations are pure gauge, and on a moving window they reproduce the quadratic term of Chamseddine-Connes-van Suijlekom. Odd ones change the angles; for M2(ℍ)M_2(\mathbb H) their field space is S4S^4. In a Yang-Mills parent with a wall connector the WW and ZZ masses are exact, with the constants 22 and 16/516/5 at small angle. Unfolded, the parent is −id/dx+DF-i\,d/dx+D_F on a circle, and at leading order in small masses, for a vector of constant profile in the transported frame, the sum rule of the spectral action becomes MW2=3−d24∑Nfmf2M_W^2=\frac{3-d}{24}\sum N_fm_f^2, with dd the spectral dimension of the circle at the cutoff, so that mt/MWm_t/M_W goes from 8/3\sqrt{8/3} at d=0d=0 to 22 at d=1d=1. All finite data are inputs and the parent algebra is matrix-valued, so the selection of the finite algebra is untouched.

1 Introduction

In an almost-commutative geometry the masses of the fermions are the entries of a finite Dirac operator DFD_F, and in a Kaluza-Klein reduction a mass can be a momentum in the internal direction, quantized by the boundary conditions. We relate the two for an interval. The Connes-Lott mass is the lowest such momentum, π/(2l)\pi/(2l), and the known boundary formula tan⁡(mnL)=MDL\tan(m_nL)=M_DL of Higgsless models (Csáki, Grojean, Hubisz, et al. 2004) gives the interpolation for one flavor. Here we carry this through for the whole finite operator, with its real structure and grading, and we ask what the parent says about the algebra, order one, and fluctuations.

The finite data remain inputs, so the existence of the window is close to a reparametrization. The content lies in the existence of a consistent parent, and in the form it gives to the wall algebra, the compression and the fluctuations.

3 Algebra, order one, compression

Let a unital algebra AFA_F act evenly and faithfully, with order zero, and let CFC_F be the commutant of DFD_F in AFA_F. Order one is not assumed.

Theorem 3. A function f∈C∞([0,l],AF)f\in C^\infty([0,l],A_F) preserves the domain of DXD_X iff f(l)∈CFf(l)\in C_F. Let AlA_l be the algebra of such ff. Then [DX,π(f)]=Eπ(f′)[D_X,\pi(f)]=E\pi(f'), and we have a real even spectral triple with order zero and order one.

Structure of the proof. The wall conditions say that π(f(l))\pi(f(l)) commutes with tan⁡(lDF)\tan(lD_F), equivalently with DFD_F, for l∥DF∥<π/2l\|D_F\|<\pi/2. The opposite action is pointwise, so by finite order zero Eπ(f′)E\pi(f') commutes with it. ◻

So AFA_F acts freely in the bulk, and the wall carries the commutant of DFD_F. In the Standard Model data with nonzero Yukawa blocks, this is {(λ,λ,m)}\{(\lambda,\lambda,m)\}, the algebra of the photon and gluons. Finite order one does not arise as a condition on the parent.

4 Fluctuations

No-go theorem (the radial mode). For one interval, fixed XX, no bulk masses, even scalar inner fluctuations and the moving window, the singular values of the finite operator are fixed. With nonzero Yukawa data, the Higgs quaternion stays on the unit quaternions, with no radial mode (Supplemental Material, part 22). The following lie outside the hypotheses, and each needs its own construction: XX as a field on the wall; bulk terms odd for the grading; bulk masses not commuting with the gauge action; more internal dimensions; an added composite degree of freedom. At the 10→910\to9 endpoint this agrees with a frozen Higgs field.

The odd escape is explicit. For W=ℍ2W=\mathbb H^2, the gradings with quaternionic eigenspaces form S4=Sp(2)/(Sp(1)×Sp(1))S^4=Sp(2)/(Sp(1)\times Sp(1)), with Γp=paΓa\Gamma_p=p_a\Gamma_a. For two points at geodesic distance dd, all principal angles equal θ=d/2\theta=d/2. On Hom(V,W)⊕Hom(W,V)\mathrm{Hom}(V,W)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(W,V), the connection K(x,y)=(θ/l)(Fx,yF)K(x,y)=(\theta/l)(Fx,yF), with FF odd in M2(ℍ)M_2(\mathbb H) and F2=−1F^2=-1, gives a universal mass θ/l\theta/l, with J1=Γ0JJ_1=\Gamma_0J and a second grading at the point pp on the first summand and its mirror image on the second. So (θ,q)(\theta,q) are polar coordinates of a Higgs quaternion on S4S^4, the coset of the minimal composite Higgs model (Agashe, Contino, and Pomarol 2005), whose warped construction and potentials are not those here; the analogy with gauge-Higgs unification (Hosotani 1983) has the same limit. That S4S^4 is also the base of the twistor window is an observation here, with no identification. J1J_1 induces an opposite action twisted by the inner automorphism γW⋅γW\gamma_W\cdot\gamma_W, a structural analogy with the twisted triples of the grand symmetry, whose automorphism is different (D’Andrea, Lizzi, and Martinetti 2014).

5 Gauge sector and the two limits

Assume a flat product and a positive five-dimensional Yang-Mills kinetic form with no boundary terms. Then AμA_\mu at a wall lies in the wall algebra, the directions in it are Neumann and the others Dirichlet, as in Higgsless models (Csáki, Grojean, Murayama, et al. 2004).

The strict family. For even walls with nonzero Yukawa data, the unbroken directions have masses nπ/ln\pi/l and the broken ones (n−12)π/l(n-\tfrac12)\pi/l, so MW=MZ=π/(2l)M_W=M_Z=\pi/(2l), with massless photon and gluons after unimodularity. Fix a light block M0M_0 and a heavy block Θ/l\Theta/l with 0<Θ<π/20<\Theta<\pi/2. As l→0l\to0, the light couplings l|M0|l|M_0| vanish at fixed mass, the heavy mass Θ/l\Theta/l and MWM_W diverge with mt/MW=2Θ/π<1m_t/M_W=2\Theta/\pi<1, and the light compression converges. This realizes the 10→910\to9 proposal at this level, with Xq∼(π/2)mq/MWX_q\sim(\pi/2)m_q/M_W, in the form of yq=2mq/v→0y_q=\sqrt2m_q/v\to0 at fixed mass. Identifying XqX_q with a four-dimensional Yukawa coupling is a hypothesis.

They point toward changed form factors, as in warped composite models (Agashe, Contino, and Pomarol 2005), or a wall scalar with its own potential, for the physical interior, consistently with the reading of the odd labels as limits.

6 The sum rule and the effective dimension

Unfolding gives a sharper statement. For x∈[−l,l]x\in[-l,l], put ΨQ(x)=QL(|x|)+isgn(x)QR(|x|)\Psi_Q(x)=Q_L(|x|)+i\,\mathrm{sgn}(x)Q_R(|x|) and ΨU(x)=UR(|x|)−isgn(x)UL(|x|)\Psi_U(x)=U_R(|x|)-i\,\mathrm{sgn}(x)U_L(|x|). Up to the factor 1/21/\sqrt2, this is unitary, and it carries R*DXR=D0+EΓFDFR^*D_XR=D_0+E\Gamma_FD_F to −iddx⊗1+1⊗DF-i\frac{d}{dx}\otimes1+1\otimes D_F(7) on the circle of length 2l2l. So the tower is DFD_F shifted by kn=nπ/lk_n=n\pi/l, n∈ℤn\in\mathbb Z, and an even generator TT constant in this transported frame becomes 1⊗T1\otimes T. In the original frame it is RTR*RTR^*, generally with an odd bulk component, so it needs an enlarged gauge algebra, such as the prealgebra above. 7 Status

As mathematics, the one-flavor formula and the gauge boundary conditions are known (Csáki, Grojean, Hubisz, et al. 2004; Csáki, Grojean, Murayama, et al. 2004), compression of operator systems is treated in (Connes and Suijlekom 2021), and spectral action on spaces with boundary in (Chamseddine and Connes 2011), for a boundary of the manifold, with a local chirality projector. In our searches we did not find the finite real triple as an exact window on an internal interval, with the wall algebra and the average formula; the searches are recorded in the Supplemental Material and do not establish absence. The parent algebra is matrix-valued, so the founding question for a commutative parent remains open, as does any selection of AFA_F. On ℂℙ3×I\mathbb{CP}^3\times I, nonlocal spectral walls give the exact window for the operator and the grading, with the higher KK-modes decoupling with the radius, but they reduce scalar functions at the walls to constants, while direct aligned compression of real functions on S4S^4 gives only ℝ⊕ℝ\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R, against ℍ⊕ℍ\mathbb H\oplus\mathbb H when the matrix algebra is generated at the intermediate stage (Supplemental Material, part 24).
