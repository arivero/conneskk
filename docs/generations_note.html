The two families of 84 and the number of generations

Draft, 4 November 2026

Abstract

With three generations and Dirac neutrinos, the 96 states of the finite Hilbert space split as 84+1284+12 in two ways, without the top or without the neutrinos, the blocks whose removal keeps the spectral-action values 5/85/8 and 8/38/3. With three families, one root per state, no structure examined exchanges the two blocks: unitaries commuting with colour, automorphisms of 𝔢8\mathfrak e_8 preserving or inverting a family grading, and isometries of E8E_8 roots in 192 slot assignments. With four families an element of W(E8)W(E_8) does, transposing colour and family, and 128=44+84128=44+84 under Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9) with 44=32+1244=32+12, a numerical fit, since colour lies in no such Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9). We propose no mechanism for three.

1 Introduction

In the Connes-Chamseddine line the number of generations is an input (Chamseddine and Connes 2008, 2007), and Krajewski diagrams (Krajewski 1998) and the Jureit-Stephan classification (Jureit and Stephan 2005) admit any multiplicity. The proposals we located that derive three, the DFTUZ notes of 1999 and 2000 (Rivero 1999, 2000) and the exceptional-Jordan line (Dubois-Violette 2016; Boyle 2026), lie outside the spectral-triple axioms; whether they give three as a theorem was not checked. In the limit of noncommutative geometry we know of no argument beyond “more than one”. The postulates behind the SO(30)SO(30) of composite scalars (Rivero 2005) fix the number at three through three counting equations (Supplemental Material, part 36, Section 1), outside the spectral-triple axioms and unrelated to the Kaluza-Klein reading pursued here. The hints are scarce; this note records one.

Counting four states per Dirac fermion and colour, Supplemental Material, part 21 splits the 96 states of HFH_F, three generations with νR\nu_R (Chamseddine, Connes, and Marcolli 2007), in two ways, 96=84⏟F1+12⏟T=84⏟F2+12⏟N,96=\underbrace{84}_{F_1}+\underbrace{12}_{T}=\underbrace{84}_{F_2}+\underbrace{12}_{N},(1) with TT the top block, NN the neutrino block, F1=HF⊖TF_1=H_F\ominus T and F2=HF⊖NF_2=H_F\ominus N. The three-form C3C_3 of eleven-dimensional supergravity and its dual C6C_6 have (93)=(96)=84\binom93=\binom96=84 on-shell components, a real count, while 84 in HFH_F is a complex dimension.

Proposition 1. Among blocks of 12 states made of whole Dirac species, those whose removal keeps cos2θNC=5/8\cos^2\theta_{\mathrm{NC}}=5/8 and the ratio 8/38/3 of the photon and gluon coefficients, in the quadratic spectral action of Supplemental Material, part 18, are exactly one up-type quark and the three neutrinos together.

Proof sketch. With the sums (∑T3Q,∑YQ;Q2,colour)(\sum T_3Q,\sum YQ;\,Q^2,\,\text{colour}) of Supplemental Material, part 21, an up-type quark (1,53;163,2)(1,\frac53;\frac{16}3,2) is proportional to the totals (6,10;32,12)(6,10;32,12) and a down-type quark is not. Three leptons with kk charged give cos2θNC=(10−32k)/(16−2k)\cos^2\theta_{\mathrm{NC}}=(10-\frac32k)/(16-2k), which is 5/85/8 only for k=0k=0 (Supplemental Material, part 33). ◻

Supplemental Material, part 21 proposed relating one family to C3C_3 or the M2-brane and the other to C6C_6 or the M5-brane, with the exchange of TT and NN as the content of the duality on the Standard Model side. We collect what the eleven-dimensional line proves about this exchange (Supplemental Material, 11D files 03 to 05): in every structure examined, three families in single slots admit none, and four admit one.

5 The four-family count and the two placements

Under Spin(9)⊂SO(16)\mathrm{Spin}(9)\subset SO(16) embedded through its spinor, the 128 is 44+8444+84 for one chirality of the embedding, the graviton and the three-form (recalled in Supplemental Material, part 34). With four families, 128−84=44=32+12,128-84=44=32+12,(5) so removing F1F_1 leaves the fourth family and TT, and removing F2F_2 leaves it and NN, completing (1). MM carries neither set of 44 onto the other, and Proposition 11 excludes every isometry.

Proposition 12. The colour SU(3)SU(3) of (4) lies in no Spin(9)⊂SO(16)\mathrm{Spin}(9)\subset SO(16) with 128→44+84128\to44+84. So the split 44+8444+84 is not colour-covariant, and the fit 44=32+1244=32+12 is numerical.

Proof. Under such a Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9) the vector 16 of SO(16)SO(16) is the spinor, which restricts to an SU(3)⊂Spin(9)SU(3)\subset\mathrm{Spin}(9) as 16(1)16(1), 2(3+3‾)+4(1)2(3+\bar3)+4(1) or 8+88+8, by the three real nine-dimensional representations of Proposition 2(b). Under colour the vector 16 is 3+3‾+10(1)3+\bar3+10(1). The stabilizer of the split in 𝔰𝔬(16)=𝔰𝔬(9)+84\mathfrak{so}(16)=\mathfrak{so}(9)+84 is 𝔰𝔭𝔦𝔫(9)\mathfrak{spin}(9), since the 84 is irreducible and 𝔰𝔬(16)\mathfrak{so}(16) is irreducible on the 128. ◻

Colour likewise lies in no 𝔰𝔬(9)\mathfrak{so}(9) of 𝔢8\mathfrak e_8 with 248=36+44+84+84248=36+44+84+84, that of Section 3 included: colour gives 248=8+78(1)+27(3+3‾)248=8+78(1)+27(3+\bar3), and the 44 contains a 66 or a 2727 under any SU(3)SU(3) in such an 𝔰𝔬(9)\mathfrak{so}(9). The scope is the regular colour SU(3)SU(3) in this class.

The top thus has two placements, in the C3C_3 sector of the SU(9)SU(9) grading and with the fourth family in the 44 of Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9); the subgroups differ, and no identification of their sectors is claimed. The 𝔰𝔬(9)\mathfrak{so}(9) of Section 3, fixed by the Chevalley involution on 𝔰𝔩(9)\mathfrak{sl}(9), is conjugate in E8E_8 to this Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9), with 𝔨=𝔰𝔬(9)+84\mathfrak k=\mathfrak{so}(9)+84 and 𝔭=44+84\mathfrak p=44+84, the 44 in the gravity sector, and inside E8E_8 both subgroups are SO(9)SO(9) (Supplemental Material, part 34, Proposition 5.6; stated by Claude Sonnet 5.5, proved by Claude Opus 5.5 as referee). For the Chevalley 𝔰𝔬(9)\mathfrak{so}(9) itself the top projects only to the three-form 84 and the νc\nu^c alone to the 44 at t=+12t=+\frac12, against the numerical count 44=32+1244=32+12; the transport between the two frames is open. A recalled analogy (Supplemental Material, part 34) is Gell-Mann’s colour-flavour SU(8)SU(8) of N=8N=8 supergravity, with the spin-12\frac12 fermions in Λ3(8)=56\Lambda^3(8)=56 and 8=3+58=3+5; Proposition 2(c) plays the role of the known failure of that content.

6 What this leaves

Escapes. Proposition 2: nonlinear colour, colour on an internal space, brane states, non-equivariant identifications. Proposition 6: automorphisms that neither preserve nor invert the family grading, other embeddings. Proposition 12: other colour embeddings and non-equivariant identifications. Propositions 7, 8 and 11: maps not linear on the roots, larger groups, and states spread over several roots, which heterotic compactification uses. In E8⊃SO(10)×SU(4)E_8\supset SO(10)\times SU(4) the SU(4)SU(4) is the structure group of a rank-four bundle VV, and the families are zero modes, as the massless fermions of a Kaluza-Klein theory are (Witten 1985), with net number an index, 3 in the heterotic Standard Models on SU(4)SU(4) bundles (recalled in Supplemental Material, part 34). A zero mode is a section of VV, in general involving all four slots, so Propositions 7 and 8 do not apply; for split bundles V=⨁iLiV=\bigoplus_iL_i with ni=−χ(Li)≥0n_i=-\chi(L_i)\ge0 and ∑ni=3\sum n_i=3, the degree multisets still differ in all 40 cases checked.

Open items. (i) How the 96 states of the three-family frame sit against the conjugated Spin(9)\mathrm{Spin}(9). (ii) A four-family finite spectral triple, or Hořava-Witten configuration, with MM or FF as a symmetry and an FF-invariant algebra, from which the statements of Supplemental Material, part 21 follow as the fourth family decouples; whether the mechanism of the top can make it heavy is open. (iii) A nonsplit bundle with index 3, its zero modes, and the action of MM on them.

We propose no mechanism for three. The obstructions say where an exchange of the top with the neutrinos can live: with four slots, and with three as a count of zero modes or of light families.
