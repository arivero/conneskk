# Dualities between nine and eleven dimensions, adapted to the spectral triple

Part of the 11D line of research ([README](README.md), [PLAN](PLAN.md), task C). Attempted by Claude (Anthropic), 4 Nov 2026. Propositions 7.1 to 7.3 are proved here by hand; the string-theory statements of section 1 are recalled, not reread; the readings are proposals. Referee pending.

## 1. The dualities (recalled)

1. **M-theory on T^2 and type IIB on S^1** (Schwarz, hep-th/9508143; Aspinwall, hep-th/9508154). The modulus tau of the torus is the IIB coupling and SL(2, Z) is IIB S-duality; the IIB radius scales as a negative power of the area A of the torus. M2-branes wrapped on T^2 are IIB momentum states, and momenta on T^2 are (p, q)-strings wound on S^1. Eleven dimensions at A -> infinity, ten (IIB) at A -> 0, nine in between.
2. **Sixteen supercharges, rank 17** (Polchinski and Witten, hep-th/9510169; Hořava and Witten, hep-th/9510209). The two heterotic strings on S^1 are T-dual through Wilson lines; their type I' form is an interval with two orientifold planes O8 and sixteen D8-branes whose positions are the Wilson-line eigenvalues; at strong coupling, M-theory on an interval times a circle, with E8 walls.
3. **Sixteen supercharges, lower rank.** The CHL string (rank 9) and M-theory on the Möbius strip; a rank-1 component related to the Klein bottle (Aharony, Komargodski and Patir, hep-th/0702195); further components with discrete theta angles (Montero and Parra de Freitas, arXiv:2209.03361).
4. **Light-cone M-theory.** On a light-like circle with momentum N, M-theory is the U(N) quantum mechanics of nine Hermitian matrices (Banks, Fischler, Shenker and Susskind, hep-th/9610043; Susskind). On T^2 with a light-like component of C_3, the torus becomes noncommutative and SL(2, Z) acts by Morita equivalence (Connes, Douglas and Schwarz, hep-th/9711162).

## 3. Proposition 7.1 (the angle of a channel lives on an interval)

**Statement.** In the parent of [lemma 22](../lemmas/22_dirac_operator_as_boundary_condition.md), a channel with singular value σ of the Yukawa block and angle θ = l σ has the signed spectrum {(n pi + θ)/l, (n pi − θ)/l : n in Z} (Theorem 22.1). As a set, this depends only on the class of θ in R modulo the group generated by θ -> −θ and θ -> θ + pi, whose fundamental domain is [0, pi/2]. At the endpoints:

1. θ = 0: the spectrum is n pi/l with a zero mode of multiplicity two (periodic);
2. θ = pi/2: the spectrum is (n + 1/2) pi/l, each value twice (antiperiodic), the spectrum of the chiral interval of Proposition 1.1; it is the limit X -> infinity of the wall data X = tan(θ), where the lowest eigenspace is degenerate, and in lemma 23 the point of antipodal gradings, where M_W = M_Z = 0 at fixed l with the fermion at pi/(2l).

**Proof.** The set is invariant under θ -> −θ (it exchanges the two progressions) and θ -> θ + pi (it shifts n). The orbit of θ meets [0, pi/2] in one point. The endpoint values are substitutions; for θ -> pi/2 the wall conditions Q_R(l) = X U_R(l), U_L(l) = −X^* Q_L(l) tend to U_R(l) = Q_L(l) = 0. The statements at θ = pi/2 are those of lemma 23 (Theorem 23.6 and the reading after Proposition 23.7). ∎

So the parameter space of one channel of the finite Dirac operator, for a given parent, is an interval with two distinguished ends, and lemma 22's restriction l ||D_F|| < pi/2 is the choice of the fundamental domain.

## 4. Proposition 7.2 (the far end is the displaced D8-brane on the other wall)

**Statement.** Put a = θ/pi in [0, 1/2]. By Proposition 6.1 the channel spectrum is that of the states (30)_{±1} of SO(32) on a circle of radius R = l/pi with the Wilson line W_a = 1_30 ⊕ R(2 pi a). The interval [0, 1/2] is the moduli interval of one Wilson-line eigenvalue modulo a -> −a and a -> a + 1; the unbroken group is SO(32) at a = 0 and SO(30) x SO(2) for 0 < a <= 1/2; at a = 1/2 the holonomy is −1 on the plane of SO(2), W_{1/2} = diag(1_30, −1_2), and the (30)_{±1} tower is (n + 1/2)/R, the antiperiodic tower of Proposition 1.1. The one-loop minimum of [lemma 24](../lemmas/24_commutative_parent.md), Theorem 9, θ = pi/2, is the point a = 1/2.

**Proof.** Proposition 6.1 and Proposition 7.1 with θ = pi a; the commutant of W_a in SO(32) for 0 < a < 1/2 is SO(30) x SO(2), and at a = 1/2 its identity component is still SO(30) x SO(2), since only two eigenvalues are −1. Lemma 24, Theorem 9, item 1, places the minimum at t = sin^2 θ = 1. ∎

**Reading (proposal).** In type I' the Wilson line W_a is fifteen D8-branes, with images, on one orientifold plane and the sixteenth at distance proportional to a from it; a = 1/2 puts it on the other orientifold plane (recalled: hep-th/9711098, hep-th/0108135). The dictionary of file 06 then reads: the top angle is the position of the top's D8-brane on the type I' interval, the two ends of the fundamental domain are the two orientifold walls, and the one-loop vacuum of the flat family of lemmas 23 and 24 carries the top brane to the far wall, where the tower of the stack-brane strings is antiperiodic. In supersymmetric type I' the D8 positions are flat directions; the potential of lemma 24 belongs to the non-supersymmetric flat family, the setting in which a force on the brane is expected.

Under the strong-coupling duality of item 2 of section 1, the D8 position is a Wilson-line eigenvalue of the heterotic string on S^1, and in eleven dimensions an E8 holonomy around the circle on a Hořava-Witten wall. So the same datum, the top angle, is a brane position at 9 and a holonomy at 11.
