## Setting

Witten's spaces M^{pqr} = G/H, with G = SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) and H = SU(2) x U(1) x U(1), are the seven-dimensional spaces of least dimension with the isometry group of the Standard Model, so 4 + 7 = 11 (Witten 1981; [LITERATURE.md §F](../LITERATURE.md#f-weinberg-angle-from-a-squashed-s3--lens-space-and-from-mpqr)). Castellani, D'Auria and Fré gave them Einstein metrics; Killing spinors exist only for p = q, where the AdS4 vacuum has N = 2 and the space is M^{111} (Fabbri, Fré, Gualtieri, Termonia, hep-th/9903036). Fabbri et al. (hep-th/9907219, §4) write the cone over M^{111} as the Kähler quotient of C^5 by a circle acting with weights 2 on a triplet U and -3 on a doublet V, with D-term 2|U|^2 = 3|V|^2, and M^{111} as a circle bundle over P^2 x P^1. In later notation it is M^{3,2}.

These weights are six times the hypercharges of the 5-bar of SU(5). The rest of the file develops that observation.

## Theorem 2.1 (M^{111} is the hypercharge quotient of the 5-bar)

**Statement.** Let 5bar = C^3 + C^2 carry the Standard Model action on the left-handed fields d^c in (3bar, 1)_{1/3} and L in (1, 2)_{-1/2}; write U for d^c and V for L, so 6Y = 2 on U and -3 on V. Let mu_Y = (2|U|^2 - 3|V|^2)/6 be the moment map of hypercharge, and

    Y_{3,2} = (mu_Y^{-1}(0) ∩ S^9) / U(1)_Y.

1. Y_{3,2} is a smooth, simply connected seven-manifold, the circle bundle of O(3, 2) over CP^2 x CP^1 (up to orientation), and its cone is the Kähler quotient C^5 // U(1)_Y of Fabbri et al.
2. The cone is Calabi-Yau (Gorenstein, sum of weights 0) because tr Y = 0 on the 5-bar, that is, because U(1)_Y lies in SU(5).
3. The group acting effectively on Y_{3,2} through the quotient is (U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y. Its subgroup S(U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y is PSU(3) x SO(3), and the overall phase of U(5) supplies one further circle, the Reeb (R-symmetry) direction.
4. The functions on Y_{3,2} that are polynomial in U, V and their conjugates have U(1)_Y-charge zero; the holomorphic ones are spanned by the monomials of degree (3k, 2k) in (U, V), whose span as tensors is Sym^{3k}(3bar) x Sym^{2k}(2) and as functions its dual Sym^{3k}(3) x Sym^{2k}(2): for k = 1 the functions form the (10, 3) of Fabbri et al.

**Proof.** On mu_Y^{-1}(0) ∩ S^9 we have |U|^2 = 3/5 and |V|^2 = 2/5, a product of round spheres S^5 x S^3. The circle acts by (lambda^2 U, lambda^{-3} V); a fixed point would need lambda^2 = lambda^3 = 1, so lambda = 1 and the action is free. The quotient of S^5 x S^3 by the full torus T^2 of phases of U and V is CP^2 x CP^1, and Y_{3,2} -> CP^2 x CP^1 is a principal bundle for T^2/U(1)_Y. A character lambda_U^m lambda_V^n of T^2 is trivial on U(1)_Y exactly when 2m - 3n = 0, so the characters of T^2/U(1)_Y are generated by (m, n) = (3, 2), whose line bundle is O(3) ⊠ O(2) up to the sign convention; this gives item 1 and item 4. Simple connectivity follows from the exact homotopy sequence of U(1) -> S^5 x S^3 -> Y_{3,2}. Item 2: the sum of weights is 3 * 2 + 2 * (-3) = 0. Item 3: an element (g, h) of U(3) x U(2) acts trivially on the quotient exactly when (g, h) = (lambda^2, lambda^{-3}), because mu_Y^{-1}(0) spans C^5. The intersection of SU(3) x SU(2) with U(1)_Y consists of lambda^6 = 1, generated by (e^{2 pi i/3} I_3, -I_2), the centre Z_3 x Z_2. ∎

So the isometry group of the Witten space with Killing spinors, SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) (Fabbri et al.), is the centralizer of hypercharge in U(5), divided by hypercharge. The SU(3) x SU(2) part is the Standard Model one; the remaining circle is the R-symmetry, and hypercharge itself has been quotiented. Witten assigned the U(1) of G to hypercharge; in this realization hypercharge is a different U(1) ([03](03_branes_hypercharge_and_the_84.md), Proposal 3.1).

## Proposition 2.2 (uniqueness of the count 7)

**Statement.** (a) Among the quotients Y_{a,b} = (S^5 x S^3)/U(1), with weights b on C^3 and -a on C^2 and gcd(a, b) = 1, a, b > 0, the cone is Calabi-Yau only for (a, b) = (3, 2). (b) For a complex representation of dimension n and a free circle action, (S^{2n-1} ∩ mu^{-1}(0))/U(1) has dimension 2n - 3; this is 7 only for n = 5. Among representations of SU(5), with hypercharge as the circle, only the 5 and the 5-bar give a seven-dimensional quotient.

**Proof.** (a) The Calabi-Yau condition is 3b - 2a = 0. (b) Dimension count: 2n - 1 for the sphere, minus one for the moment map, minus one for the circle. The irreducible representations of SU(5) of dimension 5 are the 5 and the 5-bar. ∎

Witten's count reads: 11 = 4 + (2 * 5 - 3). The seven internal dimensions come from the smallest representation of SU(5), with hypercharge as the circle. For the 10 the quotient has dimension 17, and for the 5 + 10 it has 27.

## Proposition 2.3 (topology of Y_{a,b})

**Statement.** Let Y_{a,b} -> B = CP^2 x CP^1 be the circle bundle with Euler class a h_1 + b h_2, a != 0. Then H^2(Y) = Z + Z/gcd(a, b), b_3(Y) = 0, H^4(Y) is a group of order a^2, cyclic when gcd(a, b) = 1, and Y is spin exactly when a is odd and b is even. For (a, b) = (3, 2): H^2 = Z, H^4 = Z/9, spin. The pullback H^2(B) = Z^2 -> H^2(Y) = Z is (x, y) -> 2x - 3y up to sign: the two divisor classes h_1 = [{l(U) = 0}] and h_2 = [{m(V) = 0}] go to 2 and -3, the values of 6Y on d^c and L.

**Proof.** The Gysin sequence H^{k-2}(B) -> H^k(B) -> H^k(Y) -> H^{k-1}(B) -> H^{k+1}(B), with the first map the cup product with c = a h_1 + b h_2 and H^*(B) = Z[h_1, h_2]/(h_1^3, h_2^2). In degree 2 the cokernel of 1 -> (a, b) is Z^2/(a, b). In degree 4 the map H^2(B) -> H^4(B) has matrix [[a, 0], [b, a]] in the bases (h_1, h_2), (h_1^2, h_1 h_2), with determinant a^2 and Smith invariants (gcd(a, b), a^2/gcd(a, b)); since H^3(B) = 0 this gives H^4(Y), and the same matrix is injective, so H^3(Y) = 0. For spin: w_2(B) = h_1 mod 2, and the kernel of H^2(B; Z/2) -> H^2(Y; Z/2) is spanned by c mod 2; so w_2(Y) = 0 exactly when (1, 0) = (a, b) mod 2. For (3, 2), Z^2/(3, 2) -> Z by (x, y) -> 2x - 3y is an isomorphism. ∎ (Python: Smith invariants and the spin condition, section C.)

The product member CP^2 x S^2 x S^1 of Witten's family (a = b = 0) is not spin.

## Proposition 2.4 (the Z_6 of the Standard Model)

**Statement.** (a) Every monomial in U and V, and every state of the exterior algebra Λ^*(5bar), satisfies 2t + 3d + 6Y = 0 mod 6, with t the triality (1 for 3, -1 for 3bar) and d = 2j mod 2. (b) The U(1)_Y-neutral monomials have t = 0 and d = 0. (c) Every tensor field on Y_{3,2} carries a representation of (U(3) x U(2))/U(1)_Y, on which SU(3) x SU(2) acts through PSU(3) x SO(3). So no tensor Kaluza-Klein mode on Y_{3,2}, on a background invariant under the displayed action, is a colour triplet or a weak doublet; spinors are open.

**Proof.** (a) holds for U (t = -1, d = 0, 6Y = 2) and V (t = 0, d = 1, 6Y = -3) and is additive. (b) U^alpha V^beta is neutral when 2 alpha = 3 beta, so 3 | alpha and 2 | beta. (c) The effective group of Theorem 2.1 acts on the frame bundle and on every tensor bundle, and its intersection with SU(3) x SU(2) is the centre Z_6 (Theorem 2.1, item 3). ∎

(a) is the statement that the Standard Model group is (SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1))/Z_6 = S(U(3) x U(2)). In the quotient realization the Z_6 is the kernel of SU(3) x SU(2) -> Isom(Y_{3,2}). States with fractional hypercharge are those charged under the quotient circle, and they cannot be functions or tensors on Y_{3,2}. For spinors the isometry group may lift to a double cover; whether fermionic Kaluza-Klein modes on Y_{3,2} can carry half-integer weak isospin or triality is open (next job, item 2 of the README).

## Chiral zero modes on the base with abelian fluxes

Witten's argument makes Kaluza-Klein fermions on an odd-dimensional space vectorlike, for gauge fields acting by isometries. The even base B_6 = CP^2 x CP^1 escapes it: abelian fluxes on B_6 give chiral four-dimensional fermions through the index of the twisted Dirac operator. This section asks which fluxes give one generation.

**Setting.** Ten-dimensional Weyl fermions on M^4 x CP^2 x CP^1, each with definite charges under some U(1) factors with fluxes on B_6. Since CP^2 is not spin, a fermion is a spin^c spinor, Λ^{0,*} ⊗ K^{1/2} ⊗ N with N the flux line bundle of its charges, and the four-dimensional chiral content is the virtual representation H^*(O(alpha, beta)) with (alpha, beta) = (-3/2, -1) + pairing of the charges with the fluxes. The cohomology is H^0(O(alpha)) = Sym^alpha(3bar) for alpha >= 0, H^2(O(alpha)) = Sym^{-alpha-3}(3) for alpha <= -3, and zero otherwise on CP^2; on CP^1, H^0(O(beta)) = Sym^beta(2) for beta >= 0 and H^1(O(beta)) = Sym^{-beta-2}(2) for beta <= -2. Each Standard Model Weyl field must come from one ten-dimensional field, from the whole cohomology of one line bundle.

**Lemma (required bidegrees).** Writing P_s = (alpha + 3/2, beta + 1) for the pairing of the charges of field s (up to the common sign of charge and chirality):

| field | P_s | note |
|---|---|---|
| Q (3, 2) | (-5/2, 2) | H^2(O(-4)) x H^0(O(1)) |
| u^c, d^c (3bar, 1) | (5/2, 1) | H^0(O(1)) x H^0(O(0)) |
| L (1, 2) | (+-3/2, 2) | |
| e^c, nu^c (1, 1) | (+-3/2, 1) | |

The second entry is 2j + 1 and the first is +-5/2 on colour triplets and +-3/2 on singlets. (Python, section D, by enumeration of the line bundles with these cohomologies and the Riemann-Roch check.)

### Proposition 2.5 (no generation from fluxes along hypercharge and B - L)

*Scope note (4 Nov 2026, after the referee).* The statement on equal images and the linearity of 2j + 1 assume a common ten-dimensional chirality for the fields. The conclusion holds without it: in the span of 6Y and 3(B - L) the charges of L are -3 times those of Q, so their second pairings are P_2(L) = -3 P_2(Q), against |P_2| = 2 on both doublets.

**Referee note on the third-charge search (GPT-6.1 Terra, 4 Nov 2026).** The bounded search below has an independent analytic proof. The same argument extends to any real third charge and independent source chiralities; this stronger extension was checked by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (second check: the vectors y, b, p, w have rank 4 for every sign pattern), so it stands as a no-go in the same scope with two model identifiers. See the [referee report](../reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_11d.md#proposition-25-adding-a-third-charge).

**Statement.** In the setting above, with the canonical spin^c root and fluxes only in U(1) directions spanned by Y and B - L (any flux quanta), no choice reproduces one generation, with or without nu^c. More generally, the pairing must give u^c and d^c the same image, and 2j + 1 = (2, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1) on (Q, u^c, d^c, L, e^c, nu^c) must be a linear function of the fluxed charges.

**Proof.** The pairing M is linear in the charges, and M c_s = P_s with c_s the charges of the left-handed field s. With only Y and B - L fluxed, c_u - c_d = (-6, 0) in the coordinates (6Y, 3(B - L)), so M vanishes on the hypercharge direction and P_s = 3(B - L)_s m for one vector m. The second entries 2 on Q and 1 on u^c would then need 3(B - L) to take values in the ratio 2 : 1 on (Q, u^c), against 1 : -1. In general, 2j + 1 is not in the span of 6Y = (1, -4, 2, -3, 6, 0) and 3(B - L) = (1, -1, -1, -3, 3, 3): from Q and u^c the coefficients are -1 and 3, and then d^c gives -5 against 1. ∎ An exhaustive search adding a third integer charge z in [-3, 3]^6 to Y and B - L finds no solution either (Python, section D).

**Scope and escapes.** Abelian fluxes on CP^2 x CP^1 with the Fubini-Study complex structure; one ten-dimensional Weyl field per Standard Model field; canonical spin^c root; Dolbeault index as the chiral content. Known escapes: nonabelian bundles (an SU(2)_R doublet bundle), several ten-dimensional fields per four-dimensional field, a changed common twist, orbifolds and walls, and non-Kaluza-Klein (brane) states. The result points to the next proposition.

### Proposition 2.6 (one generation from a universal charge, the grading and B - L)

**Referee note (GPT-6.1 Terra, 4 Nov 2026).** The index calculation works with the stated cohomology convention. For the colour action of the earlier quotient, reverse the first-component coefficients to obtain the labelled colour representations; global flux quantization remains to be specified. See the [referee report](../reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_11d.md#proposition-26-flux-assignments-for-one-generation).

**Statement.** Let the fluxed charges be F (one on every field: a change of the common spin^c twist), chi (+1 on Q and L, -1 on u^c, d^c, e^c, nu^c) and B - L. Then one generation with nu^c is the exact index content for the pairing

    P^{(1)} = c_0 chi + c_1 (B - L),   P^{(2)} = (3F + chi)/2,

with (c_0, c_1) = (-9/4, -3/4) or (-3/2, -3). The bidegrees are Q: (-4, 1); u^c, d^c: (1, 0); L: (-3, 1) or (0, 1); e^c, nu^c: (0, 0) or (-3, 0). Without chi, or without B - L, there is no solution.

**Proof.** Substitution, and the two failures by the span test (Python, section D2). ∎

**Reading.**

1. u^c and d^c see the same line bundle O(1, 0), and so do e^c and nu^c. The flux treats them as SU(2)_R doublets with a common internal profile; hypercharge Y = T_3R + (B - L)/2 enters through T_3R, which carries no flux. This is the left-right pattern of Pati-Salam, produced here by the index.
2. chi is the finite grading gamma_F on the particle basis. The flux therefore couples to the grading, an axial abelian connection on B_6; in the language of the finite geometry this is a fluctuation proportional to gamma_F, the structure of the two gradings of lemma 23.
3. **Mass and charge bases.** Because u^c and d^c share one zero-mode profile, overlap integrals on B_6 cannot separate them; their masses come from data that break SU(2)_R (Higgs or wall couplings), and the basis that diagonalizes the masses is set by those data, independently of the basis in which the charges are diagonal. The steer of 3 Nov 2026 remarks that u and d should still mix, since mass eigenvalues are not charge eigenvalues, and that at the 9 label, with the left-handed interactions off, charge eigenvalues can coincide. Proposition 2.6 puts the coincidence in the flux charges: equal flux charges for u^c and d^c, unequal hypercharges through T_3R. At the 9 label the electroweak part of the algebra is removed (lemma 21, STRATEGY T2), and what distinguishes u^c from d^c in the light theory is then the vectorlike charge alone. Making this precise needs the SU(2)_R-breaking wall data and the 9D reduction of this index; listed as item 4 of the README.

## What this file establishes, and the direction

**Referee note (GPT-6.1 Terra, 4 Nov 2026).** The \(M^{111}\) identification is conditional on the recalled fibration and metric normalization. The flux examples establish a particular construction and scoped obstructions; a general necessity for the grading flux remains open. See the [wording audit](../reviews/2026-11-04_terra_check_11d.md#wording-audit-results-and-proposals).

The Witten space with Killing spinors is the hypercharge quotient of the 5-bar (Theorem 2.1), the count 7 is fixed by SU(5) (Proposition 2.2), and the Z_6 of the Standard Model is the kernel of the isometry action (Proposition 2.4). The price is that hypercharge is no longer an isometry, and no tensor Kaluza-Klein mode on an invariant background carries triality, weak isospin 1/2 or fractional hypercharge. Kaluza-Klein chirality on the base needs fluxes that see the grading and leave T_3R free (Propositions 2.5 and 2.6). Both results point to states outside the Kaluza-Klein spectrum of Y_{3,2} for the quarks and leptons: [03](03_branes_hypercharge_and_the_84.md) takes them to be wrapped branes.
