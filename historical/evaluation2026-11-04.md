## Assessment

**Strongest new mathematics.** Lemma 29. It turns Conjecture 28.6 into a theorem for a metric class, with exact sums of squares at both ends from Sp(2)-equivariance, so that uniformity over representations needs no separate argument. Its weakness is the same as its strength: the proof is short where a Bismut-Lebeau analysis was expected, and it has had one referee who ran no computation. Case (b) survived two readings; the corrected case (a) one. This is the result to protect: it needs a reading by a different model with the quota back, before it carries a paper of its own.

**Most useful for the question of the project.** Two findings of the history. First, no founding text of noncommutative geometry (Connes-Lott, Madore, Dubois-Violette-Kerner-Madore, Coquereaux et al.) cites the Kaluza-Klein chirality obstruction; the discrete fifth dimension was there from 1988-1990 and was motivated by finiteness. A paper that states the obstruction in its scope and derives Connes-Lott from a chiral interval makes the link for the first time in print, within those searches. Second, no paper obtains a particle-physics finite triple as the limit of a Kaluza-Klein geometry with chiral walls. The main paper already proves the second; its introduction does not yet say the first.

**The 11D line.** Its value is uneven. The clean statements are few: Proposition 1.1 (the chiral interval is the antiperiodic circle with gamma as the reflection), Theorem 2.1 with Propositions 2.2-2.4 (Witten's space with Killing spinors as the hypercharge quotient of the 5-bar, with the Z_6), the E8 placement (Lemma 4.A, Theorem 4.4) and the root-configuration obstructions (5.1, 5.4). The string dictionary of files 06 to 09 is proposals, and 11D/10 shows that it cannot carry both the electroweak group at the far wall and the opening of a dimension on one path, so it stays a record. The obstructions are now at the consensus threshold, in their scopes. What they say together, with the user's observation 3b, is that three families in single slots admit no exchange of the top with the neutrinos, four do, and the 44 = 32 + 12 count that goes with four is numerical only (the colour SU(3) lies in no Spin(9) of the required kind, once that argument is checked). That is a scoped negative result with a direction, and it is the kind of statement AGENTS.md allows in print.

**The generation number.** Nothing in the record or in the literature fixes it inside the spectral-triple axioms (history, section 8). The record has only the obstructions above and lemma 21's countings. We do not propose a mechanism; we propose stating the constraints.

**Weak points of the moment.** (1) No referee of another kind is available; Sonnet has been the only checker since the quota ran out, and it has found real errors, but it is a smaller model and the user's caution stands. (2) The corrected texts of lemma 29 case (a), 11D/10 and 11D/11 have no second reading. (3) The vector sector on the disc bundle is untouched, so "massless electroweak limit" remains a target. (4) The supplement has 670 mobile pages and is one file; it has stopped being readable. (5) Five PDFs and a growing number of notes multiply the places where a statement lives; the dictionary note was meant to be the reader's entry, and it should stay the only synthesis.
