## Summary

**Lemma 51: three errors and ten amendments.** Propositions 51.1 and 51.2 are correct, with amendments. In Proposition 51.3 there is one error, in item 2: the heavier member of the doublet is fixed by the sign of the oriented area of (A, eta) in the complex line (P_tau, J_w), and the orientation of A does not fix it. Item 4 holds in the complex structure J_w of the Majorana line, and J_w = J or J_w = -J according to which weight line of x that line is. The intrinsic form is |y_mu| = |A + J_mu eta| on each weight line mu of x; we checked it on 500 random pairs. The Reading has two errors. A doublet with a torsion along the Wilson line has the mass (|A|^2 + |eta|^2)^{1/2}, and the Reading gives |A|. In the moduli lead, A is Lambda_coroot (x) E = Hom(Lambda_weight, E), and the Reading writes Hom(Lambda_coroot, E). The Friedman-Morgan-Witten e-print confirms the rest of the lead: the quotient is CP^2 for SU(3), the mirror points have centralizer U(2), and the nine symmetric points have central holonomy. Among the amendments:

- Agricola's normalization is now verified.
- The Wilson line is a gauge class, and under the window hypothesis this changes nothing in Proposition 51.2.
- The orientability relation at the mirror stratum fails, where the lemma only says it is unavailable.
- The mass ratios are restated at a common scale.
- The universal-torsion exclusion holds for any Wilson lines, with explicit bounds.

**Lemma 52: two errors and thirteen amendments.** Propositions 52.1 and 52.3 are correct, with amendments. In Proposition 52.1 (ii), the statement that the connector reduces the four-summand algebra to A_SM exactly on the eigenline condition is right. It needs the rank-one defect formula given below, since Theorem 20.1 alone gives the inclusion only in some A(R). The pairing matrices are exactly those that Chamseddine, Connes and Marcolli print in Proposition 2.30 (3). Proposition 52.2 has one error in its statement (iii) and one in its proof.

- **The mass statement.** A nine-dimensional Majorana field carries exactly one lepton generation only on its self-conjugate sector (k, k_5) = 0. There the mass operator is i c(eta) Gamma, purely anti-quaternionic, with the equal masses |eta|. The sectors with nonzero momentum pair into a generation and a mirror generation. So "the custodial half of the lepton Yukawa block is a quantized internal momentum" holds only on the vectorlike levels. The Reading's "the lepton masses are quantized and the quark masses are not" does not follow.
- **The proof.** The bilinear psi^T C psi vanishes for anticommuting components, and the proof says commuting. We confirmed on an explicit representation that C is symmetric in signature (1, 8). We also found that C Gamma^{(3)} is antisymmetric, so the torsion coupling of a single Majorana field survives, which supports (iii).

The Majorana-mass sentence holds for Lorentz-invariant and constant bulk data. Constant fluxes with a leg on S^1 give nu_R^c and e_R^c Majorana entries of equal modulus, so a charge-preserving Majorana mass for nu_R alone does not arise from them.

## New insights and leads

These are leads. None is a result of the lemmas, and each needs its own proof or check before use.

1. **The sign of the oriented area as the up-down order (lead).** On each weight line mu of x the coupling has modulus |A + J_mu eta|. So the up-type member is heavier exactly when <J_w A, eta> < 0. The first generation, with m_d > m_u, has the opposite sign from the second and third. A flavour-dependent orientation of the torsion relative to J_w A is then the datum behind the inverted first-generation order, a target for the three-generation form (T12).
2. **B - L from the fibre (lead, checked numerically).** On M^4 x A x S^1 the generator Q = gamma_5 Gamma_A preserves the real form and commutes with Spin(1, 3) and Spin(4)_A; here Gamma_A is the chirality of A. Its exponential leaves invariant the Majorana bilinears of the four-dimensional kinetic term, of the momenta along A and of the torsion on A. On the left-handed fields Gamma_A = -1 on the doublet and +1 on the singlets, the B - L of a lepton generation. With the U(1)_b charges it gives the hypercharges -1/2, 0 and +1 of l_L, nu_R^c and e_R^c. The S^1 momentum and the flux components with a leg on S^1 break it. So the circle direction, the grading axis of the sheet, carries exactly the B - L-violating terms: a lead for the Majorana sector and T2c.
3. **Chirality of the two sectors (lead).** A real, colour-singlet nine-dimensional field carries exactly one generation on its self-conjugate sector. A colour triplet carries a generation and its mirror. In the nine-dimensional reading the quark sector therefore needs a mechanism that removes the mirror, such as the walls of lemmas 22 and 28, and the lepton sector does not. A single lepton generation has one SU(2)_L doublet, an odd number, which carries Witten's global SU(2) anomaly; in the Standard Model the three colours of quark doublets cancel it. So the nine-dimensional Majorana singlet is consistent as a four-dimensional theory only together with the quark sector.
4. **A constant H as a background (remark with its scope).** On the compact flat torus, the leading string-frame NS-NS equation R_ij + 2 nabla_i nabla_j phi - (1/4) H_ikl H_j^kl = 0 has trace 2 Delta phi = (1/4) H_ikl H^ikl > 0. Its integral over the torus vanishes, so no constant H != 0 solves it there. The supersymmetric heterotic case is treated by Kimura and Yi (arXiv:hep-th/0605247, abstract: H != 0 with dH = 0 forces a singular compactification). This bears on the direction "a potential that relates H to A" of lemma 51 and leaves the lemma's statements unchanged, since they take A and H as data. Known escapes: Lie groups with a biinvariant metric and parallel torsion T(X, Y, Z) = g([X, Y], Z), where nabla^{+-1/4} are flat (Agricola, example on Lie groups), for instance S^3 x S^1 factors or nilmanifolds; higher-order corrections; brane and orientifold sources. This remark has not been checked by a second model.
5. **The physical normalization (lead).** Kimura and Yi (abstract) state that in heterotic flux compactifications the Dirac operator of the gauge fermions uses the connection with torsion one third, which is s = 1/4 in lemma 51. The constant enters no statement. It would enter a flux-quantization condition relating |eta| to the integer fluxes of H over the three-cycles of A.
6. **The moduli lead made concrete (lead).** With A = Lambda_coroot (x) E, the mirror strata of A are the flat SU(3)-connections on E with centralizer U(2), and the symmetric points those with central holonomy. The stabilizer algebras of lemma 49 along the same strata are C + H, C and R on the spinors. A map from the centralizer of the connection, U(2) on a mirror, to the unbroken circle of the spinor stabilizer is the concrete form of the open question in lemma 51's Scope.
