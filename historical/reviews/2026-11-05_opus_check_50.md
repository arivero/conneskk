## Summary

We find two errors and sixteen amendments. The first error is in Proposition 50.1 (3). With the universal block, the second case gives the mass to the down-type quark and the charged lepton together. By the last statement of Proposition 21.1 that pair contributes exactly what the up-type quark contributes, so both cases keep cos^2 theta_NC = 5/8 and the ratio 8/3. Proposition 21.1 selects the up-type case only for a quark-only block, which needs the split of Proposition 50.3. The second error is in the closing sentence of Proposition 50.3, repeated in the Outcome, the Reading and STRATEGY T12. The top-only block keeps order one on H_R + H_L + C + M_3(C), of real dimension 28, so "exactly on the connector's algebra C + H + M_3(C)" holds only for the sum with the connector. In addition, the split block lies outside the compressed form d_X (x) 1 that Corollary 16.8 gives for an ungraded second sheet. All other statements are confirmed: the isometry, the halves and their norms, the equivalences (a)-(c), the circle U(1)_h, J_w and the invariant blocks; the compression identity; the order-one defect formula with items (1) to (5); the centralizer of dimension 20 with its forced embedding and the charges (2/3, -1/3, -1, 0). The main amendments are four:

- **The sign of delta'.** delta' = -J_w h/2 follows in two lines from the definition of J_w, with no dependence on an orientation. It holds in all 400 random frames for both inequivalent Cl(5) representations, and it extends to every rank-one block as delta' = -J_mu h/2.
- **The compression identity.** It is Example 16.9, item 2, and a one-line consequence of the fourth moment of lemma 10.
- **The hypercharge.** In the complex structure J_w the Higgs vector lies in the doublet of hypercharge -1/2, the conjugate doublet through which u_R couples. In -J_w = J_{jw} it lies in the doublet of hypercharge +1/2.
- **Prior work.** Three sources in print cover more than section P records:
  - Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli, Proposition 3.5, gives the block as a quaternionic Higgs times diag(Y_u, Y_d).
  - Krajewski 1998 gives the first-order rule of which Proposition 50.3 is a case.
  - Chamseddine-Connes-van Suijlekom, section 8, equation SM-vev, gives a Standard Model vacuum of the spectral Pati-Salam model that aligns <phi>, <Delta> and <Sigma> on the lines of Propositions 50.1 and 50.3.

## New insights and leads

These are leads, not results.

1. **The two points of the fibre and the hypercharge sign.** [w] -> J_w is the twistor correspondence, and J_{jw} = -J_w. The choice between C mu = Cw and C mu = Cjw is the choice between two antipodal points of the fibre, equivalently between the complex structures in which h lies in the doublet of hypercharge -1/2 or +1/2. In the Standard Model the up-type quarks couple through the conjugate doublet of hypercharge -1/2, matching J_w. A parent in which the Majorana point fixes the complex structure of T_p S^4 would thereby fix which member of the doublet is heavy. This gives a reason for the up-type assignment that does not depend on Proposition 21.1.
2. **The neutral plane.** U(1)_h fixes span_R(h, J_w h) and rotates the complement with charge -2. So delta' = -J_w h/2 lies in the neutral plane of the doublet. In the reading where h is a vacuum value, J_w h is the neutral Goldstone direction, and the anti-quaternionic half of the top block points along it.
3. **The spectral Pati-Salam vacuum as the parent the Scope asks for.** The fields Delta (2_R, 1_L, 4), Sigma (1, 1, 1 + 15) and phi (2_R, 2_L, 1) of arXiv:1304.8050 are [w] and v, the lepton line, and the Higgs vector and Yukawa as fields of a finite model. Their Standard Model vacuum has the alignments of Propositions 50.1 and 50.3. A computation within that model would test whether its potential forces the right index of <phi> onto that of <Delta> (the eigenline condition) and the Sigma line onto the Pati-Salam index of <Delta> (Proposition 50.3 (5) as a vacuum statement). The authors chose that vacuum and found a nonnegative Hessian in the Delta directions. This is a positive step for T12.
4. **The rank-one blocks as pairs.** The rank-one blocks are exactly the pairs (h, [mu]) in (T_p S^4 minus 0) x P(L_p), with delta = h/2 and delta' = -J_mu delta. The dimension count 4 + 2 = 6 matches the rank-one complex 2 x 2 matrices. This is a one-line proposition that could replace the corresponding Reading sentence.
5. **The alignment of the Higgs vectors.** The centralizer is 20 when the lepton block's Higgs vector lies on the J_w-line of h and 19 otherwise. A single Higgs vector h for all blocks, as the single quaternionic Higgs of Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli supplies, is the finite form of one Higgs doublet, and it is what keeps U(1)_em.
6. **Which Yukawa patterns each second-sheet algebra admits.** By Krajewski's rule, the order-one sheet-diagonal operators for a given A_Y are sum_k E_k x Z_k with Z_k in the commutant of A_Y. This classifies the admissible Yukawa patterns for intermediate second-sheet algebras, for example a partial breaking of colour, and the list could locate the C + M_3(C) case among them.
7. **Generation structure in the connector.** With the universal block, a Majorana matrix equal on the generations gives neutrino ratios (m_c/m_t)^2, so the connector needs generation structure, three couplings kappa_i or a matrix. This is a known feature of relations M_D = M_u and bears on the three-copy remark in lemma 20, reading 1.
