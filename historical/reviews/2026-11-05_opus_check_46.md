## Item 1: Proposition 46.1

The sign is real, so the conjugate representation a -> conj(Gamma_a) has the same omega and is equivalent: C conj(Gamma_a) C^{-1} = Gamma_a with C unitary, unique up to a scalar. Then C conj(C) commutes with all Gamma_a and is a unitary scalar, equal to +-1; it equals -1 because the real algebra generated by the Gamma_a is M_2(H), the real Clifford algebra Cl(5, 0) being M_2(H) + M_2(H). So j = C K exists, j^2 = -1, and j is unique up to a phase. The involution Y -> j Y j^{-1} on complex-linear Y does not depend on that phase, so neither does the splitting into j-linear and j-antilinear parts. The Setting assumes j without saying this.

## New insights

1. **The rank-one block marks a point of the twistor fibre (lead for T14 and T12).** A rank-one X is lambda mu^* with mu in L_p, so its row line C mu is a point of P(L_p) = S^2, the twistor fibre over p, the same kind of datum as the connector's line Cw. In the C-action of Theorem 20.2 the states on the line Cw are the up-type right-handed ones: w (x) conj(v) is neutral (nu_R), and u_R shares the W-line Cw. So, with the eigenline condition of Corollary 46.4 and the up-type assignment of the top, the top's right-handed line equals the connector's line: C mu = Cw. One marked point of CP^3 over p would then carry both the Majorana line and the third-generation rank-one Yukawa block. This is a concrete target for a parent: a single datum, a point of the twistor space, fixing the connector and the top.

2. **The electroweak pattern as stabilizers in K_p.** K_p = SU(2)_L x SU(2)_R; a j-linear block (the Higgs, T_p S^4) has stabilizer the diagonal Sp(1), the custodial SU(2)_{L+R}; the j-antilinear half breaks it to a circle; the circle common to the block and the connector exists exactly under the eigenline condition and plays the role of U(1)_em. In the window variables, the custodial breaking is measured by |Im(conj(q) q')| = (m_+^2 - m_-^2)/4. This can become a lemma direction for lemma 46 and a row in the dictionary note.

3. **A bound on the splitting from the core window.** By Theorem 29.9 and Proposition 28.8, the core compression of a real function a on X converges to Q_B(a|_{S^4}), which is j-linear, with error at most C ||a||_{C^1} M^{-1/2}. So a Yukawa block taken as the odd part of a core compression of a real function has j-antilinear half of norm O(M^{-1/2}), and by Proposition 46.2 its relative up-down splitting is bounded accordingly unless its j-linear half is of the same order. So, for blocks taken from core compressions of real functions on X, a heavy top at large M needs the wall sheet or the finite-M terms. The finite-M j-breaking of the core modes, seen in the numerics of lemma 28, is the quantity to compute.

4. **The window's own axis.** Proposition 46.3 gives the window block lambda + mu n.Gamma the singular values |lambda + mu| on L_n and |lambda - mu| on L_n^perp. At mu = lambda the block is 2 lambda P_{L_n}, and the window has exact zero modes on the two copies of L_n^perp. A G-breaking block in the 5 thus marks a quaternionic line, the datum that Proposition 28.19 says still has to be chosen ("a marked Gamma_p"). This is a lead for T0 on the grading axis as an input: the axis could be the direction n of a G-breaking 5-block whose strength is comparable with the tunnelling mass, both exponentially small. Nothing here makes that comparison dynamical.

5. **The j-antilinear half as i Gamma_p times base data.** Every Hermitian odd j-antilinear block is -i Gamma_p Y' with Y' odd and j-linear. Together with the escape stated in Item 5, this shows that the base functions plus the complex structure and the grading reach the full bidoublet. A parent with complex multiplication operators and a grading-twisted coupling is then an alternative to the CP^3 sheet as the source of the up-down splitting, and the two should be compared before either is adopted.

6. **Three generations.** The criterion of Item 5, q^* q' quaternion-Hermitian, implies equal up and down spectra (Kramers pairs). The CKM matrix in the variables (q, q') is a natural next computation.
