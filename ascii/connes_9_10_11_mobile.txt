Abstract
Can the finite Standard Model geometry arise
from a controlled reduction of an ordinary
internal manifold? This paper consolidates the
investigation of seven-, six- and five-dimensional
internal spaces, corresponding to eleven-, ten-
and nine-dimensional spacetime products. The
central distinction is between metric or spectral
dimension, the KO-degree of a real spectral
triple, and the gauge and matter modes that
remain light. None determines the other two.

Several components are explicit. Heavy finite
operators select the algebra chain from
Pati-Salam to the Standard Model and then to
color plus electromagnetism, once the finite
bimodule is supplied. Twistor compression
produces a quaternionic/complex algebra pair. A
spin Aloff-Wallach seven-manifold has exact
circle, two-sphere and three-sphere fibrations.
Its monopole Dirac families can be calculated,
including their nontrivial kernel bundles and
Berry connections. A spin-twisted torus/Weyl
construction contains the Standard Model
algebra as a selected corner of a finite-orbit
quotient. Boundaries and singular quotients
provide distinct mechanisms for changes of
dimension and chirality.
